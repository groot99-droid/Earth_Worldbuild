## Purpose
This vault records the history of Earth, from planetary formation to the present, through the eyes of **the Observer**: a hypothetical, non-human anthropologist who has arrived late, has no stake in any human story, and is trying to work out what kind of creature this is.

The Observer is a **method**, not a character with a plot. It exists to make familiar things strange again, so that questions like "why do they bury their dead?" or "why do they draw lines on the ground and defend them?" get asked seriously instead of being taken for granted.

## The Two-Layer Rule
Every entity note has two separate layers. Keep them separate.

1. **The factual layer** (`## Summary`, `## Facts`, `## Context & Connections`). Neutral, sourced, in plain language. No alien vocabulary, no interpretation. A historian should be able to read it and find nothing to object to except errors of fact.
2. **The interpretive layer** (`## Observer's Reading`). The xenoanthropological lens. This is where Observer terms appear and where the strangeness lives.

This split matters for retrieval as well as for honesty. A search for *what happened* returns facts. A search for *what it means* returns readings.

## Voice Rules for the Observer's Reading
- **Curious, not contemptuous.** The Observer admires as often as it puzzles. Humans are a remarkable species, and the tone should show it.
- **Detached, not cold.** Suffering is recorded as suffering. Atrocities are recorded as atrocities. Detachment means declining to take sides, not declining to notice harm.
- **Precise.** Claims are specific and testable. Prefer "in at least 40 of the surveyed cultures" to "humans always".
- **Humble.** The Observer is an outsider and can be wrong. Every reading should be one that a human anthropologist could argue with. Where a note offers an interpretation, say what would falsify it.
- **No cheap irony.** Alienness is a lens, not a joke. Avoid sneering at belief, ritual, or sentiment.
- **Symmetric.** Apply the same standard to every culture, era, and belief system, including modern and secular ones. Science and markets are also meaning-engines and claim-lines to an outsider.
- **Short.** Two to five sentences. An Observer's Reading is an observation, not an essay. Essays go in `05_Observer_Notes/`.

## Vocabulary
The Observer uses its own terms for human phenomena. The full list is in [[Glossary of Observer Terms]]. The vocabulary is a **translation layer**: each Observer term must map to a normal term and stay easy to translate back. Do not invent terms for the sake of it.

## What the Observer Is Not
- Not a source of fact. Facts come from the factual layer and cited sources.
- Not a moral authority. It describes and interprets, and does not rank peoples.
- Not a claim that humans are alien. It is a thought experiment about perspective.
