## How to Use This Glossary
These are the Observer's working terms. Each maps to an ordinary concept. Use them **only inside `## Observer's Reading`** sections and `05_Observer_Notes/`. See [[Observer's Charter]].

## Terms

### The species
*Ordinary term:* Homo sapiens, humanity. The Observer sometimes says "the bipeds" when the physical body matters.

### Meaning-engine
*Ordinary term:* religion, ideology, cosmology, or any large shared system that explains why things happen and what one should do. Secular ideologies and scientific paradigms can also function as meaning-engines. See [[Religion and Belief]].

### Story-glue
*Ordinary term:* the shared narratives (myths, nations, corporations, laws) that let large numbers of strangers cooperate. See [[Story-Glue - How Strangers Cooperate at Scale]].

### Claim-line
*Ordinary term:* a border, boundary, or property line. A claim-line has no physical existence but reliably changes behavior. See [[The Fiction of Claim-Lines]].

### Exchange-web
*Ordinary term:* a trade network. See [[Trade and Economy]].

### Kin-lattice
*Ordinary term:* the structure of kinship, marriage, descent, and household. See [[Kinship and Society]].

### Sound-code and mark-code
*Ordinary term:* spoken language and writing systems. See [[Language and Writing]].

### Memory-outsourcing
*Ordinary term:* writing, records, archives, and later digital storage: moving memory out of bodies into objects. See [[The Great Outsourcing of Memory]].

### Grief-rite
*Ordinary term:* funerary and mourning practice. See [[Why the Species Buries Its Dead]].

### Tool-lineage
*Ordinary term:* technology considered as a chain of inheritance in which each tool makes the next tool possible. See [[Technology]].

### Sanctioned harm
*Ordinary term:* organized, socially approved violence: war, executions, raids. See [[Sanctioned Harm]] and [[War and Conflict]].

### The Settling
*Ordinary term:* the transition to agriculture and permanent settlement. See [[The Settling and Its Bargain]].

### Heat-domestication
*Ordinary term:* the control of fire. See [[Fire, Then Everything]].

### Status-signal
*Ordinary term:* prestige display: monuments, regalia, luxury goods, credentials.

### Pattern-taming
*Ordinary term:* the systematic study of nature: science, mathematics, astronomy. See [[Science]].

### Ancestor-weight
*Ordinary term:* the pull of tradition; the degree to which past generations' choices constrain present ones.

### Great Acceleration
*Ordinary term:* the sharp rise, from about 1950, in human population, energy use, and environmental impact. See [[Great Acceleration]] and [[The Species as a Geological Force]].
