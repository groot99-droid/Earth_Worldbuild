## Summary
Göbekli Tepe is a Pre-Pottery Neolithic site in southeastern Turkey with large carved T-shaped stone pillars, dating to about 9500 to 8000 BCE.

## Facts
- Pillars stand up to about 5.5 meters tall and are carved with animals.
- It was built by people who were, at the time, foragers or early farmers.
- Excavations led by Klaus Schmidt began in 1995.
- It was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2018.
