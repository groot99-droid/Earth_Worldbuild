## Summary
Athens is a Greek city that, in the 5th century BCE, became the leading city of the Greek world, with an early form of direct democracy and major achievements in philosophy, drama, and art.

## Facts
- Cleisthenes' reforms of about 508 BCE established democratic institutions.
- The Parthenon was built between 447 and 432 BCE.
- Socrates, Plato, and the tragedians worked in Athens.
- Athens lost to Sparta in the Peloponnesian War (431 to 404 BCE).

## Context & Connections
Athens was the center of the [[Ancient Greeks]]' intellectual world and the base for [[Aristotle]]'s Lyceum.
