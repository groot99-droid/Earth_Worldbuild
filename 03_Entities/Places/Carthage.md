## Summary
Carthage was a Phoenician colony on the coast of present-day Tunisia, traditionally founded in 814 BCE, which became a major Mediterranean power until Rome destroyed it in 146 BCE.

## Facts
- It grew into a naval and commercial empire across the western Mediterranean.
- Three Punic Wars against Rome were fought between 264 and 146 BCE.
- Hannibal crossed the Alps with an army in 218 BCE.
- Rome destroyed the city in 146 BCE and later refounded it as a Roman colony. It was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1979.

## Context & Connections
Carthage descended from the [[Phoenicians]] and lost its long rivalry with the [[Romans]]. See [[Rome]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes that most of what is known about Carthage comes from its enemies, since its own records were largely lost. The winners of a war often write the losers' histories.
