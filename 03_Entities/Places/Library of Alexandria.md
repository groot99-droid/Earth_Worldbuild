## Summary
The Library of Alexandria was a major research library in Ptolemaic Egypt, founded in the early 3rd century BCE as part of the Mouseion, and its decline was gradual rather than a single catastrophe.

## Facts
- It was founded under the first Ptolemaic rulers, in the 3rd century BCE.
- Scholars associated with it include Eratosthenes, who estimated the circumference of the Earth, and Callimachus, who compiled a catalog of works.
- Euclid is traditionally said to have worked in Alexandria, though details of his life are uncertain.
- Julius Caesar's fire in 48 BCE damaged some holdings, but the institution continued.
- Its decline resulted from reduced funding, political upheaval, and the loss of scholars over several centuries.
