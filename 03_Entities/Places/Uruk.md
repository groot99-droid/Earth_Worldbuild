## Summary
Uruk was a Sumerian city in southern Mesopotamia and one of the first large cities in the world, at its peak around 3200 to 2900 BCE.

## Facts
- It has yielded some of the earliest known writing, on clay tablets from the Eanna temple precinct.
- By about 2900 BCE a wall about 9 kilometers long enclosed roughly 600 hectares (6 square kilometers), with an estimated 50,000 to 80,000 residents.
- Uruk is the legendary city of Gilgamesh.
- The modern site is called Warka, in Iraq.
