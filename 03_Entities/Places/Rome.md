## Summary
Rome, traditionally founded in 753 BCE, grew from a small city on the Tiber to the capital of an empire and remains a major city today.

## Facts
- Rome had roughly a million inhabitants in the first two centuries CE, by many estimates.
- The Colosseum was completed in 80 CE.
- The city was sacked in 410 and 455 CE.
- It later became the seat of the papacy.

## Context & Connections
Rome is the capital of the [[Romans]]. See [[Augustus]] and [[Fall of the Western Roman Empire]].
