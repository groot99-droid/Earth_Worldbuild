## Summary
Çatalhöyük was a large Neolithic settlement in central Turkey, occupied from about 7100 to 5700 BCE, with houses packed together and entered from the roof.

## Facts
- It held several thousand people at its height.
- Walls bore paintings, and the dead were buried under house floors.
- It had no streets; people walked across roofs.
- It was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2012.
