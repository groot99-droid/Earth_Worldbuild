## Summary
Timbuktu is a city in Mali on the southern edge of the Sahara that became a hub of the trans-Saharan trade and of Islamic scholarship.

## Facts
- It was founded around 1100 CE as a seasonal camp.
- Salt from the Sahara and gold from the south were traded there.
- The Sankore mosque and madrasa were centers of learning.
- It holds hundreds of thousands of historical manuscripts and was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1988.
