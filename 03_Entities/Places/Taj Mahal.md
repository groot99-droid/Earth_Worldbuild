## Summary
The Taj Mahal is a white marble mausoleum in Agra, built from about 1632 to 1653 by the Mughal emperor Shah Jahan for his wife Mumtaz Mahal, who died in 1631.

## Facts
- Mumtaz Mahal died in 1631 while giving birth.
- Construction began in 1632, with the main tomb largely finished by about 1643 and the complex by 1653.
- Commonly cited estimates give around 20,000 workers.
- Ustad Ahmad Lahori is often named as its chief architect, though attribution is debated.
- It was inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage list in 1983.
