## Summary
Tenochtitlan was the capital of the Aztec Empire, founded on an island in Lake Texcoco in 1325 and destroyed after a siege in 1521.

## Facts
- Population estimates for 1519 range from about 100,000 to 400,000, with 200,000 often cited, making it one of the largest cities in the world.
- Causeways and canals connected it to the mainland; chinampas provided food.
- The Templo Mayor was its main temple.
- Mexico City was built over its ruins.

## Context & Connections
Tenochtitlan was the center of the [[Aztec Empire]]. Its fall is part of the [[Columbian Exchange]].
