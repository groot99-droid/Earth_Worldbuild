## Summary
Constantinople was founded in 330 CE by Emperor Constantine on the site of Byzantium and served as the capital of the Eastern Roman (Byzantine) Empire until the Ottoman conquest in 1453.

## Facts
- The Hagia Sophia was completed in 537 under Emperor Justinian.
- The city was sacked by the Fourth Crusade in 1204.
- It fell to Mehmed II on 29 May 1453.
- It sits on the Bosphorus, between Europe and Asia; it is now Istanbul.

## Context & Connections
The city outlived the [[Fall of the Western Roman Empire]] by almost a thousand years. It later became the capital of the [[Ottoman Empire]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes that geography kept the city valuable to every ruler. A strait that joins two seas and two continents is a permanent asset.
