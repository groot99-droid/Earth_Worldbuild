## Summary
The Giza Necropolis, near Cairo, holds three large pyramids built for the pharaohs Khufu, Khafre, and Menkaure in the 26th century BCE, along with the Great Sphinx.

## Facts
- The Great Pyramid of Khufu was originally about 146.6 meters high.
- It was the tallest human-made structure for about 3,800 years.
- Workers were organized labor teams and were fed and housed by the state.
- It is the only one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World still largely standing.

## Context & Connections
The complex reflects the power of the [[Ancient Egyptians]] state that arose from [[Unification of Egypt]].
