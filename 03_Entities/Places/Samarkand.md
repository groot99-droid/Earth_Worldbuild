## Summary
Samarkand is a city in present-day Uzbekistan, one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities of Central Asia and a leading crossroads of the Silk Roads.

## Facts
- The ancient city (Afrasiab) is thought to date from the 7th century BCE or earlier.
- It was known to the Greeks as Maracanda and was taken by Alexander the Great in 329 BCE.
- It was a major Sogdian city, was sacked by Genghis Khan in 1220, and became Timur's capital in the 1370s.
- UNESCO inscribed 'Samarkand - Crossroad of Cultures' in 2001.

## Context & Connections
Samarkand was central to the [[Sogdians]] and the [[Timurid Empire]]. See [[Alexander's Conquests]] and [[Opening of the Silk Roads]].
