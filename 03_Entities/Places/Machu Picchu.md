## Summary
Machu Picchu is a stone citadel built about 1450 in the Peruvian Andes, probably as a royal estate for the Inca ruler Pachacuti.

## Facts
- It sits at about 2,430 meters above sea level.
- It was abandoned around the time of the Spanish conquest.
- Local people knew of it; Hiram Bingham brought it to international attention in 1911.
- It was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1983.

## Context & Connections
The site belongs to the [[Inca Empire]]. See [[Andes]].
