## Summary
Mohenjo-daro was a major city of the Indus Valley Civilization in present-day Pakistan, occupied from about 2500 to 1900 BCE.

## Facts
- It had a grid layout with brick-lined drains and wells.
- The 'Great Bath' is a large water tank at the center of its citadel.
- It may have held tens of thousands of people.
- It was declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1980.

## Context & Connections
Mohenjo-daro is a key site of the [[Indus Valley Civilization]] and shows how [[The Urban Revolution]] occurred in more than one region.
