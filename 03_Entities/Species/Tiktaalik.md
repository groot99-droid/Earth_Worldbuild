## Summary
Tiktaalik is a 375-million-year-old fossil fish with limb-like fins and a flexible neck, a transitional form between fishes and four-limbed animals.

## Facts
- It was discovered in 2004 on Ellesmere Island in the Canadian Arctic by Neil Shubin, Edward Daeschler, and Farish Jenkins.
- It had a flat head with eyes on top, and fins with wrist-like bones.
- It lived in shallow freshwater habitats in the Late Devonian.
- It is a well-known example of a predicted transitional fossil.
