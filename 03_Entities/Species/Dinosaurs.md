## Summary
Dinosaurs were a diverse group of reptiles that dominated land ecosystems for about 165 million years. Birds are living descendants of one dinosaur lineage.

## Facts
- The earliest dinosaurs appeared roughly 233 million years ago in the Triassic.
- Non-avian dinosaurs ranged from chicken-sized to the largest land animals ever.
- Many had feathers, and birds descend from small feathered theropods.
- Non-avian dinosaurs died out in the [[Cretaceous-Paleogene Extinction]].

## Context & Connections
Dinosaurs define the [[Mesozoic Era]]. They rose after the [[Triassic-Jurassic Extinction]] and ended with the [[Cretaceous-Paleogene Extinction]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes that dinosaurs are still with us as birds. Extinction is not always total, and lineages can continue in other forms.
