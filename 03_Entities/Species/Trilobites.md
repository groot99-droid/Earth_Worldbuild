## Summary
Trilobites were marine arthropods with a hard, segmented exoskeleton that thrived for about 270 million years, from the early Cambrian until the end-Permian extinction.

## Facts
- More than 20,000 species have been described.
- Their compound eyes had lenses made of calcite, a mineral.
- They are widely used to date and correlate Paleozoic rocks.
- The last trilobites died out in the [[Permian-Triassic Extinction]].

## Context & Connections
Trilobites are a signature animal of the [[Paleozoic Era]] and of the [[Cambrian Explosion]].
