## Summary
Neanderthals were a human species that lived in Europe and western Asia from about 400,000 to about 40,000 years ago and interbred with Homo sapiens.

## Facts
- They had large brains, robust bodies adapted to cold, and made Mousterian stone tools.
- The Neanderthal genome was first sequenced in 2010.
- Non-African people today derive up to about 2 percent of their ancestry from Neanderthals.
- Evidence for burial, ornaments, and pigment use is debated but growing.

## Context & Connections
Neanderthals met [[Homo sapiens]] during the [[Out of Africa Dispersal]]. Compare [[Denisovans]].
