## Summary
Homo habilis ('handy man') is an early member of the genus Homo that lived in Africa from about 2.4 to 1.4 million years ago.

## Facts
- It was named in 1964 from fossils at Olduvai Gorge in Tanzania.
- Its brain was roughly 500 to 700 cubic centimeters (estimates vary).
- It is associated with [[Oldowan Stone Tools]], though the link is not certain.
- Whether it is a distinct species or a variant of other early Homo is debated.

## Context & Connections
H. habilis appears in the [[Pleistocene Epoch]] and precedes [[Homo erectus]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes that the label 'human' was pushed back in time by each new discovery. The boundary of the genus is blurred.
