## Summary
Homo sapiens is the species of modern humans: the only living member of the genus Homo, which emerged in Africa about 300,000 years ago and now numbers about eight billion.

## Facts
- Modern humans have a brain of about 1,350 cubic centimeters.
- They are notable for language, symbolic thought, and cumulative culture.
- They have inhabited every continent and now live in cities, deserts, polar stations, and orbit.
- Genetic diversity is relatively low compared with other apes.
