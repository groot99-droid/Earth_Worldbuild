## Summary
The woolly mammoth was a cold-adapted elephant of the northern Ice Age steppe, which survived on Wrangel Island until about 4,000 years ago.

## Facts
- It had long curved tusks, a shaggy coat, and a thick fat layer.
- Most populations died out around the end of the last ice age, about 11,000 years ago.
- Humans hunted mammoths and used their bones and tusks for shelters and art.
- Ancient DNA has been recovered from frozen remains.

## Context & Connections
Woolly mammoths are a symbol of the Ice Age of the [[Pleistocene Epoch]] and appear in Paleolithic art; see [[Emergence of Symbolic Art]].
