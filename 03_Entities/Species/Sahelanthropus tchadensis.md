## Summary
Sahelanthropus tchadensis is a roughly 7-million-year-old fossil ape from Chad, proposed as one of the earliest members of the human lineage.

## Facts
- The nearly complete skull, nicknamed Toumai, was found in 2001.
- The position of the skull's base suggests possible upright walking.
- Its brain was about the size of a chimpanzee's.
- Whether it is a hominin at all is debated.

## Context & Connections
Sahelanthropus is a candidate for the earliest hominin, near the [[Divergence of the Hominin Lineage]]. Compare [[Australopithecus afarensis]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes that the earliest fossil candidates for the human lineage are fragmentary and disputed. The 'first human' is a moving target.

## Open Questions
