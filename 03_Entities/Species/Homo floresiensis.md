## Summary
Homo floresiensis, nicknamed 'the hobbit', was a small-bodied human species that lived on the Indonesian island of Flores. Its bones date to about 100,000 to 60,000 years ago, and stone tools attributed to it extend to about 50,000 years ago.

## Facts
- It was discovered in 2003 at Liang Bua Cave.
- Adults stood about 1.1 meters tall, with a brain of about 400 cubic centimeters.
- It made stone tools and lived on an island isolated by deep water.
- Whether it descended from H. erectus or an earlier ancestor is debated.

## Context & Connections
H. floresiensis shows that several human species overlapped in time with [[Homo sapiens]]. See [[Homo erectus]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes that isolated islands can produce unexpected forms. For most of the Pleistocene, the species was not alone.
