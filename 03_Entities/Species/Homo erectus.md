## Summary
Homo erectus was an early human species that lived from about 1.9 million to roughly 110,000 years ago, the first known hominin to spread widely beyond Africa.

## Facts
- Fossils have been found in Africa, Georgia (Dmanisi, about 1.8 million years old), and East and Southeast Asia.
- It made Acheulean handaxes and probably used fire; see [[Control of Fire]].
- Its brain was about 900 cubic centimeters and its body proportions were similar to modern humans'.
- It may be the longest-lived human species.

## Context & Connections
H. erectus is ancestral or related to later Homo species, including [[Homo sapiens]]. See [[Pleistocene Epoch]].
