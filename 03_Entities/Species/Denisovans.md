## Summary
Denisovans are an extinct human group known first from DNA in a finger bone found in Denisova Cave in Siberia in 2010.

## Facts
- Very few fossils are known, including a jaw from Xiahe in Tibet.
- Some living populations, especially in Oceania and East Asia, carry Denisovan DNA.
- A gene variant helping Tibetans adapt to high altitude came from Denisovans.
- The Harbin cranium (at least 146,000 years old, once nicknamed 'Dragon Man' and named Homo longi) was linked to Denisovans in 2025, using mitochondrial DNA recovered from dental calculus and ancient protein evidence, in studies published in Cell and Science. It is the first Denisovan skull identified.

## Context & Connections
Denisovans are close relatives of [[Homo neanderthalensis]] and interbred with [[Homo sapiens]] during the [[Out of Africa Dispersal]].
