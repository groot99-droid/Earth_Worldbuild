## Summary
Flowering plants (angiosperms) appear in the fossil record in the Early Cretaceous, about 130 million years ago, and now make up most land plant species.

## Facts
- The oldest widely accepted angiosperm pollen dates to the Hauterivian and Barremian ages of the Early Cretaceous, about 133 to 125 million years ago.
- The oldest well-preserved flowering-plant macrofossils, such as those from China, date to about 125 million years ago.
- Some molecular-clock studies place their origin earlier, in the Triassic or Jurassic; this gap is debated.
- Flowering plants radiated rapidly in the Cretaceous alongside pollinating insects.
- Cereals, legumes, fruits, and most other crops descend from this group.
