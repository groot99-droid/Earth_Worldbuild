## Summary
The French Revolution (1789 to 1799) overthrew the monarchy in France, declared the rights of man and citizen, and led to the rise of Napoleon Bonaparte.

## Facts
- The storming of the Bastille took place on 14 July 1789.
- The Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen was adopted in August 1789.
- Louis XVI was executed in January 1793, and the Reign of Terror followed in 1793 and 1794, killing tens of thousands.
- The revolutionary government introduced the metric system.
- Napoleon seized power in a coup in 1799.

## Context & Connections
The revolution drew on ideas of the [[Enlightenment]] and was a central event of the [[Age of Revolutions]]. It spread to the colonies and inspired the [[Haitian Revolution]].
