## Summary
The First Fleet of eleven British ships reached Botany Bay in January 1788 carrying about 1,400 people, roughly half of them convicts, and founded the colony of New South Wales at Sydney Cove.

## Facts
- The fleet arrived at Botany Bay on 18 to 20 January 1788 and settled at Sydney Cove on 26 January.
- Aboriginal Australians had lived on the continent for at least 50,000 years, and before 1788 they spoke more than 250 languages.
- The British claimed the land under the legal doctrine of terra nullius, which was rejected by the High Court of Australia in the Mabo decision of 3 June 1992.
- A smallpox epidemic struck Aboriginal communities around Sydney in 1789.
- Colonization led to conflict, dispossession, and population decline among Aboriginal peoples.
