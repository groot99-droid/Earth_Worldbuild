## Summary
The Space Age began on 4 October 1957, when the Soviet Union launched Sputnik 1, the first artificial satellite.

## Facts
- Yuri Gagarin became the first human in space in 1961.
- Apollo 11 landed the first humans on the Moon on 20 July 1969.
- Satellites now provide navigation, weather forecasting, and communications.
- The International Space Station has been continuously occupied since 2000.

## Context & Connections
The Space Age is part of the [[Information Age]] and was driven by the Cold War rivalry of the [[Nuclear Age]].
