## Summary
The Battle of Kadesh, fought around 1274 BCE near the Orontes River in what is now Syria, pitted Ramesses II of Egypt against Muwatalli II of the Hittite Empire in one of the largest chariot battles on record.

## Facts
- Ancient and modern estimates put the number of chariots involved in the thousands on both sides.
- The outcome was indecisive, and both sides claimed victory.
- Ramesses commemorated the battle in temple reliefs and texts at Abu Simbel, Karnak, and other sites.
- Hittite and Egyptian accounts both survive, which is unusual for the period.
- About fifteen years later the two powers concluded a peace treaty, around 1259 BCE, of which versions survive in both Egyptian and Hittite archives.
