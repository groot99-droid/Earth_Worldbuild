## Summary
The Haitian Revolution (1791 to 1804) was a rebellion of enslaved people against the French colony of Saint-Domingue that created the independent state of Haiti.

## Facts
- Saint-Domingue was one of the most profitable sugar colonies in the world.
- An uprising began in August 1791; Toussaint Louverture became its most prominent leader.
- Independence was declared on 1 January 1804, making Haiti the first republic founded by formerly enslaved people.
- France later demanded a large indemnity (1825), which burdened Haiti's economy for generations.

## Context & Connections
The revolution is a central event of the [[Age of Revolutions]] and a turning point in the history of the [[Atlantic Slave Trade]].
