## Summary
In 1532, Francisco Pizarro's small Spanish force captured the Inca emperor Atahualpa at Cajamarca, beginning a conquest of the Inca Empire that ended with the fall of the last Inca stronghold in 1572.

## Facts
- Pizarro had around 168 men at Cajamarca.
- The empire had just come through a civil war between Atahualpa and his half-brother Huascar, and epidemic disease had already reached the Andes.
- Atahualpa was held for a room-filling ransom of gold and silver, then executed by strangulation on 29 August 1533.
- The Spanish occupied Cusco in 1533.
- The neo-Inca state at Vilcabamba was destroyed in 1572.

## Context & Connections
The conquest ended the [[Inca Empire]] and opened the silver mines of [[Potosi]] to Spanish exploitation. It followed the pattern of disease and political division seen in the [[Columbian Exchange]].
