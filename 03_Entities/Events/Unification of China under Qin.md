## Summary
In 221 BCE the state of Qin conquered its rivals and created the first unified Chinese empire under Qin Shi Huang.

## Facts
- Qin standardized writing, weights, measures, and currency.
- The dynasty collapsed in 206 BCE, only a few years after the emperor's death.
- The Han dynasty that followed kept much of the Qin administrative system.
- The tomb complex of Qin Shi Huang includes the terracotta army.

## Context & Connections
See [[Qin Shi Huang]] and [[Han Dynasty]]. The unification begins the long imperial tradition of [[East Asia]].
