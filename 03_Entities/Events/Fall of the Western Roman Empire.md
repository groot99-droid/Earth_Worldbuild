## Summary
The Western Roman Empire ended in 476 CE, when the Germanic general Odoacer deposed the last western emperor, Romulus Augustulus.

## Facts
- The 'fall' was a long process, marked by events such as the sack of Rome by the Visigoths in 410.
- The Eastern Roman (Byzantine) Empire continued until 1453.
- Political authority in the west passed to successor kingdoms.
- Historians debate whether it was a collapse or a transformation.
