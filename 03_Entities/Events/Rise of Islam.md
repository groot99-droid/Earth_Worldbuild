## Summary
Islam emerged in Arabia beginning in 610 CE and, within about a century, spread through conquest and conversion across territory from Iberia to Central Asia.

## Facts
- Muslims date their era from the Hijra, the migration to Medina, in 622 CE.
- The Umayyad Caliphate (661 to 750) expanded into Iberia and the Indus valley.
- The Abbasid Caliphate (from 750) became a center of scholarship and trade.
- Islam is now one of the world's largest religions.
