## Summary
The Protestant Reformation began in 1517 when Martin Luther publicized his Ninety-five Theses against church practices, and it split Western Christianity into Catholic and Protestant churches.

## Facts
- Luther sent his Ninety-five Theses to Archbishop Albrecht of Mainz in October 1517; the tradition that he nailed them to a church door is uncertain.
- He was summoned to the Diet of Worms in 1521 and refused to recant.
- His German New Testament appeared in 1522, and the whole Bible in 1534.
- Other reformers included Huldrych Zwingli and John Calvin, and Henry VIII established the Church of England.
- The religious wars that followed continued into the Thirty Years' War, ending in 1648.
