## Summary
Beginning in December 1978, China under Deng Xiaoping introduced market reforms and opened to foreign trade, and over the following decades the economy grew rapidly.

## Facts
- The reforms were launched at the Third Plenum of the 11th Central Committee in December 1978.
- Agricultural reforms, the household responsibility system, replaced collective farming in the early 1980s.
- Special Economic Zones, including Shenzhen, were established starting in 1980.
- China's economy grew at high rates, averaging roughly nine to ten percent a year for several decades.
- Hundreds of millions of people were lifted out of extreme poverty.

## Context & Connections
The reforms transformed the largest population in the world within the setting of the [[Cold War]] and drew on the global manufacturing networks of the [[Digital Revolution]].
