## Summary
The Vedic period, roughly 1500 to 500 BCE, is the age of the composition of the Vedas, the oldest Indian sacred texts, in the Sanskrit language.

## Facts
- The Rigveda, the oldest of the Vedas, is generally dated to roughly 1500 to 1000 BCE.
- The texts were transmitted orally for centuries with elaborate memorization techniques.
- The language of the Vedas, Vedic Sanskrit, belongs to the Indo-Aryan branch of the Indo-European family.
- Political and cultural focus shifted from the Punjab to the Ganges plain over the period.
- The Upanishads, philosophical texts, were composed in the later part of the period.
