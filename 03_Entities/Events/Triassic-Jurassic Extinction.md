## Summary
The Triassic-Jurassic extinction, about 201.4 million years ago, removed a large share of marine and land species and cleared space for the rise of the dinosaurs.

## Facts
- An estimated 80 percent of species were lost (estimates vary).
- It coincides with massive volcanic activity associated with the breakup of Pangaea (the Central Atlantic Magmatic Province).
- Many large land reptile competitors vanished, while dinosaurs survived and diversified; see [[Dinosaurs]].

## Context & Connections
The extinction occurs early in the [[Mesozoic Era]] and lets dinosaurs dominate.

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes that dinosaur dominance was itself the result of an extinction. Every dominant group in the record had a disaster to thank for its opportunity.
