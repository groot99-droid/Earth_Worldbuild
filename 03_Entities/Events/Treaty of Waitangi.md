## Summary
The Treaty of Waitangi, first signed on 6 February 1840, is the founding agreement between the British Crown and many Maori chiefs of Aotearoa New Zealand.

## Facts
- The English and Maori texts differ in key words, especially about sovereignty and chieftainship.
- Hundreds of chiefs signed copies of the treaty in 1840.
- Conflict over land and authority followed in the New Zealand Wars of the 1840s to 1870s.
- The Waitangi Tribunal, created in 1975, investigates breaches of the treaty.

## Context & Connections
The treaty concerned the [[Polynesians]] of Aotearoa, who had settled the islands centuries earlier; see [[Polynesian Settlement of the Pacific]]. Compare the colonial treaties of the [[Scramble for Africa]].
