## Summary
The Mfecane ('the crushing') is the name given to a period of upheaval, warfare, and migration across southern Africa in the early 19th century, associated with the rise of the Zulu Kingdom.

## Facts
- Several new states emerged, including the Ndebele under Mzilikazi and the Swazi under Sobhuza.
- Many adopted Zulu-style military organization.
- Population movements reshaped the region and left large areas depopulated in some accounts.
- Historians debate the scale, the causes, and the role of Shaka, and some argue that European slave raiding and colonial pressure were also important.

## Context & Connections
