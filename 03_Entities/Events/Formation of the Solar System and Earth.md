## Summary
The Solar System formed about 4.57 billion years ago from a collapsing cloud of gas and dust, and Earth accreted from that material over tens of millions of years.

## Facts
- The Sun formed at the center of the collapsing cloud, and a disk of material around it clumped into planets.
- The oldest dated solids in the Solar System are calcium-aluminum-rich inclusions in meteorites, about 4.567 billion years old.
- Earth's age of about 4.54 billion years comes from radiometric dating of meteorites and of Earth's oldest lead.
- Earth's early interior heated and separated into a metallic core and rocky mantle.

## Context & Connections
