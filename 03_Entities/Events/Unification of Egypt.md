## Summary
Upper and Lower Egypt were joined into a single state around 3100 BCE, traditionally by a ruler called Narmer or Menes.

## Facts
- The Narmer Palette depicts a king in the crowns of Upper and Lower Egypt.
- Egypt's Old Kingdom pyramids were built about 500 years later.
- The state used hieroglyphic writing for administration and religion.
- Whether unification was a single conquest or a gradual process is debated.

## Context & Connections
The unification founds the long history of the [[Ancient Egyptians]] in the [[Nile Valley]]. See [[Giza Necropolis]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes that Egypt's unity lasted, with breaks, for about three thousand years. It relied on a strong shared story about the king's role in maintaining cosmic order.
