## Summary
Snowball Earth refers to glaciations in the late Proterozoic (the Sturtian and Marinoan, roughly 717 to 635 million years ago) in which ice may have reached the tropics.

## Facts
- Glacial deposits formed near the paleo-equator support the hypothesis.
- The glacial deposits are capped by unusual carbonate rocks.
- Scientists debate whether the oceans froze completely or whether open water remained (a 'slushball').
- The Ediacaran biota appear soon after the last glaciation.

## Context & Connections
The glaciations come shortly before the [[Cambrian Explosion]] and may be linked to it. See [[Proterozoic Eon]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes that Earth has gone through a nearly lifeless deep freeze and still recovered. Planetary systems can undergo extreme shifts and reset.
