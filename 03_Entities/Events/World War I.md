## Summary
World War I (1914 to 1918) was a global conflict centered in Europe that killed roughly 10 million soldiers and millions of civilians.

## Facts
- Trench warfare, machine guns, artillery, poison gas, and aircraft characterized the fighting.
- Four empires (German, Austro-Hungarian, Russian, and Ottoman) collapsed.
- The 1918 influenza pandemic, which killed tens of millions, spread partly through wartime movement.
- The war's settlement contributed to the conditions for [[World War II]].

## Context & Connections
The war is a turning point in the [[Industrial Age]]. See [[Sanctioned Harm]] and [[War and Conflict]].
