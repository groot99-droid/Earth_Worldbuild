## Summary
The Dutch East India Company (VOC) was a chartered trading company founded in 1602 with a monopoly on Dutch trade in Asia, among the first companies to issue tradable shares.

## Facts
- The Dutch States General chartered it in 1602.
- It sold shares that were traded on the Amsterdam exchange and is often described as one of the first publicly traded companies.
- It had powers to wage war, build forts, and make treaties in Asia.
- It founded Batavia (now Jakarta) in 1619.
- In 1621 its forces killed or expelled most of the population of the Banda Islands to secure a monopoly on nutmeg; it was dissolved in 1799.

## Context & Connections
The company built on the sea route opened by the [[Portuguese Sea Route to India]] and competed in the [[Indian Ocean Trade Network]]. It also transported enslaved people and took part in the wider [[Atlantic Slave Trade]] system.
