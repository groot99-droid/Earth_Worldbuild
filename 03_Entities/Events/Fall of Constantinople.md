## Summary
On 29 May 1453, Ottoman forces under Sultan Mehmed II captured Constantinople after a siege of about eight weeks, ending the Byzantine Empire.

## Facts
- The siege lasted from 6 April to 29 May 1453.
- The last Byzantine emperor, Constantine XI, died in the final assault.
- The Ottomans used very large cannon, including those cast by the Hungarian engineer Urban (also spelled Orban).
- The city became the Ottoman capital, later called Istanbul.
- Byzantine scholars who moved to Italy are often said to have contributed to Renaissance learning, though the extent is debated.

## Context & Connections
The fall ended the [[Byzantine Empire]], a state that had continued the Roman Empire in the east, and transformed [[Constantinople]] into the capital of the [[Ottoman Empire]].
