## Summary
The Taiping Rebellion (1850 to 1864) was a civil war in Qing China led by Hong Xiuquan, who claimed to be the younger brother of Jesus Christ, and it was among the deadliest conflicts in history.

## Facts
- The rebels captured Nanjing in 1853 and made it their 'Heavenly Capital'.
- The war devastated about 17 provinces.
- Most estimates put deaths at 20 to 30 million people, about 5 to 10 percent of China's population, and some estimates are far higher.
- Hong Xiuquan died on 1 June 1864, and Nanjing fell shortly after.

## Context & Connections
The rebellion unfolded as the [[Qing Dynasty]] reeled from the [[Opium Wars]]. See [[War and Conflict]] and [[Sanctioned Harm]].
