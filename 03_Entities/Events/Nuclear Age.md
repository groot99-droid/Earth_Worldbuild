## Summary
The Nuclear Age began on 16 July 1945 with the first nuclear weapon test, followed by the bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki on 6 and 9 August.

## Facts
- Nuclear weapons rely on [[Nuclear Fission]] (later also fusion).
- The Cold War produced arsenals of tens of thousands of warheads; about 12,000 remain today.
- Nuclear deterrence, arms-control treaties, and nonproliferation shaped world politics.
- Nuclear power also provides a share of the world's electricity.

## Context & Connections
The Nuclear Age sits within the [[Information Age]]. See [[Albert Einstein]] and [[Marie Curie]] for related science.
