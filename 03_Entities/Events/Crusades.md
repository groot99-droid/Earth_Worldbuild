## Summary
The Crusades were a series of religiously sanctioned military expeditions launched by Western European Christians from 1095, largely to capture and hold Jerusalem and other holy sites in the Levant.

## Facts
- Pope Urban II called for the First Crusade at the Council of Clermont in 1095.
- The First Crusade captured Jerusalem in 1099 and established Crusader states.
- Saladin retook Jerusalem in 1187.
- The Fourth Crusade sacked the Christian city of Constantinople in 1204.
- The last Crusader stronghold on the mainland, Acre, fell to the Mamluks on 28 May 1291.

## Context & Connections
The Crusades brought Western Europe into sustained contact with the Islamic world and the [[Byzantine Empire]], and involved the sack of [[Constantinople]] in 1204. They followed the [[Rise of Islam]] and the conquest of the Levant.
