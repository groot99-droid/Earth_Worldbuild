## Summary
The Boxer Rebellion (1899 to 1901) was an anti-foreign, anti-Christian uprising in northern China, supported by the Qing court, that ended with defeat by an eight-nation alliance.

## Facts
- The Boxers (the 'Righteous and Harmonious Fists') attacked foreign missionaries and Chinese Christians.
- The Empress Dowager Cixi eventually backed the movement and declared war on foreign powers.
- An eight-nation alliance relieved the besieged legations in Beijing.
- The Boxer Protocol of 1901 imposed a large indemnity on China.

## Context & Connections
The uprising followed the pressures begun by the [[Opium Wars]] and weakened the [[Qing Dynasty]]. See [[Cixi]].
