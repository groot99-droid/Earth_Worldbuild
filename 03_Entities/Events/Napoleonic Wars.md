## Summary
The Napoleonic Wars (1803 to 1815) were conflicts between Napoleon's French Empire and shifting coalitions of European powers, ending in his defeat at Waterloo.

## Facts
- Napoleon crowned himself emperor in 1804 and issued the Napoleonic Code.
- He defeated Austria and Russia at Austerlitz in 1805.
- His invasion of Russia in 1812 destroyed most of his Grande Armee, which numbered several hundred thousand men.
- He was defeated at Waterloo on 18 June 1815.
- The occupation of Spain and Portugal weakened imperial control and helped trigger Latin American independence movements.
