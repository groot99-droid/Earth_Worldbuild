## Summary
The Soviet Union dissolved on 26 December 1991 after the revolutions of 1989 in Eastern Europe and a failed coup in August 1991, ending the Cold War.

## Facts
- The Berlin Wall opened on 9 November 1989.
- Mikhail Gorbachev's reforms of glasnost (openness) and perestroika (restructuring) began in the mid-1980s.
- Hardliners attempted a coup from 18 to 22 August 1991 and failed.
- Russia, Ukraine, and Belarus agreed to dissolve the Union on 8 December 1991.
- Fifteen successor states emerged.
