## Summary
Writing was invented independently at least three or four times, first in Mesopotamia about 3400 to 3200 BCE, as a way to record goods and obligations.

## Facts
- The earliest proto-cuneiform tablets from Uruk are accounting records.
- Egyptian hieroglyphs appear by about 3200 BCE, and Chinese writing is securely attested by about 1250 BCE.
- Mesoamerican writing appears by roughly 600 BCE, and possibly earlier.
- Writing systems developed from pictures and tokens toward signs that represent sounds.

The Observer calls this memory-outsourcing: information moves from the mind to an object. The first users were not poets but accountants, which suggests that the tool arose from a need for trust between strangers.
