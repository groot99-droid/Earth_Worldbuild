## Summary
The Russian Revolution of 1917 overthrew the Tsarist monarchy, and the Bolsheviks under Vladimir Lenin then seized power, leading to a civil war and the formation of the Soviet Union in 1922.

## Facts
- The February Revolution in 1917 forced Tsar Nicholas II to abdicate in March by the Western calendar.
- The Bolsheviks seized power in the October Revolution, on 7 November in the Western calendar.
- A civil war between the Bolshevik Reds and their opponents lasted from 1918 to 1922.
- The Soviet Union was established in December 1922.
- Russia withdrew from World War I through the Treaty of Brest-Litovsk in 1918.
