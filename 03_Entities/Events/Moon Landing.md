## Summary
On 20 July 1969, Apollo 11 astronauts Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin landed on the Moon while Michael Collins orbited above, the first time humans stood on another world.

## Facts
- The lunar module Eagle landed in the Sea of Tranquility on 20 July 1969.
- Armstrong stepped onto the surface on 20 July in the United States, which was 21 July in Universal Time.
- Twelve people walked on the Moon between 1969 and 1972 in six landings.
- The Apollo missions returned about 382 kilograms of lunar rock and soil.
- Analysis of the samples, notably their oxygen isotopes, which match Earth's very closely, is consistent with the giant impact hypothesis for the Moon's origin, provided the impactor had an Earth-like composition. That close match is also a puzzle for the hypothesis.
