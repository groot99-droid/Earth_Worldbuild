## Summary
The Suez Canal, opened on 17 November 1869, is a sea-level waterway across Egypt linking the Mediterranean and the Red Sea and shortening the sea route between Europe and Asia.

## Facts
- It was built by a French company under Ferdinand de Lesseps, with a great deal of Egyptian forced and paid labor.
- It is about 193 kilometers long.
- Britain bought Egypt's shares in the canal company in 1875 and occupied Egypt in 1882.
- The canal cut the voyage between Europe and India by thousands of kilometers.

## Context & Connections
The canal reshaped the [[Indian Ocean Trade Network]] for the industrial age and sits in the [[Nile Valley]] region. See [[Scramble for Africa]] and [[Steam Engine]].
