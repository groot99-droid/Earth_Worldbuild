## Summary
The Green Revolution was the spread of high-yield crop varieties, fertilizers, and irrigation from the 1940s to the 1970s, which greatly increased grain production.

## Facts
- Plant breeder Norman Borlaug developed high-yield semi-dwarf wheat in Mexico, and IR8 rice was released in 1966.
- India, Pakistan, Mexico, and the Philippines were among the earliest and largest adopters.
- The revolution is credited with averting large famines, but also brought heavy fertilizer and water use.
- Yields and populations rose together.
