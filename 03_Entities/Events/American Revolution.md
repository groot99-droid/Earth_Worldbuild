## Summary
The American Revolution (1775 to 1783) was the war by which thirteen British colonies in North America became the independent United States.

## Facts
- Fighting began at Lexington and Concord in April 1775.
- The Declaration of Independence was adopted on 4 July 1776.
- France entered the war on the American side in 1778, and the British army surrendered at Yorktown in 1781.
- The Treaty of Paris of 1783 recognized American independence.
- The Haudenosaunee Confederacy divided over the war, with most nations siding with Britain and some with the Americans.

## Context & Connections
The revolution followed the debts of the [[Seven Years' War]] and was one of the first events of the [[Age of Revolutions]]. It split the [[Haudenosaunee Confederacy]].
