## Summary
Modern climate change is the warming of Earth caused mainly by human emissions of greenhouse gases, mostly from burning fossil fuels.

## Facts
- Global surface temperature was about 1.1 degrees Celsius above 1850 to 1900 levels in 2011 to 2020 (IPCC), and has risen further since.
- Atmospheric carbon dioxide has risen from about 280 parts per million before the Industrial Revolution to a global average of about 423 in 2024 (422.8 in NOAA's record).
- The Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change states that human influence has unequivocally warmed the atmosphere, ocean, and land.
- The Paris Agreement, reached on 12 December 2015, set goals of holding warming well below 2 degrees Celsius and pursuing efforts to limit it to 1.5 degrees.
