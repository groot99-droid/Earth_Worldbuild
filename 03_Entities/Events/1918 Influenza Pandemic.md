## Summary
The 1918 influenza pandemic, caused by an H1N1 virus, infected roughly a third of the world's population and killed an estimated 50 million people from 1918 to 1920.

## Facts
- Estimates of deaths range widely, from about 17 million to 100 million, with 50 million often cited.
- It is called the Spanish flu because neutral Spain reported openly on it during wartime censorship in other countries.
- It killed an unusually high share of healthy young adults.
- Troop movements during World War I helped spread it.
- Researchers reconstructed the virus from preserved tissue, publishing the genome in 2005.

## Context & Connections
