## Summary
Pachacuti (about 1418 to 1471) was the ninth Sapa Inca, under whom the Inca state expanded from a regional power into an empire.

## Facts
- He is credited with rebuilding Cusco and expanding Inca rule along the Andes.
- Machu Picchu is believed to have been built as one of his royal estates.
- He organized the state with labor obligations and roads.
- Sources on him were written after the Spanish conquest and blend history and legend.

## Context & Connections
Pachacuti is the central figure of the rise of the [[Inca Empire]]. See [[Machu Picchu]].
