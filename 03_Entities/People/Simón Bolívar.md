## Summary
Simón Bolívar (1783 to 1830) was a Venezuelan military and political leader who led the independence of several South American countries from Spain and tried to unite them as Gran Colombia.

## Facts
- He was born in Caracas to a wealthy family.
- He and Sucre led the campaigns that freed present-day Venezuela, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, and Bolivia.
- He met José de San Martín at Guayaquil in 1822.
- Gran Colombia split apart within seven years, and Bolívar died in 1830.

## Context & Connections
Bolívar is the central figure of the [[Latin American Wars of Independence]], part of the [[Age of Revolutions]].
