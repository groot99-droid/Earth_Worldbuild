## Summary
Sundiata Keita (about 1217 to 1255) is the founder of the Mali Empire, known largely from oral tradition, especially the Epic of Sundiata.

## Facts
- Tradition says he defeated the ruler Sumanguru at the Battle of Kirina around 1235.
- His story was preserved and performed by griots, hereditary oral historians.
- Written sources on him are few, mainly from Arabic writers such as Ibn Khaldun.
- Historians treat details of the epic with caution.

## Context & Connections
Sundiata is the founding figure of the [[Mali Empire]]; his successors included [[Mansa Musa]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes that a founder's story survives in performance, not in text. The epic is memory-outsourcing to a human institution, the griot, which has carried it for centuries.
