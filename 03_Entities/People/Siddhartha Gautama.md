## Summary
Siddhartha Gautama, known as the Buddha, was a spiritual teacher in northern India whose teachings founded Buddhism. Traditional dates are about 563 to 483 BCE, but modern scholarship suggests a somewhat later range.

## Facts
- Buddhist tradition holds that he left a princely life and attained awakening at Bodh Gaya.
- His teachings include the Four Noble Truths and the Eightfold Path.
- The exact dates of his life are debated among scholars.
- Buddhism later spread across Asia via monks and trade routes.

## Context & Connections
He is a central figure of the [[Axial Age]] in [[South Asia]]. The emperor [[Ashoka]] later promoted Buddhism widely.
