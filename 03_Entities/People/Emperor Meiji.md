## Summary
Emperor Meiji (1852 to 1912) was the Emperor of Japan from 1867, in whose name the Meiji Restoration transformed Japan into a modern industrial state.

## Facts
- He came to the throne in 1867 at the age of about fourteen.
- The Charter Oath (1868) was issued in his name, promising reform.
- His reign saw a new constitution (1889), industrialization, and Japan's rise as a regional power.
- He died in 1912.

## Context & Connections
He was the symbol of the [[Meiji Restoration]] that ended the [[Tokugawa Shogunate]].

## Observer's Reading
