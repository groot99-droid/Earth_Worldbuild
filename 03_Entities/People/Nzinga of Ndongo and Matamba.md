## Summary
Nzinga (about 1583 to 1663) was a ruler of the kingdoms of Ndongo and Matamba in present-day Angola who resisted Portuguese expansion and the slave trade for decades.

## Facts
- She negotiated with the Portuguese in 1622 as an ambassador for her brother.
- She later took power and led resistance, including an alliance with the Dutch (1641 to 1648).
- She used both diplomacy and war, and took in people escaping from slavery.
- She is a symbol of resistance in Angola today.

## Context & Connections
Nzinga's reign shows African states as active participants in the [[Atlantic Slave Trade]] period, both as resisters and as traders. See [[Sub-Saharan Africa]].
