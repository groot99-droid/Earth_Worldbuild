## Summary
Zheng He (1371 to 1433) was a Chinese admiral who led seven large naval expeditions between 1405 and 1433, reaching Southeast Asia, India, Arabia, and East Africa.

## Facts
- He served the Ming emperor Yongle and was a Muslim of Yunnan origin.
- His fleets included very large ships and thousands of crew members.
- The voyages displayed Ming power and collected tribute; they were then stopped.
- They came decades before European ocean voyages of the same type.

## Context & Connections
Zheng He's voyages linked [[East Asia]], [[Southeast Asia]], and the western Indian Ocean during the [[Medieval Period]].
