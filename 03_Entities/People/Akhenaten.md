## Summary
Akhenaten was an Egyptian pharaoh (reigned about 1353 to 1336 BCE) who promoted the worship of the sun disk Aten above the traditional gods.

## Facts
- He built a new capital at Akhetaten (modern Amarna).
- Art of his reign has a distinctive, naturalistic style.
- Traditional religion was restored soon after his death, under his successors including Tutankhamun.
- His wife was Nefertiti.

## Context & Connections
Akhenaten was a ruler of the [[Ancient Egyptians]]. His religious reform is a rare example of a state attempting to replace a whole meaning-engine from the top.

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes that the reform did not outlast the ruler. A meaning-engine embedded in daily life, priesthoods, and local ritual proved harder to replace than to declare.
