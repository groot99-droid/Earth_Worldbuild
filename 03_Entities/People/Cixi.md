## Summary
The Empress Dowager Cixi (1835 to 1908) was the dominant political figure of late Qing China, ruling as regent for most of the period from 1861 to 1908.

## Facts
- She became regent in 1861 for her young son, the Tongzhi Emperor, and later for the Guangxu Emperor.
- Her period includes the Self-Strengthening Movement, an effort to modernize China's defenses and industry.
- She supported the Boxers in 1900, and after their defeat she pursued the 'New Policies' reforms.
- Historians debate her legacy; older accounts portray her as a reactionary, and recent work stresses her political skill and the limits she faced.

## Context & Connections
Cixi ruled the [[Qing Dynasty]] through the [[Opium Wars]] aftermath and the [[Boxer Rebellion]]. Compare [[Wu Zetian]].
