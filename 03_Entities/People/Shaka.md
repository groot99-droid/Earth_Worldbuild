## Summary
Shaka kaSenzangakhona (about 1787 to 1828) was the founder of the Zulu Kingdom, who transformed its military and political organization.

## Facts
- He came to power around 1816 and expanded the Zulu state quickly.
- He is credited with new weapons and tactics, including the short stabbing spear and a 'buffalo horns' formation.
- He was assassinated in 1828 by his half-brothers.
- Much of what is told about him comes from colonial-era European accounts and oral tradition, and is debated.
