## Summary
Mansa Musa (about 1280 to about 1337) was the ruler of the Mali Empire, famous for his pilgrimage to Mecca in 1324 and for the great wealth of his empire.

## Facts
- Mali controlled major gold-producing regions of West Africa.
- His pilgrimage to Mecca, with a large retinue and quantities of gold, was widely reported and reportedly disrupted the gold market in Cairo.
- He supported building projects and scholarship in cities such as [[Timbuktu]].
- Sources about his life are mainly Arabic accounts and later oral tradition.

## Context & Connections
Mansa Musa ruled the [[Mali Empire]] in [[Sub-Saharan Africa]] and linked it to the wider Islamic world.
