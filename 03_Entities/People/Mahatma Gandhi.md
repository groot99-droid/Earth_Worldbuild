## Summary
Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi (1869 to 1948) was an Indian lawyer and political leader who led the movement for Indian independence using nonviolent resistance.

## Facts
- He developed satyagraha (insistence on truth), first in South Africa, then in India.
- The Salt March of 1930 was a major act of civil disobedience.
- India became independent in 1947 and was partitioned; he opposed partition.
- He was assassinated in January 1948.

## Context & Connections
Gandhi's methods influenced later movements, including that of [[Nelson Mandela]]'s generation. See [[South Asia]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes a distinctive experiment: a mass movement that deliberately renounced sanctioned harm as a tactic and still shifted a global empire.
