## Summary
Augustus (63 BCE to 14 CE) was the first Roman emperor, who ended a century of civil war and built the imperial system that lasted for centuries.

## Facts
- Born Gaius Octavius, he was the adopted heir of Julius Caesar.
- After defeating rivals, he took the name Augustus in 27 BCE while keeping republican forms.
- His reign began the Pax Romana, a long period of relative stability.
- He reorganized the army, provinces, and taxation.

## Context & Connections
See [[Romans]] and [[Rome]]. Augustus created the political shape of the empire that dominated the [[Mediterranean Basin]].
