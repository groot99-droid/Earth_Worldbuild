## Summary
Johannes Gutenberg (about 1400 to 1468) was a German metalworker who developed a movable-type printing press in Mainz around 1440 and printed the Gutenberg Bible about 1455.

## Facts
- He combined metal type, oil-based ink, and a press adapted from wine-making.
- The 42-line Bible is the earliest major book printed from movable type in Europe.
- He faced financial difficulties and lost control of his workshop to a creditor.
- Printing spread rapidly across Europe within decades.

## Context & Connections
See [[Movable-Type Printing Press]]. Gutenberg's work supported the [[Scientific Revolution]] and religious reform.
