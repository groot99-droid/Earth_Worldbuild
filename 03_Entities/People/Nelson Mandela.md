## Summary
Nelson Mandela (1918 to 2013) was a South African anti-apartheid leader who was imprisoned for 27 years and became the country's first democratically elected president in 1994.

## Facts
- He co-founded the African National Congress Youth League and helped lead the resistance to apartheid.
- He was imprisoned from 1962 to 1990.
- After release, he negotiated the end of apartheid.
- He served as president from 1994 to 1999.
