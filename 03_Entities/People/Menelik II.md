## Summary
Menelik II (1844 to 1913) was emperor of Ethiopia from 1889 and the leader who defeated Italy at Adwa in 1896.

## Facts
- He expanded the empire southward and founded Addis Ababa as the capital.
- He imported modern arms and pursued technological modernization.
- He won the [[Battle of Adwa]] on 1 March 1896.
- His empire's expansion brought conquest of neighboring peoples, and it is debated.

## Context & Connections
Menelik led Ethiopia through the [[Scramble for Africa]]. See [[Battle of Adwa]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes that a ruler could both resist a colonial power and build his own empire in his neighborhood. The line between the colonized and the colonizer was not always simple.
