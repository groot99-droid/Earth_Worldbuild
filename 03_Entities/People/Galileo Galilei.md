## Summary
Galileo Galilei (1564 to 1642) was an Italian scientist who used the telescope and experiments to support the Copernican view that the Earth orbits the Sun.

## Facts
- In 1609 and 1610 he observed the Moon's mountains and four moons of Jupiter.
- He studied falling bodies and motion using experiments.
- In 1633 the Roman Inquisition tried him and he was placed under house arrest for the rest of his life.
- His 'Dialogue Concerning the Two Chief World Systems' was published in 1632.

## Context & Connections
Galileo is a central figure of the [[Scientific Revolution]] and a predecessor of [[Isaac Newton]].
