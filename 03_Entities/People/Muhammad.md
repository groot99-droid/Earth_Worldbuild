## Summary
Muhammad (about 570 to 632 CE) was an Arabian religious and political leader, regarded by Muslims as the last prophet, who founded the community that became the Islamic world.

## Facts
- He was born in Mecca and, according to Islamic tradition, began receiving revelations around 610, later compiled as the Quran.
- In 622 he and his followers migrated to Medina (the Hijra), the start of the Islamic calendar.
- By his death, most of the Arabian Peninsula had joined the community.
- Islam is now followed by roughly a quarter of the world's population.

## Context & Connections
See [[Rise of Islam]]. His community's expansion after his death led to the [[Abbasid Caliphate]] and a new zone of shared learning.
