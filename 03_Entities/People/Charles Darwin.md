## Summary
Charles Darwin (1809 to 1882) was an English naturalist who proposed the theory of evolution by natural selection, published in 'On the Origin of Species' in 1859.

## Facts
- His voyage on HMS Beagle (1831 to 1836) gave him observations that led to his theory.
- Alfred Russel Wallace independently reached the same idea, and the two announced it together in 1858.
- 'The Descent of Man' (1871) applied the theory to human origins.
- Modern genetics later provided the mechanism of inheritance that Darwin lacked.
