## Summary
Isaac Newton (1642 to 1727) was an English mathematician and physicist whose laws of motion and universal gravitation formed the basis of classical mechanics.

## Facts
- His 'Principia' (1687) presents the laws of motion and the law of gravitation.
- He developed calculus, independently of Leibniz, leading to a priority dispute.
- He studied optics, showing that white light is composed of colors.
- He also spent much time on alchemy and theology.

## Context & Connections
Newton completed the work of the [[Scientific Revolution]] begun by [[Galileo Galilei]]. His physics framed science until the time of [[Albert Einstein]].
