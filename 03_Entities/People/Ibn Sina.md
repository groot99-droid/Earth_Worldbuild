## Summary
Ibn Sina (Avicenna, 980 to 1037) was a Persian physician and philosopher whose 'Canon of Medicine' became a standard medical text in the Islamic world and Europe for centuries.

## Facts
- He was born near Bukhara in Central Asia.
- The Canon of Medicine (completed about 1025) organized medical knowledge in a systematic way.
- It was translated into Latin and used in European universities into the 17th century.
- His philosophical 'Book of Healing' also influenced medieval Europe.

## Context & Connections
Ibn Sina drew on the tradition of the [[Translation Movement and the House of Wisdom]] and the wider [[Abbasid Caliphate]] world.
