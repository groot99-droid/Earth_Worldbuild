## Summary
Genghis Khan (born Temujin, about 1162 to 1227) united the Mongol tribes in 1206 and founded the Mongol Empire.

## Facts
- He built an army based on merit and loyalty rather than clan.
- His campaigns overran Central Asia, northern China, and Persia.
- He promoted a written script and a legal code (the Yassa) and encouraged religious tolerance.
- He died in 1227; the location of his tomb is unknown.

## Context & Connections
See [[Mongol Conquests]] and [[Mongol Empire]]. His empire brought the [[Central Asian Steppe]] to the center of Eurasian history.
