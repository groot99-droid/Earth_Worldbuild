## Summary
Albert Einstein (1879 to 1955) was a German-born physicist who developed the theories of special and general relativity and made key contributions to quantum theory.

## Facts
- In 1905 he published papers on special relativity, the photoelectric effect, and Brownian motion.
- General relativity (1915) describes gravity as the curvature of space and time.
- He won the 1921 Nobel Prize in Physics for the photoelectric effect.
- In 1939 he signed a letter to President Roosevelt about the possibility of atomic weapons.

## Context & Connections
Einstein's work extended that of [[Isaac Newton]] and connects to the [[Nuclear Age]] through the equivalence of mass and energy.
