## Summary
Paper was developed in China around the 2nd century BCE to the 2nd century CE and spread across Asia and, by the 12th century, to Europe.

## Facts
- Tradition credits the court official Cai Lun in 105 CE, but earlier paper fragments have been found.
- It was made from plant fibers, rags, and bark.
- Paper-making spread through the Islamic world, notably in Samarkand and Baghdad.
- Paper was cheaper than parchment or papyrus and made large-scale record-keeping possible.

## Context & Connections
Paper developed under the [[Han Dynasty]], spread via the [[Abbasid Caliphate]], and enabled the [[Movable-Type Printing Press]].
