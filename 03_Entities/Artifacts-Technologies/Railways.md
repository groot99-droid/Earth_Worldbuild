## Summary
Railways using steam locomotives began with the Stockton and Darlington Railway in England in 1825 and reshaped transport, trade, and the experience of time in the 19th century.

## Facts
- The Stockton and Darlington Railway opened in 1825 as the first public railway to use steam locomotives.
- The Liverpool and Manchester Railway, opened in 1830, was the first intercity line run entirely by steam to a timetable.
- The locomotive Rocket, built by Robert Stephenson, won the Rainhill Trials in 1829.
- The first United States transcontinental railroad was completed at Promontory Summit, Utah, on 10 May 1869.
- Railway timetables led to standardized time zones, as in Britain from the 1840s and North America in 1883.
