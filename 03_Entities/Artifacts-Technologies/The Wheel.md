## Summary
Wheeled vehicles appear in the archaeological record around 3500 to 3000 BCE, in Mesopotamia, Europe, and the steppe.

## Facts
- The oldest surviving wooden wheel, from the Ljubljana Marshes in Slovenia, is about 5,150 years old (radiocarbon dated to roughly 5,100 to 5,350 years ago).
- Pictographs from Uruk show sleds and wheeled carts.
- The wheel arrived in East Asia later, and was not used for transport in the Americas before contact.
- Potter's wheels used rotation for shaping clay.

## Context & Connections
The wheel belongs to the [[Bronze Age]] and to the horse-and-wheel complex of the [[Indo-European Language Spread]].
