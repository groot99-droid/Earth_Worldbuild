## Summary
Pottery, fired clay containers, appeared in East Asia at least 20,000 years ago, well before farming, and later became widespread in the Neolithic.

## Facts
- Pottery from Xianrendong Cave in China dates to about 20,000 years ago.
- Early Japanese Jomon pottery dates to about 16,000 years ago.
- Pots allow cooking, storage, and transport of food and liquids.
- Pottery styles are widely used by archaeologists to identify cultures.

## Context & Connections
Pottery links the [[Paleolithic]] to the [[Neolithic]]. See [[The Agricultural Revolution]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes that pots are a technology of storage, and storage enables saving, exchange, and inequality. Clay fragments are also the most durable record of everyday life.
