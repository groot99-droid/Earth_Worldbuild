## Summary
Woodblock printing, carving a whole page into a block of wood, inked and pressed onto paper, was developed in East Asia by the 8th century CE.

## Facts
- The Diamond Sutra of 868 is the oldest dated printed book.
- Early printing was largely for Buddhist texts and calendars.
- The technique spread to Korea, Japan, and Vietnam.
- Bi Sheng invented movable type in China in about 1040.

## Context & Connections
Woodblock printing depended on [[Paper]], developed under the [[Tang Dynasty]], and preceded the [[Movable-Type Printing Press]] of Europe.

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes that printing appeared in more than one place and in a religious setting first. Copying scripture for merit was a strong motive for copying at scale.
