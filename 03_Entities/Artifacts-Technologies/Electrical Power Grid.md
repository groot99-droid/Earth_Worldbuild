## Summary
Electrical power grids distribute electricity from central generators to homes and factories. Thomas Edison opened the first commercial power station in New York in 1882.

## Facts
- Alternating current, promoted by Westinghouse and Tesla, won because it could be transmitted over long distances.
- Niagara Falls began delivering power to Buffalo in 1896.
- Electricity powers lighting, motors, communication, and computers.
- Today, grids connect most of the world's population.

## Context & Connections
The grid followed the [[Steam Engine]] and became the base for the [[Digital Computer]].
