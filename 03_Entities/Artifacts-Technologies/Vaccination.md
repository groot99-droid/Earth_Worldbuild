## Summary
Vaccination began in 1796 when Edward Jenner showed that cowpox inoculation protected against smallpox, and it led to the eradication of smallpox in 1980.

## Facts
- Inoculation with smallpox material (variolation) was practiced earlier in Asia, Africa, and the Middle East.
- Smallpox killed about 300 million people in the 20th century alone before eradication.
- The World Health Organization declared smallpox eradicated in 1980.
- Vaccines have since been developed against many diseases.
