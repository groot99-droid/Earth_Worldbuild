## Summary
Gunpowder, a mix of saltpeter, sulfur, and charcoal, was discovered in China around the 9th century and became a military technology under the Song.

## Facts
- It was probably discovered by alchemists seeking elixirs.
- The Song dynasty used gunpowder in fire arrows, bombs, and early firearms.
- It spread west through the Mongol empire and reached Europe by the 13th century.
- Cannons and guns transformed warfare and state power.

## Context & Connections
Gunpowder emerged in [[East Asia]], was developed under the [[Song Dynasty]], and spread with the [[Mongol Conquests]].
