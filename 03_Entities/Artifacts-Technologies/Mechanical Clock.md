## Summary
Mechanical clocks driven by weights and controlled by an escapement appeared in European towns and monasteries around 1300, after earlier Chinese and Islamic water clocks.

## Facts
- Su Song built an astronomical clock tower in China in 1088.
- Town clocks in European cities regulated work, markets, and prayer.
- The pendulum clock (Huygens, 1656) greatly improved accuracy.
- Accurate clocks were needed to measure longitude at sea.

## Context & Connections
The clock changed how people lived in the [[Medieval Period]] and prepared for factory schedules in the [[Industrial Revolution]].
