## Summary
The digital computer is a programmable machine that processes information as binary numbers. The ENIAC was completed in 1945, after earlier machines such as Zuse's Z3 (1941) and Colossus (1943).

## Facts
- The transistor (1947) and the integrated circuit (about 1958) allowed computers to shrink and become cheaper.
- The microprocessor (1971) put a computer on one chip.
- Computing power has roughly doubled every two years, a pattern known as Moore's law.
- Computers now run in phones, cars, appliances, and networks.

## Context & Connections
The computer implements ideas of [[Alan Turing]] and [[Ada Lovelace]] and drives the [[Digital Revolution]].
