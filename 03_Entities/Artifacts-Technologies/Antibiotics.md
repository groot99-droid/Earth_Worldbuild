## Summary
Antibiotics are drugs that kill or inhibit bacteria. Alexander Fleming discovered penicillin in 1928, and mass production began in the 1940s.

## Facts
- Howard Florey and Ernst Chain developed penicillin for medical use, and the three shared the 1945 Nobel Prize.
- Antibiotics greatly reduced deaths from infections and surgery.
- Overuse has caused widespread antibiotic resistance.
- Life expectancy rose sharply in the 20th century, helped by sanitation, vaccines, and antibiotics.
