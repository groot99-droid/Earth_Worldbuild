## Summary
The magnetic compass was used for navigation at sea in China by about the 12th century, after earlier use in divination, and reached Europe soon after.

## Facts
- Early compasses used lodestone, a naturally magnetized mineral.
- Chinese texts of the 11th and 12th centuries describe floating needle compasses.
- European references appear in the late 12th century.
- The compass allowed navigation out of sight of land.

## Context & Connections
The compass developed under the [[Song Dynasty]] and supported voyages such as [[Zheng He]]'s and later European oceanic voyages.
