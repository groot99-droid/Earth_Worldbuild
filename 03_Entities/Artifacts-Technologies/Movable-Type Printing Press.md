## Summary
The movable-type printing press, developed by Johannes Gutenberg in Mainz around 1440, allowed books to be made quickly and cheaply in Europe.

## Facts
- Earlier movable type was used in China (Bi Sheng, about 1040) and Korea (metal type, 1377).
- Gutenberg's innovations included an alloy for metal type, oil-based ink, and a screw press.
- An estimated 20 million printed volumes had been produced by 1500.
- Printing helped spread Reformation pamphlets and scientific works.
