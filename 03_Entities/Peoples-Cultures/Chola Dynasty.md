## Summary
The Chola dynasty ruled much of southern India from around 850 to 1279 CE and was a naval power that traded and fought across the Bay of Bengal.

## Facts
- Vijayalaya Chola captured Thanjavur around 850 CE.
- Rajaraja I (r. 985 to 1014) built the Brihadisvara Temple in Thanjavur, completed around 1010, now a UNESCO World Heritage site.
- Rajendra I sent a naval expedition against Srivijaya around 1025.
- The Cholas traded with China and Southeast Asia.
- Chola bronzes, including the dancing Shiva (Nataraja), are celebrated works of art.
