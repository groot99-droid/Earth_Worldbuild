## Summary
The Phoenicians were seafaring traders from city-states on the Levant coast (such as Tyre, Sidon, and Byblos) who founded colonies across the Mediterranean.

## Facts
- They founded Carthage, traditionally in 814 BCE.
- They spread a simple alphabet of about 22 letters.
- They were known for purple dye and for shipbuilding.
- Few Phoenician writings survive; most of what is known comes from others.

## Context & Connections
The Phoenicians linked the [[Mediterranean Basin]] by sea and spread the [[Alphabet]] after the [[Bronze Age Collapse]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes a network of commercial city-states rather than a territorial empire, an example of an exchange-web that carries a technology further than any army.
