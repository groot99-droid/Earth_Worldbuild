## Summary
The Ming dynasty ruled China from 1368 to 1644 after expelling the Mongol-led Yuan dynasty.

## Facts
- The Forbidden City in Beijing was completed in 1420.
- Much of the Great Wall in its present form was built in this period.
- [[Zheng He]]'s voyages took place under the early Ming.
- American silver, arriving through the Manila trade, became a major part of the Ming economy.

## Context & Connections
The Ming succeeded the [[Song Dynasty]] and [[Mongol Empire]] rule and was drawn into the global silver flows of the [[Columbian Exchange]].
