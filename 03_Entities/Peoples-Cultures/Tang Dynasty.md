## Summary
The Tang dynasty ruled China from 618 to 907 CE and is regarded as a high point of Chinese poetry, art, and cosmopolitan culture.

## Facts
- Its capital Chang'an was one of the world's largest cities, with about a million people.
- Poets such as Li Bai and Du Fu wrote in this period.
- The earliest dated printed book, the Diamond Sutra, was made in 868; see [[Woodblock Printing]].
- The An Lushan Rebellion (755 to 763) weakened the dynasty; death-toll estimates range from about 13 to 36 million and are disputed.

## Context & Connections
The Tang followed the [[Han Dynasty]] tradition and preceded the [[Song Dynasty]]. [[Wu Zetian]] ruled during it.

## Observer's Reading
