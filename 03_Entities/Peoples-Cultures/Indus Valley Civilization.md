## Summary
The Indus Valley Civilization (also called the Harappan) was a Bronze Age urban culture of the Indus River basin, at its height around 2600 to 1900 BCE.

## Facts
- Major cities included Harappa and [[Mohenjo-daro]].
- Cities had planned streets and drainage, and goods were made to standard weights.
- It traded with Mesopotamia.
- Its script has not been deciphered.

## Context & Connections
The civilization is a parallel case to [[The Urban Revolution]] in Mesopotamia; see [[South Asia]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes the puzzle of a large urban society with few obvious palaces or royal monuments, which challenges the assumption that cities always come with kings.
