## Summary
The ancient Greeks were a group of city-states around the Aegean and beyond, whose politics, philosophy, and art shaped Mediterranean and later world culture.

## Facts
- They developed citizen-based political systems, including Athenian democracy from about 508 BCE.
- Philosophers and scientists included Socrates, Plato, and [[Aristotle]].
- City-states such as Athens and Sparta fought frequently, including the Peloponnesian War (431 to 404 BCE).
- The Hellenistic period followed [[Alexander the Great]].
