## Summary
The Mongol Empire was the largest contiguous land empire in history, founded by Genghis Khan in 1206 and later divided into four khanates.

## Facts
- Kublai Khan founded the Yuan dynasty in China in 1271.
- The empire ran a relay postal system (the yam) that sped communication across Eurasia.
- It promoted trade and tolerated many religions.
- The successor khanates gradually adopted local languages and religions.
