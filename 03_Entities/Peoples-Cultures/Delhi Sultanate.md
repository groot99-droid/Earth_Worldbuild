## Summary
The Delhi Sultanate was a series of five Muslim dynasties that ruled much of northern India from 1206 until Babur's victory at Panipat in 1526.

## Facts
- It began after the conquests of Muhammad of Ghor, and Qutb al-Din Aibak became the first sultan.
- The five dynasties were the Mamluk, Khalji, Tughlaq, Sayyid, and Lodi.
- It resisted several Mongol invasions in the 13th century.
- Timur sacked Delhi in 1398.
- It was replaced by the Mughal Empire after the First Battle of Panipat in 1526.

## Context & Connections
The sultanate connected northern India to the wider Islamic world, faced the [[Mongol Empire]], suffered the raid of [[Timur]], and was followed by the [[Mughal Empire]].
