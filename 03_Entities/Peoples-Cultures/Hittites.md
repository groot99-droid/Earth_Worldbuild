## Summary
The Hittites were a Bronze Age people of Anatolia who built an empire from their capital Hattusa, speaking the earliest attested Indo-European language.

## Facts
- Hattusa lies near modern Bogazkale in central Turkey, and its archive of cuneiform tablets was found in the early 20th century.
- Hittite is the oldest recorded Indo-European language; Bedrich Hrozny showed its Indo-European nature in 1915.
- Hittite forces raided Babylon around 1595 BCE, according to the conventional chronology.
- The Hittites fought Egypt at the Battle of Kadesh around 1274 BCE, and later made a treaty with Ramesses II, around 1259 BCE.
- The empire collapsed around 1200 to 1180 BCE in the Bronze Age Collapse.
