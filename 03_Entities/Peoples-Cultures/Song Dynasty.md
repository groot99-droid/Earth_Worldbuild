## Summary
The Song dynasty ruled China from 960 to 1279 CE and presided over major advances in commerce, technology, and urban life.

## Facts
- It issued some of the world's first government paper money.
- Cities such as Kaifeng and Hangzhou were among the largest in the world.
- Advances included [[Gunpowder]] weapons, the [[Magnetic Compass]] for navigation, and movable-type printing.
- The examination system and Neo-Confucian thought shaped elite culture.

## Context & Connections
The Song built on the [[Han Dynasty]] tradition and was later conquered by the [[Mongol Empire]].

## Observer's Reading
