## Summary
The Timurid Empire was a Turco-Mongol state founded by Timur in 1370 that ruled Central Asia and Iran, with Samarkand as its capital, and is remembered for its architecture and science.

## Facts
- Timur took power in the region in 1370 and died in 1405.
- After his death, his son Shah Rukh and grandson [[Ulugh Beg]] ruled key parts of the empire.
- Ulugh Beg built a madrasa and an observatory at [[Samarkand]] in the 1410s and 1420s.
- Babur, founder of the [[Mughal Empire]], was a descendant of Timur.

## Context & Connections
The Timurids claimed the heritage of the [[Mongol Empire]] and passed their court culture to the [[Mughal Empire]].
