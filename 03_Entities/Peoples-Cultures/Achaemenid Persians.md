## Summary
The Achaemenid Persians built the largest empire the world had yet seen, founded by Cyrus the Great about 550 BCE and lasting until Alexander's conquest in 330 BCE.

## Facts
- The empire stretched from Egypt and Anatolia to the Indus valley.
- It was divided into provinces called satrapies and connected by the Royal Road.
- The Cyrus Cylinder is often cited as a record of an imperial policy of tolerance toward local customs.
- Zoroastrianism was influential at the Persian court.
