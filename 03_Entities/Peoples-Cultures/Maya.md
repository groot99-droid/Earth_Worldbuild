## Summary
The Maya are a group of Mesoamerican peoples who developed city-states, a full writing system, and advanced astronomy, and whose Classic period lasted about 250 to 900 CE.

## Facts
- Major cities included Tikal and Calakmul.
- Maya writing combines signs for words and syllables and is largely deciphered.
- They kept precise calendars and used the concept of zero.
- Many Classic-period cities were abandoned around 800 to 900 CE; Maya peoples and languages continue today, with millions of speakers.

## Context & Connections
The Maya built on earlier traditions such as those of the [[Olmecs]]. See [[Mesoamerica]] and [[Teotihuacan]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes that a Classic-period 'collapse' left the culture intact and only shifted its centers. The Maya are a living people, not a vanished one.
