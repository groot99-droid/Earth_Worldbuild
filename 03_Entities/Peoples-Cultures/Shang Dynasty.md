## Summary
The Shang dynasty was the earliest Chinese dynasty with written records, ruling parts of the Yellow River region from about 1600 to 1046 BCE.

## Facts
- Divination inscribed on oracle bones is the earliest securely attested Chinese writing (about 1250 BCE).
- Elite tombs contain bronze ritual vessels and evidence of human sacrifice.
- The late capital was at Yin, near modern Anyang.
- The Shang were overthrown by the Zhou.
