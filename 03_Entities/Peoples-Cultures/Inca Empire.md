## Summary
The Inca Empire (Tawantinsuyu) was the largest state of the pre-Columbian Americas, ruling millions of people along the Andes from about 1438 until Spanish conquest in 1533.

## Facts
- Its capital was Cusco, and its road system extended roughly 30,000 to 40,000 kilometers.
- It recorded information on knotted cords called quipu.
- The state exacted labor service (mit'a) rather than money taxes.
- Civil war and smallpox weakened it before Pizarro's arrival.

## Context & Connections
See [[Machu Picchu]] and [[Andes]]. Its fall is tied to the [[Columbian Exchange]].
