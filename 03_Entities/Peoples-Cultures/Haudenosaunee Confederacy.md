## Summary
The Haudenosaunee (Iroquois) Confederacy is a political union of Indigenous nations in the northeastern woodlands of North America, formed centuries before European contact.

## Facts
- The original five nations were the Mohawk, Oneida, Onondaga, Cayuga, and Seneca; the Tuscarora joined in the 18th century.
- The Great Law of Peace is the oral constitution of the confederacy.
- Clan mothers have the power to appoint and remove chiefs.
- Estimates of the founding date range from about 1142 to the 15th century; scholars disagree.

## Context & Connections
The confederacy is an example of a durable non-state political union in [[North America]]. Its influence on the U.S. founding is debated.

## Observer's Reading
