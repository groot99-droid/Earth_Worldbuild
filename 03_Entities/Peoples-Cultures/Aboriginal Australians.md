## Summary
Aboriginal Australians are the Indigenous peoples of the Australian mainland, whose ancestors arrived at least 50,000 years ago and whose cultures are among the oldest continuous living traditions.

## Facts
- More than 250 languages were spoken before European colonization in 1788.
- Knowledge was passed on through oral traditions, songlines, and ceremony.
- Groups managed the landscape with fire and sophisticated ecological knowledge.
- Rock art in Australia includes some of the oldest known paintings.

## Context & Connections
See [[Out of Africa Dispersal]] and [[Oceania]]. Aboriginal societies show that complex culture does not require farming or cities.
