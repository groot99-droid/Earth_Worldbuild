## Summary
The Maurya Empire (322 to 185 BCE) was the first empire to unite most of the Indian subcontinent, with its capital at Pataliputra.

## Facts
- It was founded by Chandragupta Maurya.
- It reached its peak under [[Ashoka]], who ruled about 268 to 232 BCE.
- The Arthashastra, a treatise on statecraft traditionally attributed to Kautilya, is associated with the empire, though its date is debated.
- The empire fragmented after Ashoka's successors.

## Context & Connections
The Maurya Empire ruled [[South Asia]] in [[Classical Antiquity]] and helped spread Buddhism; see [[Siddhartha Gautama]].
