## Summary
Polynesians are the Austronesian-speaking peoples of the central and eastern Pacific, who developed long-range voyaging and settled the islands from Hawai'i to Aotearoa and Rapa Nui.

## Facts
- Ancestors of Polynesians, the Lapita people, spread through Near Oceania and reached Fiji, Tonga, and Samoa between about 1300 and 900 BCE.
- Voyagers used double-hulled canoes and non-instrument navigation.
- Societies ranged from chiefdoms to kingdoms, such as in Hawai'i and Tonga.
- Rapa Nui (Easter Island) is known for its large stone statues (moai).
