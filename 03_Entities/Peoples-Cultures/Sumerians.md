## Summary
The Sumerians were the people of southern Mesopotamia who built some of the world's first cities and developed cuneiform writing.

## Facts
- Their city-states included Uruk, Ur, and Lagash.
- They used cuneiform script on clay tablets for administration, literature, and law.
- They built temple towers called ziggurats.
- The Sumerian language is unrelated to any known living language family.
