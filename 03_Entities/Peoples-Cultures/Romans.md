## Summary
The Romans built a state that grew from the city of Rome into an empire encircling the Mediterranean, lasting in the west until 476 CE.

## Facts
- Rome was a republic from 509 BCE; Augustus established the empire in 27 BCE.
- The empire built roads, aqueducts, and cities, and developed law and engineering, including concrete.
- Citizenship was extended to nearly all free inhabitants in 212 CE.
- The Roman world was tied together by Latin and Greek, and by trade.
