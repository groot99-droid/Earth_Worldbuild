## Summary
The Aztec (Mexica) Empire was a Triple Alliance of the cities of Tenochtitlan, Texcoco, and Tlacopan that dominated central Mexico from 1428 until Spanish conquest in 1521.

## Facts
- It collected tribute from conquered city-states.
- Tenochtitlan was a large island capital with causeways and floating gardens (chinampas).
- Religion included large-scale ritual sacrifice, which Spanish accounts sometimes exaggerated.
- Cortes allied with local peoples who resented Aztec tribute, and smallpox devastated the city.

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes that the empire fell not to a superior army alone but to alliances, disease, and internal grievances. Systems that rely on tribute create enemies as well as revenue.
