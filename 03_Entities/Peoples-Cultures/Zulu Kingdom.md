## Summary
The Zulu Kingdom rose in southeastern Africa under Shaka from about 1816, and lasted until the British victory at Ulundi in 1879 ended its independence.

## Facts
- Shaka turned the Zulu from one of many small Nguni clans into a large military state within a decade.
- He introduced close-quarters fighting with short stabbing spears and regiment-based organization.
- On 22 January 1879 a Zulu army destroyed a British column at Isandlwana, one of the greatest African victories over a European army.
- The British won at Ulundi in July 1879, ending the independent kingdom.

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes that Zulu military organization was a social technology: regiments tied young men to the state in place of their families. Institutions can be more decisive than weapons.
