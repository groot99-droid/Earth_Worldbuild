## Summary
The Songhai Empire was a West African state that succeeded Mali as the dominant power on the Niger River from about 1464 until its defeat by a Moroccan army in 1591.

## Facts
- Sunni Ali expanded the state and took Timbuktu in 1468.
- Askia Muhammad organized a bureaucracy and promoted scholarship.
- Trans-Saharan trade in gold and salt sustained it.
- Moroccan forces with gunpowder firearms defeated it at Tondibi in 1591.

## Context & Connections
Songhai inherited the region of the [[Mali Empire]] and the scholarly city of [[Timbuktu]]. Its defeat shows the effect of [[Gunpowder]] weapons.
