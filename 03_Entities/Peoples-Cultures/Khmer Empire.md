## Summary
The Khmer Empire ruled much of mainland Southeast Asia from about 802 to 1431 CE, with its capital at Angkor.

## Facts
- It was founded by Jayavarman II.
- Angkor Wat was built in the early 12th century under Suryavarman II.
- The empire developed a large water-management network of reservoirs and canals.
- It shifted between Hindu and Buddhist state religion. The traditional end date of 1431 (the sack of Angkor) is debated; Angkor Wat was never abandoned.
