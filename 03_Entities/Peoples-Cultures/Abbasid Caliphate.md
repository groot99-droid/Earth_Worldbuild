## Summary
The Abbasid Caliphate ruled much of the Islamic world from 750 CE, with its capital at Baghdad, until the Mongols sacked the city in 1258.

## Facts
- Baghdad was founded in 762 and became one of the world's largest cities.
- The House of Wisdom and the translation movement drew on Greek, Persian, and Indian works.
- Paper manufacturing spread through the caliphate.
- Political control fragmented from the 9th century, with regional dynasties taking power.
