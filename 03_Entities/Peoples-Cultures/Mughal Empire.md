## Summary
The Mughal Empire ruled much of the Indian subcontinent from 1526, when Babur won the Battle of Panipat, until 1857, when the British deposed the last emperor.

## Facts
- Akbar (reigned 1556 to 1605) built a large administration and adopted policies of religious tolerance.
- Shah Jahan built the Taj Mahal (1632 to 1653).
- By 1700 the empire ruled about 100 million people, and India was one of the world's largest economies.
- It declined in the 18th century, as regional powers and the British East India Company rose.

## Context & Connections
The empire dominated [[South Asia]] in the [[Early Modern Period]], alongside the [[Ottoman Empire]]. It was tied to the [[Indian Ocean Trade Network]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes that a dynasty of foreign origin blended Persian, Central Asian, and Indian traditions into a distinctive court culture. Empires tend to hybridize what they conquer.
