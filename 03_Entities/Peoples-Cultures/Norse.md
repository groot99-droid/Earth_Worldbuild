## Summary
The Norse (Vikings) were seafaring peoples from Scandinavia who raided, traded, and settled across Europe and the North Atlantic, from about 793 to 1066 CE.

## Facts
- The raid on Lindisfarne monastery in 793 is often taken as the start of the Viking Age.
- Norse settlers colonized Iceland (from about 870) and Greenland (from about 985).
- They reached North America; the L'Anse aux Meadows site in Newfoundland has been dated by tree rings to 1021 CE.
- Norse traders also traveled the rivers of eastern Europe, exchanging goods for silver from the Islamic world.

## Context & Connections
The Norse connected [[Europe]] to the North Atlantic and to the Eurasian exchange-web.
