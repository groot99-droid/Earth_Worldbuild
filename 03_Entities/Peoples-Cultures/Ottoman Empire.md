## Summary
The Ottoman Empire was a state founded in Anatolia about 1299 that grew into a multiethnic empire across Southeast Europe, West Asia, and North Africa, lasting until 1922.

## Facts
- Mehmed II conquered Constantinople in 1453.
- The empire peaked under Suleiman the Magnificent (reigned 1520 to 1566).
- The millet system gave religious communities a degree of self-government.
- The sultanate was abolished in 1922, and the Republic of Turkey was proclaimed in 1923.

## Context & Connections
See [[Constantinople]]. The empire inherited earlier states such as the [[Abbasid Caliphate]] and lasted into the [[Industrial Age]].
