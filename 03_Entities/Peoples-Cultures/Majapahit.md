## Summary
Majapahit was a Hindu-Buddhist empire based in eastern Java, founded in 1293 after the Yuan dynasty's failed invasion of Java, and dominant in the Indonesian archipelago in the 14th century.

## Facts
- Raden Wijaya founded the kingdom in 1293, after helping Mongol forces defeat a rival and then turning against them.
- It reached its height under Hayam Wuruk with his minister Gajah Mada in the mid-14th century.
- The court poem Nagarakretagama, written in 1365, lists many regions as dependencies.
- How far actual control extended is debated.
- Its power declined in the 15th and 16th centuries as Islamic port-sultanates grew.

## Context & Connections
Majapahit followed [[Srivijaya]] as the main power of the archipelago and repelled the naval expedition of the [[Mongol Conquests]]. It profited from the [[Indian Ocean Trade Network]].
