## Summary
The Safavid Empire (1501 to 1736) was a Persian dynasty that made Twelver Shia Islam the state religion of Iran and ruled from Tabriz and later Isfahan.

## Facts
- Ismail I proclaimed himself shah at Tabriz in 1501 and imposed Twelver Shia Islam.
- The Ottomans defeated Safavid forces at Chaldiran in 1514.
- Abbas I (r. 1588 to 1629) moved the capital to Isfahan and rebuilt the empire's army and economy.
- Isfahan became known for its mosques, squares, and gardens.
- The Safavid state collapsed after an Afghan invasion in 1722 and ended in 1736.

## Context & Connections
The Safavids re-established a strong Persian state and rivaled the [[Ottoman Empire]] and the [[Mughal Empire]]. They ruled the heartland of the earlier [[Achaemenid Persians]].
