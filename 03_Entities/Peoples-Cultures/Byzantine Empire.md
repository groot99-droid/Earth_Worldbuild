## Summary
The Byzantine Empire is the name given to the eastern Roman Empire, which continued from the founding of Constantinople in 330 CE until the Ottoman conquest in 1453.

## Facts
- Its people called themselves Romans and spoke Greek.
- Justinian I (reigned 527 to 565) compiled Roman law and built the Hagia Sophia.
- The empire preserved and copied many Greek and Roman texts.
- The 1204 sack of Constantinople by the Fourth Crusade badly weakened it.

## Context & Connections
The empire outlived the [[Fall of the Western Roman Empire]] by almost a thousand years. Its capital was [[Constantinople]], and it ended with the [[Ottoman Empire]].
