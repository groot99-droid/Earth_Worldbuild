## Summary
The Kingdom of Kush was a Nubian state on the Nile south of Egypt whose rulers conquered Egypt and governed it as the 25th Dynasty (about 744 to 656 BCE).

## Facts
- Kushite kings such as Piye and Taharqa ruled Egypt and Nubia together.
- Later the capital moved to Meroe, a center of iron working.
- Nubia has more pyramids than Egypt, though they are smaller.
- The Meroitic script has been read phonetically but the language is not well understood.

## Context & Connections
Kush interacted with the [[Ancient Egyptians]] for over a millennium in the [[Nile Valley]]. See [[Iron Smelting]].
