## Summary
The Mali Empire was a West African state, founded about 1235 by Sundiata Keita, that controlled the trans-Saharan trade in gold and salt.

## Facts
- It was among the largest empires in Africa in the 14th century.
- Oral tradition records the epic of Sundiata, told by griots.
- The rulers were Muslim, and scholarship flourished in cities such as [[Timbuktu]].
- Its power declined from the 15th century as the Songhai Empire rose.

## Context & Connections
See [[Mansa Musa]] and [[Timbuktu]]. The empire was part of the exchange-web that linked [[Sub-Saharan Africa]] with the Islamic world.
