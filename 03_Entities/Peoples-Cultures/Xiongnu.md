## Summary
The Xiongnu were a confederation of steppe peoples united under Modu Shanyu in 209 BCE, which became the first great steppe empire and the main rival of Han China.

## Facts
- Chinese sources report that in 200 BCE Modu surrounded the Han emperor Gaozu at Baideng.
- The Han then adopted the 'heqin' policy: marrying a princess to the ruler and sending annual gifts of silk, grain, and other goods.
- The treaty was renewed many times until about 135 BCE.
- The confederation split around 51 BCE, and the northern Xiongnu were defeated by Han and allied forces around 89 to 93 CE.

## Context & Connections
The Xiongnu shaped the frontier policy of the [[Han Dynasty]], following the [[Unification of China under Qin]]. They are the first in a line of steppe powers that culminated in the [[Mongol Empire]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes how a mobile people without cities forced a large agrarian state to pay them for peace. Wealth flowed from the settled to the mobile, and that shaped both.
