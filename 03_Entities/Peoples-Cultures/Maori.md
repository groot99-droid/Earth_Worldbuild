## Summary
The Maori are the Polynesian people of Aotearoa (New Zealand), who settled the islands around 1250 to 1300 CE and developed a distinct culture and language.

## Facts
- Radiocarbon dating suggests the first settlement of New Zealand was around 1250 to 1300 CE.
- The giant flightless moa was hunted to extinction within roughly a century or two of settlement.
- Maori society was organized around kin groups (whanau, hapu, and iwi).
- The Treaty of Waitangi was signed with the British Crown in 1840; disputes over its meaning continue.
- Te reo Maori became an official language of New Zealand in 1987.
