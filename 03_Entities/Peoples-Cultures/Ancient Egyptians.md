## Summary
The ancient Egyptians built a state along the Nile that lasted, with interruptions, for about three thousand years, until the Roman annexation in 30 BCE.

## Facts
- Their history is divided into kingdoms (Old, Middle, and New) with intermediate periods.
- They built pyramids and temples and developed hieroglyphic writing.
- Religion emphasized the afterlife, and bodies of the elite were mummified.
- The last pharaoh of the Ptolemaic dynasty was Cleopatra VII.
