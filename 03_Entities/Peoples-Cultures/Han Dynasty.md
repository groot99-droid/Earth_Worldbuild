## Summary
The Han dynasty ruled China from 206 BCE to 220 CE and consolidated the imperial system begun under Qin, so that 'Han' is still the name of the majority ethnic group.

## Facts
- It expanded westward and opened contact with Central Asia; see [[Opening of the Silk Roads]].
- A census in 2 CE recorded about 58 million people.
- Confucian learning became the basis of state education.
- Paper and other inventions were developed under the Han.

## Context & Connections
The Han built on [[Unification of China under Qin]] and on the ideas of [[Confucius]]. See [[Paper]].

## Observer's Reading
