## Summary
The Göktürk Khaganate was a Turkic steppe empire, founded about 552 CE by Bumin Khagan, that stretched across Inner Asia and left the earliest known Turkic writing.

## Facts
- The First Turkic Khaganate lasted from 552 to 603; a Second Khaganate followed from about 682 to 744.
- The Orkhon inscriptions, erected between 732 and 735 in Mongolia to honor Kul Tigin and his brother Bilge Khagan, are the oldest known Turkic writing.
- They use a runic-like Old Turkic script and were discovered in 1889.
- Sogdian was a court language for writing documents.

## Context & Connections
The Göktürks followed the [[Xiongnu]] tradition of steppe empire and dealt with the [[Tang Dynasty]] and the [[Sogdians]]. Later Turkic peoples spread across Asia and Anatolia.
