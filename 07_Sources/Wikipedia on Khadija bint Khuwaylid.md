## Citation
'Khadija bint Khuwaylid,' Wikipedia, The Free Encyclopedia, accessed 2026-09-27

## What It Is Used For
Supports her marriage to Muhammad circa 595 CE after he managed her Syria caravan, her role as first believer after his first revelation circa 610, her four daughters (including Fatima) and two sons who died young, her death in November 619 as the start of the Year of Sorrow before the 622 Hijra, and Ibn Ishaq/Ibn Sa'd as her earliest eighth-ninth century biographers.

## Caveats
Wikipedia is a collaboratively edited encyclopedia rather than a primary source, and the article itself notes her age at marriage (40 vs. roughly 28) is disputed among the traditional sources it draws on.
