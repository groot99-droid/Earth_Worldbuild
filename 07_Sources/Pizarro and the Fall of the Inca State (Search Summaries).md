## What It Is Used For
Pizarro's force of about 168 men at Cajamarca in November 1532, the ransom offer and Atahualpa's execution on 29 August 1533, Spanish entry into Cusco in November 1533, and the end of the Vilcabamba state in June 1572.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-25 (results listed Britannica, Wikipedia, HISTORY, Study.com and several tourism and history sites); none of the pages was opened. The figure of 168 is described as the number 'by most careful estimates', so it is approximate. Exact days differ slightly between accounts.
