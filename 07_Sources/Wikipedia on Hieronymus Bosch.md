## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Hieronymus Bosch' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Bosch's estimated c. 1450 birth, family of painters, c. 1479-81 marriage, 1486/7 Brotherhood membership, 1516 death, the count of about 25 attributed paintings, Philip II's collecting and the Prado holdings, and the 2016 Bosch Research and Conservation Project attributions.

## Caveats
A general encyclopedia article that does not mention the 1504 Last Judgment commission, a 1488 sworn membership, or Dali and Ernst, and gives Garden of Earthly Delights as c. 1495-1505.
