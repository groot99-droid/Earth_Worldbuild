## What It Is Used For
Supports Hokusai's birth and death dates and birthplace, early training, Manga volume count, Fuji series dating and the age-70 postscript.

## Caveats
Gives the birth date as supposedly 31 October 1760 and does not mention the 90-plus house moves, Cassatt or the Sumida Hokusai Museum.
