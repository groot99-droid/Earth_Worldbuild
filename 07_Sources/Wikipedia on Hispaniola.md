## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Hispaniola' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the island's area and rank, the roughly 6,000-year-old earliest human presence, the five Taino kingdoms, Columbus's 1492 arrival and early settlements, the 1697 cession to France, the Haitian Revolution dates and the Taino population decline.

## Caveats
Tertiary encyclopedia article that is internally inconsistent on Pico Duarte's height (3,175 m in the infobox, 3,101 m in the text) and gives Santo Domingo as 1498 rather than 1496.
