## Citation
Web search results on the Apollo landings and lunar samples, listing NASA (Apollo 11 overview, Lunar Sample Laboratory), the Smithsonian National Air and Space Museum, Britannica and Space.com.

## What It Is Used For
Neil Armstrong's first step at 02:56 UTC on 21 July 1969, six crewed landings (Apollo 11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 17) between 1969 and 1972, twelve astronauts walking on the Moon, and about 382 kilograms of lunar samples returned.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-25 (results listed NASA, the Smithsonian National Air and Space Museum, Britannica and Space.com); none of the pages was opened. The 02:56 UTC time is given to the second in one summary (02:56:15); the vault gives the date only.
