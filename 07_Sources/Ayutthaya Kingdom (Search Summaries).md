## Citation
Web search results on the Ayutthaya Kingdom and the siege of 1766 to 1767, listing Wikipedia ('Ayutthaya Kingdom', 'Siege of Ayutthaya', 'Taksin'), the New World Encyclopedia, Humanities LibreTexts and Facts and Details.

## What It Is Used For
The founding in 1351, names for the kingdom, its trading role and relations with France, and the fall of the capital on 7 April 1767.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-20 (results listed Wikipedia, the New World Encyclopedia, Humanities LibreTexts and Facts and Details); none of the pages was opened. The summaries gave no population figures or casualty numbers for the sack, and none are recorded. The Thonburi capital is credited to Taksin in a listed page; that attribution was not in the summary text and is not stated in the note.
