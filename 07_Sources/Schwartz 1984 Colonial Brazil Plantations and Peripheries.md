## Citation
Schwartz, Stuart B. 'Colonial Brazil, c. 1580-1750: plantations and peripheries.' In Leslie Bethell (ed.), The Cambridge History of Latin America, vol. 2. Cambridge University Press, 1984. Read in the Portuguese translation, 'O Brasil colonial, c. 1580-1750: as grandes lavouras e as periferias', in Historia da America Latina, vol. II (Edusp).

## What It Is Used For
Table 1 counts of Brazilian sugar mills (engenhos) from contemporary observers: 60 in 1570 (Gandavo), 115 in 1583 (Cardim), 192 in 1612 and 350 in 1629.

## Caveats
Read in full on 2026-11-19 as text extracted from a PDF of the Portuguese translation hosted on a Federal University of Parana (UFPR) course page; the English original was not read. Schwartz notes that the observers' counts are variable and somewhat inconsistent, and that a synthesis of sources for 1583-1585 gives a slightly higher figure (120).
