## What It Is Used For
The start of the war on 7 July 1937, the Second United Front, and the range of estimates for the Nanjing Massacre.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-20 (results listed Britannica, Wikipedia, History Today and a University of Massachusetts Boston paper); none of the pages was opened. The Nanjing figures are contested and the quoted scholarly range comes from one source's assessment; no single number is recorded as established. The date of the fall of Nanjing (13 December 1937) is from the summary. Total war deaths were not covered.
