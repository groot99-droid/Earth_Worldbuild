Christian, David. Maps of Time: An Introduction to Big History. University of California Press, 2004 (2nd ed. 2011).

## What It Is Used For
Big-history framework that connects cosmic, geological, biological, and human timescales.

## Caveats
A popularizing synthesis; specific dates for the human periods should be checked against specialist sources.
