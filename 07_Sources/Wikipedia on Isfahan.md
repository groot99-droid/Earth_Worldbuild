## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Isfahan' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the mid-11th-century Seljuk capital, the 1387 Timur massacre with the reported 70,000 toll, and the 1598 Safavid move from Qazvin with the rebuilt Shah Square and Shah Mosque.

## Caveats
Gives no exact year for the Seljuk capital and no UNESCO inscription years, population estimates or 1722 death toll, and its Armenian resettlement figure (as many as 300,000) is much larger than the one on the New Julfa page.
