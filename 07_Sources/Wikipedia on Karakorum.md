## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Karakorum' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the 1220 founding, the 1235 to 1260 capital span, Ogedei's 1235 palace decree, Rubruck's 1254 description, the Kublai-era decline, and the Ming damage dates of 1372, 1380 and 1388.

## Caveats
Wikipedia is a tertiary source and is internally inconsistent on the Ming destruction (1372, 1380 and a passing 1388), so the destruction date should be hedged.
