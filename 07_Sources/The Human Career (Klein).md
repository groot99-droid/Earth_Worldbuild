## Citation
Klein, Richard G. The Human Career: Human Biological and Cultural Origins. 3rd ed. University of Chicago Press, 2009.

## What It Is Used For
Standard reference on human evolution and Paleolithic archaeology.

## Caveats
Published in 2009; discoveries since then (including new Denisovan and Homo naledi finds) are not covered.
