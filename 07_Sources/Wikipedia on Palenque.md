## What It Is Used For
Supports that the Temple of the Inscriptions was Pakal's funerary monument, houses the second-longest known Maya hieroglyphic text, and records roughly 180 years of the city's dynastic history; also documents the Palace's enlargement during Pakal's reign.

## Caveats
General site-history article; does not itself name the Oval Palace Tablet, so that detail was verified via a separate specialized source.
