## Citation
Web search results on Brahmagupta's Brahmasphutasiddhanta (628 CE) and Fibonacci's Liber abaci (1202), listing Britannica, MacTutor, Wikipedia and mathematics-education pages.

## What It Is Used For
Brahmagupta giving rules for arithmetic with zero in 628 CE, and Fibonacci's 1202 Liber abaci introducing Hindu-Arabic numerals to Europe.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-25 (results listed Britannica, MacTutor, Wikipedia and several teaching sites); none of the pages was opened. Brahmagupta is described as the first to give systematic rules for zero in recorded history; that is a common claim and the summaries do not address rival early uses of zero.
