## Citation
Encyclopaedia Britannica. 'Maya.'

## What It Is Used For
The Classic Period (about 250 to 900 CE), the cities of Tikal and Calakmul, hieroglyphic writing, the Long Count calendar with its zero date in 3113 BCE, positional notation and the use of zero, and more than five million speakers of some 30 Mayan languages in the early 21st century.
