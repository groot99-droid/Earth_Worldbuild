## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Tomb of Cyrus' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Alexander's visit to the pillaged tomb and, per Arrian, his order to restore it, and the structure of a six-step platform with a gabled chamber.

## Caveats
Alexander's restoration rests on the later account of Arrian as reported by the article, and the article names no masons or ethnic groups involved in building the tomb.
