## What It Is Used For
Corroborates her role as interpreter and go-between for Cortes and the Aztec emperor Moctezuma II, the 1519 Tabasco enslaved-women presentation, her bearing Cortes a son Martin, and her subsequent marriage to Juan Jaramillo.
