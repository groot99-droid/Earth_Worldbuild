## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Portuguese Gold Coast' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the sixteenth-century Benin to Elmina trade in enslaved people exchanged for gold and the Dutch takeover of Elmina on 29 August 1637 with Portuguese expulsion by 1642.

## Caveats
Tertiary encyclopedia article that says the Portuguese were expelled by 1642 and does not itself describe a formal cession that year.
