## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Kamāl ud-Dīn Behzād' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Behzad's uncertain birth year (1455 to 1460), upbringing under Mirak Naqqash, the 1486 appointment as head of the Herat ateliers, the 1522 Tabriz appointment, the 1535 death, the Chaldiran cave fable and the attribution problem.

## Caveats
Tertiary source; it gives the death as 1535 in one place and around 1536 in another, and does not mention Mir Sayyid Ali, Abd al-Samad or hidden signatures.
