Wikipedia. 'José Martí' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Martí's birth and death dates, the 1869 arrest and six-year sentence, the 1871 exile to Spain, his major works, the 1892 founding of the Cuban Revolutionary Party and Patria, and the April 1895 landing.

## Caveats
Wikipedia is a tertiary source; the page says six years in prison rather than hard labor, does not say Dos Ríos was his first engagement, and does not cover 1902 independence.
