## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Tomyris' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Tomyris as a 6th-century BC Massagetae queen known only from Herodotus, the 530 BC date of Cyrus's death in her battle, the marriage refusal, and the death of her son Spargapises.

## Caveats
A general-audience encyclopedia article that retells Herodotus, so it verifies what the tradition says rather than what historically happened, and my read was a WebFetch extract of the page rather than a full-page reading.
