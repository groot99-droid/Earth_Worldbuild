## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Newgrange' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the construction date, monument dimensions, roofbox solstice alignment and its 17-minute duration, the debated quartz facade reconstruction, and the Roman-era offerings.

## Caveats
Tertiary source whose date of around 3100 BC is a little later than the 3200 BCE often quoted, and it gives no year for the facade reconstruction beyond the 1962-1975 excavation.
