## Citation
Web search results on Gonzalo Jimenez de Quesada's 1536 expedition and the Spanish conquest of the Muisca, listing J. Michael Francis, 'Invading Colombia: Spanish Accounts of the Gonzalo Jimenez de Quesada Expedition of Conquest' (Penn State University Press), Encyclopedia.com and Wikipedia ('Spanish conquest of New Granada', 'Spanish conquest of the Muisca').

## What It Is Used For
The expedition leaving Santa Marta in April 1536 with roughly 800 to 900 Spaniards plus native carriers and enslaved Africans, the loss of nearly three-quarters of the Spaniards (179 survivors in one scholarly account), the conquest of Muisca territory from 1537 and the founding of Bogota.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-25 (results listed Penn State University Press, the University of South Florida digital commons, Encyclopedia.com and Wikipedia); none of the pages was opened. Sources differ on the size of the force (about 800 in Francis's account, more than 900 elsewhere) and on survivors (179 in one, about 166 in others), so the note gives a range and says 'nearly three-quarters'. The summaries did not confirm the Muisca ruler titles or the 1540 end date of the conquest.
