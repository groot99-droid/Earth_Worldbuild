## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Ayutthaya Kingdom' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the conventional 1351 founding, the 14-month siege and fall of 7 April 1767, the deportations to Ava, the 1511 Portuguese mission and the 1686 embassy to Louis XIV.

## Caveats
Tertiary encyclopedia article that hedges the founder's identity and the early chronology, and does not confirm the Angkor 1431 date, the VOC 1608 post or the population figures.
