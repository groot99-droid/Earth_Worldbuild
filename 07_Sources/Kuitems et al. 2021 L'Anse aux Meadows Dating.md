## Citation
Kuitems, M., et al. 'Evidence for European presence in the Americas in AD 1021.' Nature, 2021 (as reported by Smithsonian Magazine).

## What It Is Used For
Tree-ring dating of Norse activity at L'Anse aux Meadows to 1021 CE, Viking travel from about 793 to 1066, the settlement of Iceland by 874, and about 500 years of Norse colonization of Greenland.
