## Citation
Web search results on the discovery of the DNA double helix, the Human Genome Project and the telomere-to-telomere human genome, listing the Science History Institute, Nature, Science News, the National Human Genome Research Institute, Oak Ridge National Laboratory and Science ('The complete sequence of a human genome').

## What It Is Used For
Watson and Crick's 1953 double helix, made with crystallographic evidence from Rosalind Franklin shown to them via Maurice Wilkins; the Human Genome Project from October 1990 to April 2003; and the 2022 complete T2T-CHM13 sequence covering all chromosomes except Y.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-25 (results listed the Science History Institute, Nature, Science News, the National Human Genome Research Institute and Oak Ridge National Laboratory); none of the pages was opened. Franklin's evidence reached Watson and Crick without her knowledge, as reported in the summaries; the note's wording that they used her data is consistent with that but leaves out the dispute over credit.
