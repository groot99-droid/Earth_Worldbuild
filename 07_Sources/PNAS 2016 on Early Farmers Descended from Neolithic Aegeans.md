## Citation
'Early farmers from across Europe directly descended from Neolithic Aegeans.' PNAS (2016). Also Wikipedia, 'Early European Farmers.'

## What It Is Used For
Farming spread into Europe from Anatolia by migration beginning about 9,000 years ago, reaching central Europe by the mid-6th millennium BCE and northwestern Europe by the mid-4th.

## Caveats
Read in full on 2026-09-26 as raw text from a Wayback Machine snapshot dated 2025-07-27 of pnas.org; the snapshot may differ from the current page (59,535 characters). The text supports farming reaching Europe from Anatolia by migration (an unbroken chain of ancestry from central and southwestern Europe back to Greece and northwestern Anatolia); it dates Anatolian farming communities to about 6,600 to 6,500 cal BCE but does not state that farming reached central Europe in the mid-6th millennium BCE, which an earlier check had claimed.
