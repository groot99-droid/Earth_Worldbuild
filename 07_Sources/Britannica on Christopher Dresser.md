## Citation
Encyclopaedia Britannica. 'Christopher Dresser' (online biography).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Dresser's birth and death dates, his School of Design study (1847-54), his 1855 professorship of artistic botany, the 1859 Jena doctorate, and The Art of Decorative Design (1862).

## Caveats
Short entry: it says a doctorate was awarded on submission of books and a paper without calling it honorary, and it does not cover Linthorpe or the Tiffany commission.
