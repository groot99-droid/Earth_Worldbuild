## What It Is Used For
The Brooklyn Museum painting 1995.29.10, oil on canvas, dated between 1750 and 1800, is titled Pachacuti, Tenth Inca, 1 of 14 Portraits of Inca Kings, and belongs to the museum's set of fourteen portraits of Inca kings.
