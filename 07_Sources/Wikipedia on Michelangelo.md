## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Michelangelo' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Michelangelo's birth and death dates and places, his 1488 apprenticeship to Ghirlandaio, the dates of the Pietà, David and Sistine ceiling, the 1505 tomb commission, his 1546 appointment at St Peter's, and the lifetime-biography claim.

## Caveats
General-audience encyclopedia article; it is silent on the secret transfer of the body, the Sistine cleaning, and whether Vasari's Lives ends with Michelangelo, and it gives the Last Judgment as 1534 to 1541 in prose but 1536 to 1541 in work listings.
