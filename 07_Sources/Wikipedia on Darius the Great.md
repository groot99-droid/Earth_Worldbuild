## What It Is Used For
Supports Darius I's birth and death dates, his accession in 522 BCE, the Gaumata account and early revolts, the twenty satrapies, the Ionian Revolt, Marathon and the succession of Xerxes I.

## Caveats
General-purpose encyclopedia article read through a WebFetch extraction; it does not confirm the Sardis to Susa endpoints of the Royal Road or that the daric was a gold coin, and describes the daric as gold and silver currency.
