## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Enheduanna' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports her office and parentage, approximate dating (fl. c. 2300 BC), the 1927 Woolley disk, the attributed hymns, the Lugal-ane episode, and the late manuscript tradition with divided authorship opinion.

## Caveats
Tertiary encyclopedia article; it gives only an approximate floruit rather than birth and death years, and calls the disk alabaster where other sources say calcite.
