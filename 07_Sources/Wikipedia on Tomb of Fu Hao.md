## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Tomb of Fu Hao' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the 1976 intact excavation of tomb M5 at Yinxu by Zheng Zhenxiang, the object counts, the inscribed bronzes naming Mu Xin, and the sixteen human and six dog burials.

## Caveats
The article's itemized counts do not sum exactly to its stated total of 1,900+ pieces, and it differs from the Fu Hao article on the jade count (755 versus 775).
