EBSCO Research Starters. 'Monte Alban.'

## What It Is Used For
Monte Alban established around 500 BCE as the capital of the Zapotec civilization on a hilltop above the Oaxaca Valley, the danzantes stone slabs with hieroglyphs, and UNESCO's 1987 World Heritage designation.
