## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Al-Biruni' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports birth in Kath in 973, the hedged death date (9 December 1048 in the infobox, around 1050 in the lead), the July 1017 Ghaznavid conquest of Khwarazm, the Chronology (about 1000) and Kitab al-Hind (about 1030) dates, Sanskrit fluency, and the Nandana Earth-radius method.

## Caveats
Tertiary source; the article is internally inconsistent on the death date and gives the radius in miles with noted cubit-conversion uncertainty, so precise figures should be hedged.
