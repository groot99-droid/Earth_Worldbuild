## Citation
Wikipedia. 'New Julfa' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the establishment of New Julfa in 1606 as an Armenian quarter of Isfahan by the mandate of Abbas the Great, for Armenians forcibly moved from Julfa.

## Caveats
Does not give the 1604-1605 deportation dates or any death toll on the march, and its figure of over 150,000 people moved differs from the Isfahan article.
