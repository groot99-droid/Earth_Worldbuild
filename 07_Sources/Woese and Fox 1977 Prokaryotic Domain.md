## Citation
Woese, C. R., and Fox, G. E. 'Phylogenetic structure of the prokaryotic domain: the primary kingdoms.' Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences 74(11), 1977: 5088-5090.

## What It Is Used For
The 1977 ribosomal RNA evidence that methane-producing microbes form a lineage separate from both bacteria and eukaryotes, the basis of the domain Archaea.

## Caveats
Cited from memory and not consulted. The three-domain scheme was formalized later, in 1990, and the two-domain alternative is still argued.
