## Citation
National Park Service. 'Theodore Roosevelt and Conservation' (Theodore Roosevelt National Park).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the approximately 230 million acres of public land protected during his presidency, about 150 million of it national forests, and the 1905 creation of the US Forest Service.

## Caveats
The 230 million acre figure is stated as approximate and the breakdown by category is only partly quoted in the extracted text.
