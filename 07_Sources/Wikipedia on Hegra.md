## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Hegra' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Hegra's status as the second Nabataean city after Petra, the 2008 UNESCO inscription, the tomb and well counts, the legal function of tomb inscriptions, and the Roman annexation of 106 CE with the Legio III Cyrenaica garrison.

## Caveats
Tertiary encyclopedia article whose tomb and well counts (131 tombs, 86 with facades, 130 wells) differ from UNESCO's own figures, and which does not give the Hejaz Railway station year or detail curse penalties.
