## Citation
Encyclopaedia Britannica. 'Rigveda.'

## What It Is Used For
The Rigveda as the oldest of the sacred books of Hinduism, composed in an ancient form of Sanskrit about 1500 BCE in the Punjab region and preserved orally until it was written down about 300 BCE.
