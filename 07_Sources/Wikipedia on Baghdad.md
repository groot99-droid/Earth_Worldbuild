Wikipedia. 'Baghdad' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the founding of Baghdad by al-Mansur on 30 July 762 and its name City of Peace.

## Caveats
General encyclopedia article that is internally inconsistent on the British capture (says 1918 while its dedicated article says 11 March 1917) and gives a 9th-10th century population of over a million to 2 million at the high end of estimates.
