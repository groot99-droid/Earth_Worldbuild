## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Attila' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Attila's contested birth year, 434 joint rule with Bleda, sole rule from about 445, the Margus and post-443 tribute figures, the 451 and 452 campaigns, the 453 death account and the unfound capital and tomb.

## Caveats
General-reference tertiary source; the article itself hedges the birth year and the exact date of Bleda's death, and the death narrative rests on Priscus via Jordanes.
