## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Queen Seondeok of Silla' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports her January 632 accession as Silla's first queen regnant, her February 647 death, the 642 Baekje campaign, the Tang alliance decision, the Bidam rebellion and the construction of Cheomseongdae and the Hwangnyongsa pagoda.

## Caveats
Birth year is listed as unknown, the article does not detail Kim Chunchu's Tang missions, and its rebellion and death chronology is loosely worded and differs slightly from the linked Bidam article.
