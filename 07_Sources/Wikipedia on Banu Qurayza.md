## What It Is Used For
Supports the 627 date of the Qurayza episode, the reported range of 400 to 900 men executed, the expulsion of Qaynuqa and Nadir, and modern historians' doubts about the traditional account.

## Caveats
Relies on later Islamic sources such as Ibn Ishaq, and the article itself presents the accounts as disputed.
