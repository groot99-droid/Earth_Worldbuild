## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Mimar Sinan' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Sinan's birth and death dates, disputed origin, 1512 devshirme conscription, 1539 appointment as chief architect, the sultans he served, and the number of structures credited to him.

## Caveats
General encyclopedia article; the birth year and the exact appointment and death years are given with hedging, and the article text I read did not state prize or UNESCO details for the Selimiye.
