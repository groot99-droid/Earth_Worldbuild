## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Indus Valley Civilisation' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the Mature Harappan dates of about 2600 to 1900 BCE, standardized weights and 4:2:1 bricks, seals with an undeciphered script found in Mesopotamian contexts, and the proposed climate and river explanations for decline.

## Caveats
A general encyclopedia article; population and decline explanations are estimates and hypotheses that the article itself treats as debated.
