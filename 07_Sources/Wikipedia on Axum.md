## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Axum' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Axum's location in Tigray, its role as the historic Aksumite capital, the Great Stele and the 1937 removal, 2005 return and 2008 reinstallation of the Obelisk of Axum, and the 1980 UNESCO listing.

## Caveats
General-audience encyclopedia article; the stele heights and dates come from its summary text and were not checked against UNESCO or specialist archaeology.
