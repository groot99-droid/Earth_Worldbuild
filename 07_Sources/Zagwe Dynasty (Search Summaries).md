## What It Is Used For
The Zagwe span (about 1137 to 1270), Lalibela's reign and eleven rock-hewn churches, and the Solomonic claim of the dynasty that followed.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-20 (results listed Britannica, Wikipedia, EBSCO Research Starters, the Biblical Archaeology Society library and African History Extra); none of the pages was opened. Regnal dates for Lalibela are approximate and sources vary. The summaries did not describe how the churches were built.
