## Citation
Science News. 'Single exodus from Africa gave rise to today's non-Africans.' Coverage of three Nature genetic studies published in September 2016.

## What It Is Used For
Genetic studies placing the single major dispersal that gave rise to present-day non-Africans at roughly 50,000 to 72,000 years ago, and evidence of an earlier, smaller exodus about 120,000 years ago that left about 2 percent of Papuan genomes.
