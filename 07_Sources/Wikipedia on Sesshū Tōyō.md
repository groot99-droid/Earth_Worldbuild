## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Sesshū Tōyō' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports c. 1420 birth, death on August 26, 1506, about 20 years of training under Tenshō Shūbun at Shōkoku-ji, the 1467 trip to China (three years), and the dated works Haboku Sansui (1495) and Huike Offering His Arm to Bodhidharma (1496).

## Caveats
The article is thin and internally inconsistent on his age at death, and does not mention the Ōnin War, Hasegawa Tōhaku, the World Peace Council or the number of National Treasures.
