## Citation
Wikipedia. 'History of Paris' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the Parisii settlement date and disputed fort location, the Roman conquest of 52 BC, Clovis in 508, the 1328 population and the 1348 plague toll, and the Vel' d'Hiv and Liberation dates.

## Caveats
A general encyclopedia article that gives the University of Paris date as both the mid-12th and the mid-13th century, and cites nearly 7,000 Commune dead against 10,000 to 15,000 in the Paris Commune article.
