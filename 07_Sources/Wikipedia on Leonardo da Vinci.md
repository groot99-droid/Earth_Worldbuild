## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Leonardo da Vinci' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Leonardo's birth date and parentage, the 1516 move to Clos Lucé, his death on 2 May 1519, the Cesare Borgia service, the start of the Mona Lisa and the Salvator Mundi sale price.

## Caveats
General-purpose encyclopedia article read as an extract, so it is a secondary source and its dating of some works (Vitruvian Man c. 1485, Virgin of the Rocks c. 1483 to 1493) differs from figures in the note.
