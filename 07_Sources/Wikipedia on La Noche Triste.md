## Citation
Wikipedia. 'La Noche Triste' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the night of 1 July 1520 date of the Spanish flight from Tenochtitlan and the sequence of Cortes's return, Moctezuma's death and the Aztec election of Cuitlahuac.

## Caveats
Tertiary encyclopedia article; it gives 1 July 1520 for the night of the breakout and states the death accounts only briefly (Spanish versus Aztec attribution).
