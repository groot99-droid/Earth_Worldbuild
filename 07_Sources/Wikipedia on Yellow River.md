## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Yellow River' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the river's length, source and mouth, silt load and raised bed at Kaifeng, flood and course-change counts, the southern course period, and the 1887 and 1938 death-toll estimates.

## Caveats
Tertiary encyclopedia article; it does not discuss the Neolithic millet farming or Shang and Zhou claims, and it dates the southern course from 1128 rather than 1194.
