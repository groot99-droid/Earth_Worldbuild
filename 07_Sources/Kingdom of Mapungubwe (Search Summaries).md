## Citation
Web search results on Mapungubwe, listing the Metropolitan Museum of Art ('Mapungubwe, ca. 1050-1270'), Wikipedia ('Kingdom of Mapungubwe'), South African History Online, World History Encyclopedia and southafrica.info.

## What It Is Used For
Location, the K2 and Mapungubwe Hill occupation dates, Indian Ocean trade connections, imported glass beads and celadon, royal-style burials, and UNESCO listing in 2003.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-20 (results listed the Metropolitan Museum of Art, Wikipedia, South African History Online, World History Encyclopedia and southafrica.info); none of the pages was opened. Sources give slightly different spans (about 1050 to 1270 at the Metropolitan Museum; about 1220 to 1300 as a trading centre elsewhere). The summaries did not explain why the site declined.
