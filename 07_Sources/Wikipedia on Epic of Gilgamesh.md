## What It Is Used For
Supports the Epic's description as the oldest surviving narrative literature, its Sumerian, Old Babylonian and standard Akkadian versions, and the Enkidu and immortality plot.

## Caveats
General-purpose encyclopedia article; version dates are given as ranges and debated, and the Gilgamesh article gives a slightly different Middle Babylonian range (c. 1600-1155 BC).
