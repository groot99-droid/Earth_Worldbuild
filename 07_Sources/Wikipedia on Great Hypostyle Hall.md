## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Great Hypostyle Hall' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the hall's 134 columns arranged in 16 rows and its construction under Seti I with Ramesses II completing the decoration of the southern wing.

## Caveats
Its tallest-column figure (24 m) differs from the Karnak article (21 m), so column heights should be hedged.
