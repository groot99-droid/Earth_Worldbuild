## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Amanitore' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Amanitore's co-rule with her son Natakamani from Meroe in the mid-1st century AD, the kandake title, and the Amun temples at Meroe, Naqa and Amara.

## Caveats
General encyclopedia article that gives no precise reign dates and does not mention the Lion Temple at Naqa.
