## Citation
Hershkovitz, I., et al. 2018. 'The earliest modern humans outside Africa.' Science 359.

## What It Is Used For
A fossil upper jaw from Misliya Cave, Mount Carmel, Israel, dated to about 177,000 to 194,000 years ago, and the earlier oldest non-African fossils at Skhul and Qafzeh (about 90,000 to 120,000 years ago).

## Caveats
Only the abstract was read (the PubMed record, fetched on 2026-09-26). The full text was not reachable by any route that works for automated requests: routes tried without success were europepmc-fulltext, openalex-oa-pdf, publisher-direct, wayback (web.archive.org answered HTTP 403 to automated requests), and no CAPTCHA, cookie wall or paywall was bypassed.
