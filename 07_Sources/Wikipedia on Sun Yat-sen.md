## What It Is Used For
Supports Sun Yat-sen's birth and death dates and places, the 1894 Revive China Society, the 1905 Tongmenghui and its 1906 membership, his role around the 1911 Wuchang uprising and provisional presidency, and the 1904 Hawaii birth certificate.
