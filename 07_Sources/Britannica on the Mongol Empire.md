## Citation
Encyclopaedia Britannica. 'Mongol empire.'

## What It Is Used For
The founding of the Mongol empire in 1206, when Temujin was elected Genghis Khan, and its greatest extent of some 9 million square miles (about 23 million km2), making it the largest contiguous land empire in history.
