Encyclopaedia Britannica. 'Haiti: The Haitian Revolution.'

## What It Is Used For
The revolution of 1791 to 1804, independence declared on 1 January 1804, and France's 1825 recognition of Haiti in return for an indemnity of nearly 100 million francs payable annually until 1887.
