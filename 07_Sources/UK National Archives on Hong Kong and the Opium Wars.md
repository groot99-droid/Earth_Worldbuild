The National Archives (UK). 'Hong Kong and the Opium Wars' (education resource).

## What It Is Used For
The Treaty of Nanking (the cession of Hong Kong Island to Britain in perpetuity), the Convention for the Extension of Hong Kong Territory (a 99-year lease of the territory above Kowloon), and the causes of the First Opium War.
