## Citation
Encyclopaedia Britannica. 'Chauvet-Pont d'Arc.'

## What It Is Used For
Discovery in 1994 (Jean Clottes visited the cave on 29 December 1994), radiocarbon ages (a majority of samples about 35,500 years old and a smaller group 30,000 to 31,000 years old), UNESCO listing in 2014 and the replica opened in 2015.
