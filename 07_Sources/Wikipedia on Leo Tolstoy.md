## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Leo Tolstoy' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Tolstoy's birth and death dates and places, the cause of death, the dates of his major works, his 1870s crisis and 1901 excommunication, and the 1909-1910 correspondence with Gandhi.

## Caveats
A general-purpose encyclopedia article that is internally inconsistent on the Anna Karenina date (1877 in the body, 1878 in the lead) and gives only a brief account of his final days.
