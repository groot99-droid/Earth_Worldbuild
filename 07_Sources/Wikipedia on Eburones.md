## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Eburones' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the safe-passage deception, the roughly 6000 Roman dead, the deaths of both commanders, Caesar's 53 and 51 BCE campaigns to annihilate the Eburones, and Cativolcus's suicide by yew poison.

## Caveats
A tribe-level overview article that summarises Caesar's account rather than citing it in detail, and it gives only an approximate casualty figure.
