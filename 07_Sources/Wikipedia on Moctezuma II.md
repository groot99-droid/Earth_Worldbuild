## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Moctezuma II' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports his dates and ninth-ruler status, the 8 November 1519 meeting with Cortes, his captivity, the conflicting accounts of his death, and the succession by Cuitlahuac and Cuauhtemoc.

## Caveats
Tertiary encyclopedia article whose infobox and lead give different birth years (c. 1471 vs c. 1466) and which leaves the manner of his death unresolved.
