## Citation
Web search results on the Meiji Restoration, listing Wikipedia ('Meiji Restoration', 'Perry Expedition', 'Convention of Kanagawa', 'Satcho Alliance', 'Iwakura Mission'), Britannica, EBSCO Research Starters and Nippon.com.

## What It Is Used For
Perry's arrival at Uraga on 8 July 1853, the 1854 Convention of Kanagawa opening Shimoda and Hakodate, the 1866 Satsuma-Choshu alliance, the restoration decree of 3 January 1868 in the name of the fifteen-year-old emperor, and the Iwakura Mission of 1871 to 1873.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-25 (results listed Wikipedia, Britannica, EBSCO Research Starters, Nippon.com and university course texts); none of the pages was opened. Several listed pages are popular summaries. Whether the palace was seized by Satsuma forces or the decree issued by court process is described differently between sources.
