## Citation
Gaunitz, C., et al. 'Ancient genomes revisit the ancestry of domestic and Przewalski's horses.' Science 360, 2018.

## What It Is Used For
Botai horses as ancestors of Przewalski's horses, not of modern domestic horses.

## Caveats
Only the abstract was read (the PubMed record, fetched on 2026-09-26). The full text was not reachable by any route that works for automated requests: routes tried without success were europepmc-fulltext, openalex-oa-pdf, publisher-direct, wayback (web.archive.org answered HTTP 403 to automated requests), and no CAPTCHA, cookie wall or paywall was bypassed. This source therefore confirms only what the abstract states. The abstract confirms the Botai evidence for horse husbandry about 5,500 years ago, the descent of Przewalski's horses from horses herded at Botai, and the 2.7 percent of Botai-related ancestry in domestic horses from about 4,000 years ago. It does not mention mare's milk, which is checked against Outram et al. 2009 Earliest Horse Harnessing and Milking. An abstract-only source never moves a note toward reviewed.
