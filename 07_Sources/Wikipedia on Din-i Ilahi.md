## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Din-i Ilahi' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the 1582 founding of Din-i Ilahi by Akbar, its very small following (about 19 identified adherents, mostly courtiers), and the meaning of Sulh-i Kul as universal peace.

## Caveats
Encyclopedic tertiary source; it dates the repeal of the jizya to 1568, which conflicts with the 1564 date in the vault note.
