## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Real Audiencia of Santo Domingo' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports that the Audiencia was created by Ferdinand V's decree of 1511, was delayed until reestablished by Charles V in 1526, and is described as the first court of the Spanish crown in America.

## Caveats
Tertiary encyclopedia; the 1511 decree versus 1526 reestablishment framing means the date the court actually began sitting is not settled by this page alone.
