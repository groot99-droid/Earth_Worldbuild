## Citation
Encyclopaedia Britannica. 'Qing dynasty.'

## What It Is Used For
Qing rule from 1644 to 1911/12, the extension of the empire to include Outer Mongolia, Tibet, Dzungaria, Turkistan and Nepal, and the growth of the population from some 150 million to 450 million.
