## What It Is Used For
Supports Khusrau's birth in 1253 at Patiyali, his Turkic father and Indian mother, his service under more than seven Delhi Sultanate rulers, his death in October 1325 six months after Nizamuddin Auliya, and the contested sitar and tabla attributions.
