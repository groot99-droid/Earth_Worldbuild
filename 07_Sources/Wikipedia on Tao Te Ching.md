## What It Is Used For
Supports the 81-chapter and roughly 5,000-character length, the 4th-3rd century BCE composition consensus, and the Guodian (c. 300 BCE) and Mawangdui (168 BCE) manuscript dates.

## Caveats
Read via WebFetch extraction rather than raw article text, and the article gives a range of 5,162 to 5,450 characters that varies by edition.
