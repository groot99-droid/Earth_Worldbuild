## Citation
Art-Talk expansion profiles: artist profiles added to the local project folder Art-Talk-main/ (artists/*.md) after the original 53, written from Wikipedia and museum pages for the artist-section expansion.

## What It Is Used For
Biographical facts, dates and lists of key works for the artist notes in 03_Entities/People tagged `artist` that were added after the original 53. Each of those notes records its profile in the `art_talk` frontmatter field. The original 53 cite the earlier source note Art-Talk (Artist Profiles) instead.

## Caveats
A compiled secondary source, like the original. Each profile is drafted from Wikipedia and a few museum pages by a research agent, then read in full when it is converted into a vault note. It has not been checked against primary or scholarly sources, so the resulting vault notes carry no `fact_checks` until the fact-check sweep reaches them. Pictures and their credits stay in Art-Talk-main/images and Art-Talk-main/data/image-credits.json; the vault notes carry text only. Artists who died recently or are living have no freely licensed images and get text-only notes that do not cite this source.
