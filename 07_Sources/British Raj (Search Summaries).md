## Citation
Web search results on the British Raj and the Government of India Act 1858, listing Britannica, Wikipedia ('British Raj'), the UK Parliament's Living Heritage site and several Indian civil-service study sites.

## What It Is Used For
The Government of India Act of 1858, the transfer from the East India Company to the Crown, the new offices in London and Calcutta, and the end of the Raj in 1947.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-20 (results listed Britannica, Wikipedia, the UK Parliament Living Heritage site, a university course text and several Indian exam-preparation sites); none of the pages was opened. The summaries did not cover the Empress of India title, the Indian National Congress, railways, or famines, so the note does not assert them; the Bengal famine is covered in its own note. Some listed pages were exam-preparation sites of lower reliability.
