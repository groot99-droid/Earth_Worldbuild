## Citation
Web search results on the Malacca Sultanate and Parameswara, listing the New World Encyclopedia, Wikipedia ('Malacca'), Humanities LibreTexts (OpenStax), Facts and Details and Fiveable.

## What It Is Used For
The founding by Parameswara about 1400, the tradition of his conversion and the name Iskandar, the port's trade and role in spreading Islam, and the Portuguese conquest of 1511.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-20 (results listed the New World Encyclopedia, Wikipedia, Humanities LibreTexts (OpenStax), Facts and Details and Fiveable); none of the pages was opened. The conversion account depends on Malay tradition and the name of the converting ruler varies between sources; it is marked 'by tradition' in the note. Some listed pages are student-facing summaries.
