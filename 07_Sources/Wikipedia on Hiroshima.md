## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Hiroshima' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the 1589 founding by Mōri Terumoto, the 8:15 a.m. 6 August 1945 Enola Gay/Little Boy bombing, at least 70,000 direct deaths, and the 1996 World Heritage status of the A-Bomb Dome.

## Caveats
Tertiary encyclopedia article whose total death estimate (90,000-140,000) is narrower than the specialist article's 90,000-166,000, and the altitude of detonation is not given in the text I read.
