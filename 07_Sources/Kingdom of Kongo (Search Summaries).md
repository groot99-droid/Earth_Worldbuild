## Citation
Web search results on the Kingdom of Kongo, Afonso I and the Battle of Mbwila, listing Wikipedia ('Kingdom of Kongo', 'Afonso I of Kongo', 'Battle of Mbwila'), EBSCO Research Starters, Britannica and World History Commons.

## What It Is Used For
Portuguese contact in 1483, the conversion of Nzinga Nkuwu, Afonso I's reign (1506 to 1543) and his letters against the slave trade, and the Battle of Mbwila on 29 October 1665.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-20 (results listed Wikipedia, EBSCO Research Starters, Britannica, the Dictionary of African Christian Biography and World History Commons); none of the pages was opened. The summaries did not give a founding date for the kingdom, so the note covers only the documented period from 1483. The number of Afonso's letters (twenty-four) comes from one summary and should be checked against the edited letters.
