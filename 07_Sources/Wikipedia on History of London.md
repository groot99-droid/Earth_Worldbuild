## Citation
Wikipedia. 'History of London' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the AD 47 founding of Londinium, the Boudica destruction around AD 60, and the end of Roman rule in 410.

## Caveats
Tertiary encyclopedia article; it notes that some sources call Londinium the capital of Roman Britain but that no surviving evidence proves it.
