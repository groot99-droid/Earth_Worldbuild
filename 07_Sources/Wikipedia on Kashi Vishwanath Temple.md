## What It Is Used For
Supports the destruction and rebuilding history of the Kashi Vishwanath temple, Aurangzeb's 1669 demolition, the Gyanvapi Mosque built on the site, and the present temple built in 1780 by Ahilyabai Holkar.

## Caveats
General-purpose encyclopedia article on a politically contested site; it notes scholarly debate over Aurangzeb's motives and describes the Gyanvapi Mosque as disputed.
