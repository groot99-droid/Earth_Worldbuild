Wikipedia. 'Fu Hao' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Fu Hao's death about 1200 BC, her status as a consort of Wu Ding, and her roles as military general and high priestess.

## Caveats
Gives 775 jade objects in the tomb, which conflicts with the 755 in the Tomb of Fu Hao article, and offers no birth date.
