## What It Is Used For
Supports that the extractive reserve land category became operative in Brazil in 1990, the year the first such reserve was created by federal decree (99.144 of 12 March 1990).

## Caveats
The article documents the creation of this specific reserve rather than giving a full legislative history of the extractive-reserve category as such (a broader consolidating law, the SNUC, followed in 2000).
