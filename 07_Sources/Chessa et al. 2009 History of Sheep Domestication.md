## Citation
Chessa, B. et al. 'Revealing the history of sheep domestication using retrovirus integrations.' Science 324 (2009): 532-536.

## What It Is Used For
The first agricultural systems, based on cereals, legumes and domesticated livestock, developed in South-West Asia about 11,000 years before present; sheep and goats were the first livestock species to be domesticated; sheep were at first reared mainly for meat, with specialization for wool appearing later.

## Caveats
Read in full on 2026-11-19 from the PMC full-text XML (PMC3145132) through NCBI E-utilities efetch (about 26,500 characters of text). The paper gives about 11,000 years before present (about 9000 BCE) for the start of farming with livestock in South-West Asia, not a dated window for sheep domestication itself. The other work the Wikipedia article cites for its date, Zeder 2008 (PNAS, doi 10.1073/pnas.0801317105), could be read only as an abstract, which gives no sheep date, so it is not cited.
