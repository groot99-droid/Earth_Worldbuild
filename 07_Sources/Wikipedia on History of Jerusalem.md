## Citation
Wikipedia. 'History of Jerusalem' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the 516 BCE Second Temple, 70 CE destruction, Bar Kokhba and Aelia Capitolina, the 1099 massacre, the 1187 Saladin recapture and the 1517 Ottoman conquest.

## Caveats
Tertiary source; it dates the Bar Kokhba revolt 132 to 136 CE and the Muslim conquest to 638 CE, and archaeologists are divided over the biblical account of Solomon.
