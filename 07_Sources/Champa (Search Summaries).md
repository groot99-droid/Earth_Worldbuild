## What It Is Used For
The dating of Champa from 192 CE, its religious mix, the My Son temples, the capital at Vijaya, its fall in 1471, and the annexation of Panduranga in 1832.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-20 (results listed Wikipedia, Humanities LibreTexts, EBSCO Research Starters, Facts and Details and travel and photography pages); none of the pages was opened. The 40,000 killed and 30,000 captured figures come from a search summary of Wikipedia and reflect a source position, so they are flagged as unconfirmed. The 192 CE date is the conventional start; the state's earliest phase is poorly documented.
