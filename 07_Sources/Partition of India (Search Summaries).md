## Citation
Web search results on the Partition of India, listing EBSCO Research Starters, The Conversation, The Quint, Wikipedia ('Assassination of Mahatma Gandhi', 'History of India (1947-present)') and Facts and Details.

## What It Is Used For
The division of British India into India and Pakistan in 1947, Cyril Radcliffe's Boundary Commission line drawn in a matter of weeks with out-of-date maps and census data, death estimates of 1 to 2 million (up to 3 million in some sources) and about 15 million displaced, and Gandhi's assassination on 30 January 1948.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-25 (results listed EBSCO Research Starters, The Conversation, The Quint, Wikipedia and Facts and Details); none of the pages was opened. Death estimates are contested and no reliable count exists. The vault's earlier lower bound of about 200,000 was not supported by these summaries and has been removed.
