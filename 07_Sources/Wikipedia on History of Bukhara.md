## Citation
Wikipedia. 'History of Bukhara' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the walled Sogdian settlements by 500 BCE, Bukhara becoming the Samanid capital in 892, the fifteen-day Mongol siege of 1220 and the Manghit emirate from 1785 to 1920.

## Caveats
A tertiary article that differs from the main Bukhara article on when Bukhara became the Samanid capital (892 versus by 850).
