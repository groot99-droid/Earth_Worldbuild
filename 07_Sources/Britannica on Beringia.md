## What It Is Used For
Beringia as an intermittent Pleistocene land connection between northeastern Asia and northwestern North America: the most recent one began to appear about 38,000 years ago, was at its greatest extent about 20,000 years ago when sea level was lowered by as much as about 120 m, ran some 1,600 km north to south, and was largely dry and unglaciated with tundra vegetation; it is also called at least one route by which humans reached the Americas.
