## What It Is Used For
Barley reached eastern Finland by 4200 BC, appears in Harappan sites 5,700 to 3,300 years ago, spread throughout Eurasia by 2000 BC, and was grown in Korea from the Early Mumun period (about 1500 to 850 BC).
