## Citation
Wikipedia. 'List of National Treasures of Japan (paintings)' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the count of six National Treasure painting entries by Sesshū Tōyō and the dates of Haboku Sansui (1495), Huike Offering His Arm to Bodhidharma (1496) and View of Amanohashidate (1501 to 1506).

## Caveats
The six-entry count and the most-for-any-artist comparison come from counting artist cells in the list wikitext, not from a stated claim in the article, and the list covers paintings only.
