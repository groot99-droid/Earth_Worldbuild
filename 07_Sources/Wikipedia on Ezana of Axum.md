## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Ezana of Axum' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the reign span (320s to c. 360), the disc-and-crescent to cross coin shift, Frumentius as tutor and converter, and the hedged debate over the Meroe campaign.

## Caveats
Short article that gives no birth date and cites Munro-Hay for the dating; its reign end (c. 360) differs from the Ezana Stone article (356).
