## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Suleiman the Magnificent' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports his birth and death dates, the 1520 to 1566 reign, the 1521, 1522, 1526 and 1529 campaigns, the legal reforms with Ebussuud Efendi, and the origin of the Kanuni epithet.

## Caveats
General-audience encyclopedia article that is open to editing, and it dates Suleiman's entry into Baghdad to 1535 while a companion article dates the capture to December 1534.
