## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Frumentius' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Frumentius as Ezana's tutor, his consecration by Athanasius (traditionally 328, or 340 to 346 by other accounts), and his baptizing the king.

## Caveats
Describes Frumentius as a Phoenician from Tyre, while the Ezana of Axum article once calls him Syrian, and the consecration date is uncertain.
