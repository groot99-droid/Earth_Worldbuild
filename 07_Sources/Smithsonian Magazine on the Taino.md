## Citation
Smithsonian Magazine. 'Who Were the Taino, the Original Inhabitants of Columbus' Island Colonies?'

## What It Is Used For
Greater Antilles chiefdoms under caciques, English words derived from Taino (canoe, hammock, barbecue, hurricane), a possible loss of 85 percent of the Taino population by the early 1500s, and surviving mitochondrial ancestry in Puerto Rico.
