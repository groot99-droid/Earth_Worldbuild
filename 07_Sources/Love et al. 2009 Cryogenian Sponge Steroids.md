## Citation
Love, G. D. et al. 'Fossil steroids record the appearance of Demospongiae during the Cryogenian period.' Nature 457, 2009.

## What It Is Used For
The reading of 24-isopropylcholestane in rocks 635 million years old or older as a demosponge biomarker.

## Caveats
Cited from memory and not consulted. Later work (Nettersheim 2019, Bobrovskiy 2021) argues that algae or protists could make the same compounds, so the claim is disputed.
