## Citation
Engelman, R. K. 2023. Reassessment of the body length of Dunkleosteus terrelli (Placodermi: Arthrodira). Diversity 15(3): 318. Title cited from memory; check the exact wording.

## What It Is Used For
The revised length estimate of about 3.4 m for typical adults and about 4.1 m for the largest individuals, replacing older figures of 6 to 10 m.

## Caveats
Cited from memory and not consulted. The revision rests largely on one author's method and has no independent replication yet.
