Web search results on the end of the Cold War in Europe, listing HISTORY, EBSCO Research Starters, Wikipedia ('1991 Soviet coup attempt', 'Dissolution of the Soviet Union'), Britannica and the National Security Archive.

## What It Is Used For
The opening of the Berlin border crossings on 9 November 1989, Gorbachev's perestroika from 1986 and glasnost as a policy from 1985 to 1988, the failed hardliner coup of 18 to 22 August 1991, and the formal dissolution of the Soviet Union on 26 December 1991.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-25 (results listed HISTORY, EBSCO Research Starters, Wikipedia, Britannica, the National Security Archive and university course texts); none of the pages was opened. Sources differ on when glasnost began (used as a strategy from 1985, broadly introduced in 1988), so the note says 'mid-1980s'.
