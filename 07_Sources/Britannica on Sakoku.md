## Citation
Encyclopaedia Britannica. 'Sakoku.'

## What It Is Used For
The Edo (Tokugawa) period of 1603 to 1867 and its sakoku policy of self-isolation: Spain expelled in 1624, Portuguese ships forbidden in 1639, the Dutch and Chinese confined to Dejima Island at Nagasaki, Perry's arrival in 1853, and the collapse of the Tokugawa shogunate in 1867 followed by the Meiji Restoration.
