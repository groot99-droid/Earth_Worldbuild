## Citation

## What It Is Used For
Turing's universal machine idea in 1936, his codebreaking work at Bletchley Park (the Bombe in operation from March 1940), the 1950 Turing test, his 1952 conviction for gross indecency, and the posthumous royal pardon in 2013.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-25 (results listed Bletchley Park, Science/AAAS, CBC News, Biography.com and Fox News); none of the pages was opened. One summary loosely credits Turing alone with breaking Enigma; codebreaking at Bletchley Park was a team effort, and the note says only that he worked on it.
