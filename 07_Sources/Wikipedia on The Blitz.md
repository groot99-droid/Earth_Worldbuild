## Citation
Wikipedia. 'The Blitz' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the more than 40,000 civilian deaths from Luftwaffe bombing, almost half of them in London.

## Caveats
The London share is stated as almost half, not as an exact figure, and Wikipedia's History of London puts the whole-war London toll at just under 30,000.
