Wikipedia. 'Kupe' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Kupe as the traditional first discoverer of New Zealand, the octopus and landmark-naming traditions, the 925 CE orthodox date, and the contention over the Smith version.

## Caveats
Wikipedia is a tertiary source; its arrival-date section is thin and cites Waitangi Tribunal and Percy Smith material secondhand.
