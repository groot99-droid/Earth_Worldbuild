## Citation
Wikipedia. 'The Emergency (India)' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the 25 June 1975 declaration, its end on 21 March 1977, the Article 352 basis, press censorship and mass imprisonment.

## Caveats
Crowd-edited article; arrest numbers are stated as totals and its 1977 seat count differs by one from the Indira Gandhi article, so no seat figure is relied on.
