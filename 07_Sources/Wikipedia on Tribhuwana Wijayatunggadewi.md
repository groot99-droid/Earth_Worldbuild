## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Tribhuwana Wijayatunggadewi' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports her reign of 1328 to 1350, her half-sibling relationship to Jayanegara, the 1343 conquest of Bali, her 1350 abdication in favour of Hayam Wuruk, and the infobox birth (before 1309) and death (ca. 1372-1375) estimates.

## Caveats
Crowd-edited tertiary source; it dates her accession to 1329 per the Nagarakretagama while listing the reign from 1328, and its Palapa oath date (1334) differs from the Majapahit article (1336).
