## What It Is Used For
Supports Bruegel's estimated birth range, 1551 guild mastership, Italy trip, 1563 marriage and move to Brussels, death on 9 September 1569, his sons, and the count of about forty surviving paintings.

## Caveats
General encyclopedia article that hedges his birth year and place; it did not confirm the Van Mander peasant-dress anecdote or a 'largest collection' claim for Vienna (it gives twelve paintings there).
