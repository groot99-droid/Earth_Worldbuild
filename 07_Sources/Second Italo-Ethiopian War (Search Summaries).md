## Citation
Web search results on the Second Italo-Ethiopian War, listing Wikipedia ('Second Italo-Ethiopian War'), BlackPast, the New World Encyclopedia, a SIPRI paper on chemical weapons in the war and Study.com.

## What It Is Used For
The start and end of the war, the fall of Addis Ababa, Haile Selassie's exile, Italian use of mustard gas, the League of Nations' failure, and the 1941 liberation.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-20 (results listed Wikipedia, BlackPast, the New World Encyclopedia, SIPRI and Study.com); none of the pages was opened. The summaries gave no casualty figures for the war or for the gas attacks, so none are recorded.
