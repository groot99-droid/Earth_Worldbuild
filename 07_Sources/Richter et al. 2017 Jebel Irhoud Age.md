## Citation
Richter, D., Grün, R., Joannes-Boyau, R., Steele, T. E., et al. 2017. 'The age of the hominin fossils from Jebel Irhoud, Morocco, and the origins of the Middle Stone Age.' Nature 546(7657): 293-296.

## What It Is Used For
The weighted average age of 315 plus or minus 34 thousand years for the Middle Stone Age artefacts and hominin fossils at Jebel Irhoud, Morocco, with support from a recalculated uranium-series and electron spin resonance date of 286 plus or minus 32 thousand years.

## Caveats
Only the abstract was read: the Nature page (fetched directly on 2026-09-26) shows the abstract and reference list behind a subscription, and the PubMed abstract matches. No open-access copy was found. This is the companion dating paper to Hublin et al. 2017 Jebel Irhoud, which describes the fossils and quotes the age as 300 to 350 thousand years. An abstract-only source never moves a note toward reviewed.
