## Citation
Wikipedia. 'War of the Golden Stool' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Hodgson's arrival in Kumasi on 25 March 1900 and Golden Stool demand, the siege and relief of the fort on 15 July 1900, the 1901 exile of Yaa Asantewaa to the Seychelles, and the fact that the Golden Stool was never captured.

## Caveats
Tertiary, crowd-edited reference; the article's quoted speeches are reported through translation and later sources, and it does not give a precise annexation date.
