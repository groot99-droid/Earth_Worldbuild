## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Yermak Timofeyevich' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Yermak's life dates (c. 1532 to 5 or 6 August 1585), the 840-man force, the October 1582 capture of Qashliq, the tsar's pardon and gifts, the death ambush and drowning legend, and Tyumen 1586 / Tobolsk 1587.

## Caveats
The article is internally inconsistent on the death year (1585 in the infobox, 1584 in the narrative) and on the expedition's start year, and it rests on late, biased chronicles (Stroganov Chronicle, Sinodnik).
