## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Rebellion of Túpac Amaru II' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the 4 November 1780 start of the uprising, Arriaga's hanging on 10 November, the causes, the April 1781 capture, and the 18 May 1781 executions of Túpac Amaru II and Micaela Bastidas in Cusco.

## Caveats
Tertiary reference; gives 7 April 1781 for the capture whereas Encyclopedia.com gives 6 April, and it does not state his birth year.
