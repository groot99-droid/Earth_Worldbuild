## Citation
Web search results on the Rano Raraku quarry, listing Wikipedia ('Rano Raraku'), Google Arts and Culture, Imagina Easter Island and several travel pages.

## What It Is Used For
About 397 moai in the Rano Raraku quarry and about 95 percent of moai carved from its volcanic tuff.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-25 (results listed Wikipedia, Google Arts and Culture, Imagina Easter Island, travel sites and one social media post); none of the pages was opened. The sources conflict: most gave 397 moai in the quarry, and one social media post gave 887 in the quarry, which is likely a confusion with an island-wide count. The vault therefore says about 400 in the quarry and treats total counts as uncertain. The 95 percent figure appears in the same low-grade pages and should be checked against an archaeological source.
