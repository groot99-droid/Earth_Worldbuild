## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Yaa Asantewaa' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports her birth around 1840, her role as Queen Mother of Ejisu, the 1896 exile of Prempeh I, the March 1900 siege of Kumasi fort, her death in exile in the Seychelles on 17 October 1921, and the 1 January 1902 annexation.

## Caveats
Tertiary, crowd-edited reference; it quotes two different versions of her council speech and gives the birth year only as c. 1840.
