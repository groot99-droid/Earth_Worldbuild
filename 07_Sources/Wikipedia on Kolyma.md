## What It Is Used For
Supports the first prisoners arriving in February 1932, the growth in prisoner numbers and the contested death toll of 250,000 to over a million.

## Caveats
Wikipedia is a tertiary source; the death-toll and prisoner figures are estimates that vary by source, and the article says only that gold was found in the early 20th century.
