## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Christopher Dresser' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Dresser's birth and death dates and places, the Government School of Design training, the 1876-77 Japan mission and Tiffany commission, and his 1879-82 Linthorpe Art Pottery post.

## Caveats
Does not mention the Jena doctorate, botany lecturing, Coalbrookdale, James Dixon & Sons, William Morris or the Bauhaus, and the body text says Principles of Design (1873) while its bibliography says Principles of Decorative Design (1873).
