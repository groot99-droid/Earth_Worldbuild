## Citation
Encyclopaedia Britannica. 'Tunguska event.'

## What It Is Used For
The 30 June 1908 explosion (7:14 am, an airburst at 5 to 10 km altitude with no crater), an energy of as much as 15 megatons, trees felled radially for 15 to 30 km over some 2,000 square km, and Kulik's expeditions of 1927 to 1930.
