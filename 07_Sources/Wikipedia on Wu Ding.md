## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Wu Ding' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the c. 1250 to 1192 BC dating of Wu Ding's reign and the note that other chronologies differ.

## Caveats
Reign dates depend on which chronology is followed, and the c. 1250 to 1192 BC figures come from the state-sponsored Xia-Shang-Zhou Chronology Project.
