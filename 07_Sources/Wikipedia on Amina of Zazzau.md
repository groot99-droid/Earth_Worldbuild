## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Amina of Zazzau' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the 1576-1610 reign dating, the queen versus princess tradition, absence from the Chronicle of Abuja regnal lists, the Kano Chronicle synchronism with Muhammad Dauda, and the attributed conquests, trade routes and earthen walls.

## Caveats
A general encyclopedia article that is internally inconsistent on the date of Infaq al-Maysur (1812 versus about 1836) and shows an infobox birth of 1533 and death of 1610 that the text itself treats as disputed.
