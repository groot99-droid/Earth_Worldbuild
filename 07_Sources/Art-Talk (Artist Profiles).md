## Citation
Art-Talk: an illustrated timeline of the original 53 artists, local project folder Art-Talk-main/. Profiles in artists/*.md, cross-referenced from TIMELINE.md.

## What It Is Used For
Biographical facts, dates and lists of key works for the original 53 artist notes in 03_Entities/People (tagged `artist`). Each of those notes records its profile in the `art_talk` frontmatter field. Artists added later cite Art-Talk (Expansion Profiles) instead.

## Caveats
A compiled secondary source. Its profiles were written from Wikipedia, a few museum pages and a few printed works, and have not been checked here against primary or scholarly sources. The pictures and image credits stay in Art-Talk-main/images and Art-Talk-main/data/image-credits.json; the vault notes carry text only. All 53 profiles were read in full when converting them (2026-09-26 to 2026-09-27), but the resulting vault notes still carry no `fact_checks` — their facts have not been independently verified against primary sources, only transcribed accurately from this compiled one.
