## What It Is Used For
The war of 1618 to 1648, conventionally dated from the revolt of the Protestant nobles of Bohemia and Austria against Ferdinand II in 1618, and its end with the Treaty of Westphalia (negotiated from 1644 at Munster and Osnabruck, the Spanish-Dutch treaty signed on 30 January 1648 and the treaty of 24 October 1648).
