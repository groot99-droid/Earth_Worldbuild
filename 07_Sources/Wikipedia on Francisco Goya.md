## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Francisco Goya' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Goya's birth and death dates and places, his 1773 marriage to Josefa Bayeu, the 1792-93 deafness, the 1799 Caprichos, the 1863 publication of The Disasters of War, the 1814 Second and Third of May paintings, the 1819-1823 Black Paintings and the 1824 move to Bordeaux.

## Caveats
The article is internally inconsistent on the date he became First Court Painter (1799 in the lead, 1790 in the body), and it does not mention Rembrandt as a master, the Manet/Picasso lineage or the Prado's holdings in the passages read.
