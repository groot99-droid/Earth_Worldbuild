## Citation
Encyclopaedia Britannica. 'Filippo Brunelleschi.'

## What It Is Used For
Brunelleschi's rediscovery of linear perspective (about 1410 to 1415), Alberti's codification of it in 1435, the dome of Florence Cathedral (1420-36), and the patronage of the Medici bank.
