## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Pasargadae' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the 6th-century BCE founding by Cyrus near the 550 BCE victory over Astyages, its capital status until Darius moved the capital to Persepolis, its location (about 90 km northeast of Shiraz, about 40 km from Persepolis) and the 2004 UNESCO inscription.

## Caveats
General encyclopedia article that gives only a 6th-century BCE founding date and does not detail Lydian/Ionian masons or the Darius I inscription dispute.
