## Citation
Encyclopaedia Britannica. 'Timur' (biography entry).

## What It Is Used For
Birth year given as 1336 at Kesh, dominion over Transoxania by the 1360s and proclamation as sovereign of the Chagatai line of khans about 1370, and death at Otrar in February 1405 while preparing an expedition to China.

## Caveats
Read in full on 2026-09-25 as raw text from a Wayback Machine snapshot dated 2026-09-20; the live britannica.com page returns HTTP 403 to automated requests, so the snapshot may differ from the current article. The article gives the birth as 1336 at Kesh without qualification, says that about 1370 Timur proclaimed himself at Samarkand sovereign of the Chagatai line after the assassination of Amir Husayn, whom he had besieged in Balkh, and says he fell ill at Otrar and died in February 1405 (19 February in the opening line) after preparing an expedition to China. It does not name the Ming dynasty and does not discuss the argument for a birth in the late 1320s, which the vault takes from Wikipedia on Timur, so the birth year stays contested in the notes.
