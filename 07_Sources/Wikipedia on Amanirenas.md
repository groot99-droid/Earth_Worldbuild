## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Amanirenas' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports her reign dates and titles, the 25 to 22 BCE war, the Syene raid, the sack of Napata, the Samos embassy with no further tribute, and the 1910 discovery of the Meroe Head.

## Caveats
A general encyclopedia article that relies largely on Strabo and does not date the Samos peace; its birth and death dates are estimates.
