## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Wassily Kandinsky' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the birth and death dates and places, the 1896 move to Munich, Der Blaue Reiter in 1911, Bauhaus teaching 1922 to 1933, French citizenship in 1939 and the Impressions, Improvisations and Compositions scheme.

## Caveats
The article is inconsistent on the return to Germany (1920 in the lead, 1921 in the Russia section) and on the treatise date (1910 versus 1911), and it does not mention Odessa, a cello colour, Guggenheim holdings or the Murnau with Church II sale.
