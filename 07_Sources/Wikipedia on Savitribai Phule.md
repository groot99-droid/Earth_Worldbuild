## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Savitribai Phule' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports her birth and death dates, marriage at nine in 1840, being taught by Jyotirao Phule and Sagunabai Kshirsagar, the 1848 Bhidewada school, and death from plague on 10 March 1897.

## Caveats
Open-edit tertiary source that is internally inconsistent on the infanticide-prevention home date (1853 in the lead, 1863 in the body) and relies on secondary writers such as Divya Kandukuri for the harassment story.
