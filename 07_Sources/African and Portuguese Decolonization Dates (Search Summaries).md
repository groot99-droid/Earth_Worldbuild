## Citation
Web search results on African independence and Portuguese decolonization, listing CBC News ('1960: The Year of Africa'), the African Studies Centre Leiden, Britannica, EBSCO Research Starters, Wikipedia ('Evian Accords', 'Carnation Revolution') and Warscapes.

## What It Is Used For
Ghana's independence on 6 March 1957 as the first in sub-Saharan Africa, 17 African countries independent in 1960, the Evian Accords of 18 March 1962 and Algerian independence on 3 July 1962, and the Carnation Revolution of 25 April 1974 followed by independence for Portugal's African colonies by the end of 1975.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-25 (results listed CBC News, the African Studies Centre Leiden, Britannica, EBSCO Research Starters, Wikipedia and Portugal.com); none of the pages was opened. One listing gave 16 African countries in 1960 and most gave 17, so the count depends on how borderline cases are treated. The summary on the end of the Portuguese empire referred to its African territories and did not cover Macau or East Timor.
