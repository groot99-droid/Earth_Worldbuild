## What It Is Used For
The Thirteenth Amendment banned slavery in the United States and was ratified on 6 December 1865. Also that the Emancipation Proclamation of 1 January 1863 was a wartime measure freeing people held in states in rebellion and did not abolish slavery everywhere.
