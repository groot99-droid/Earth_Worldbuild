Wikipedia. 'Razia Sultan' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Razia's birth and death dates, accession on 19 November 1236, the Yaqut appointment, and the Altunia rebellion, marriage and death in 1240.

## Caveats
A tertiary source that gives two different days (13 and 15 October 1240) for her death, and the fetch tool returned a summary of the page, not the full text, so Minhaj al-Siraj's assessment of her could not be checked.
