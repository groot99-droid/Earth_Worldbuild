## What It Is Used For
Independence declared 1 January 1804, and Haiti described as the first country founded by formerly enslaved people and the first nation to permanently abolish slavery.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-20; the page was not read in full. "First to permanently abolish slavery" is a common framing but depends on how earlier and partial abolitions are counted (French emancipation decree of 1794, Saint-Domingue's 1801 constitution). Re-verify before citing in print.
