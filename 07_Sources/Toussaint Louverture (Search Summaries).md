## Citation
Web search results on Toussaint Louverture, listing Wikipedia ('Toussaint Louverture', 'Haitian Revolution', 'Haitian Declaration of Independence'), Britannica, the Gresham College lecture by Sudhir Hazareesingh and the Library of Congress.

## What It Is Used For
Birth about 1743 and freedom in the 1770s, his start as a lieutenant in the 1791 uprising, alliance with Spain then switch to France in 1794, the 1798 treaty with General Maitland, the constitution of 7 July 1801 making him governor-general for life, and Dessalines's declaration of independence on 1 January 1804.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-25 (results listed Wikipedia, Britannica, Gresham College, the Library of Congress and history sites); none of the pages was opened. Sources give his freedom as 1776 or 1777 and his birth as 20 May 1743 or 'around 1743'; the note states the ranges.
