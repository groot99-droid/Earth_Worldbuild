Encyclopaedia Britannica. 'Parsi.'

## What It Is Used For
The Parsis as descendants of Persian Zoroastrians who emigrated to India to escape persecution by Muslims, arriving in the 8th century (the migration may have been as late as the 10th), settling first at Diu and then in Gujarat.
