## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Bukhara' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the 6th century BC founding, Samanid capital status by 850, the 1220 Mongol siege, the September 1920 Red Army assault on the Ark, the 1993 UNESCO designation and the birth-death years of al-Bukhari and Ibn Sina.

## Caveats
A general tertiary encyclopedia article that gives no date for the Arab conquest and no 1868 protectorate date, and describes the Ark as destroyed in 1920.
