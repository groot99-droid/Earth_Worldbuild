Wikipedia. '1938 Yellow River flood' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the June 1938 Huayuankou breach, its authorization by Chiang Kai-shek as a defensive measure against the Japanese advance, and the death-toll and displacement estimates.

## Caveats
Tertiary encyclopedia article; the death toll is contested, with immediate drownings estimated far lower than total deaths from drowning, famine and plague.
