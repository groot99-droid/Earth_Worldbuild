## Citation
Higham, T., et al. 'The timing and spatiotemporal patterning of Neanderthal disappearance.' Nature 512 (2014).

## What It Is Used For
Radiocarbon dating of 40 sites placing the end of the Mousterian at about 41,030 to 39,260 calibrated years before present, and the overlap with modern humans at about 2,600 to 5,400 years.

## Caveats
Cited from memory and not consulted. Radiocarbon dates near the method's limit are sensitive to contamination and calibration.
