## Citation
'La Malinche,' Wikipedia, The Free Encyclopedia (accessed 2026-09-27)

## What It Is Used For
Supports the birth/death date estimates, Bernal Diaz del Castillo's account of her noble Nahua birth near the Coatzacoalcos River and enslavement, the 1519 Tabasco presentation of enslaved women to Cortes, her Nahuatl/Maya/Spanish language abilities, her interpreting role alongside Geronimo de Aguilar including at the 1519 Moctezuma II meetings in Tenochtitlan, her son Martin (b. c. 1522), and her marriage to Juan Jaramillo.

## Caveats
Wikipedia is collaboratively edited; the article itself flags that her exact birthplace and death date are disputed among historians and gives only 'before February 1529' rather than a 1527-1529 range. Read in full via WebFetch on 2026-09-27.
