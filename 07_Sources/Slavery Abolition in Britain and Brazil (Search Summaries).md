## Citation
Web search results on the Slave Trade Act 1807, the Slavery Abolition Act 1833 and Brazil's Lei Aurea of 1888, listing Wikipedia, the History of Parliament, the Library of Congress, EBSCO Research Starters and Duke University Press.

## What It Is Used For
Britain's 1807 ban on the slave trade, the 1833 abolition of slavery in its colonies with £20 million compensation paid to slaveholders and none to the enslaved, and Brazil's Lei Aurea of 13 May 1888 as the last abolition in the Americas.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-25 (results listed Wikipedia, the History of Parliament, the Library of Congress, EBSCO Research Starters and Duke University Press); none of the pages was opened. The summaries describe the £20 million as about 40 percent of government spending in 1833; that comparison was not used. Actual freedom under the 1833 act was staged through apprenticeship, which the note does not cover.
