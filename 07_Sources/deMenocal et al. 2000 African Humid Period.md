## Citation
deMenocal, P. B. et al. 'Abrupt onset and termination of the African Humid Period: rapid climate responses to gradual insolation forcing.' Quaternary Science Reviews 19 (2000).

## What It Is Used For
Marine sediment-core evidence for an abrupt AHP onset (~14,800 cal BP) and termination (~5,500 cal BP), contrasted with the archaeological record's more gradual picture.

## Caveats
Cited from memory and not consulted. Kuper and Kröpelin's 2006 archaeological chronology argues for a more diachronous termination.
