## Citation
Web search results on the Mabo decision, the First Fleet and Aboriginal languages, listing AIATSIS, Wikipedia ('Mabo v Queensland (No 2)'), the Museums of History NSW, the National Museum of Australia and EBSCO Research Starters.

## What It Is Used For
The High Court's Mabo (No 2) decision of 3 June 1992 rejecting terra nullius, the arrival of the First Fleet (last ships at Botany Bay on 20 January 1788, settlement at Sydney Cove on 26 January 1788), and more than 250 Aboriginal languages before 1788.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-25 (results listed AIATSIS, Wikipedia, the Museums of History NSW, the National Museum of Australia and EBSCO Research Starters); none of the pages was opened. The language count (more than 250, with hundreds of dialects) is a commonly cited estimate; linguists count differently.
