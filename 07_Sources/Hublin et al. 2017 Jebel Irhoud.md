## Citation
Hublin, J.-J., et al. 'New fossils from Jebel Irhoud, Morocco and the pan-African origin of Homo sapiens.' Nature, 2017.

## What It Is Used For
Description and interpretation of the Jebel Irhoud fossils: a mosaic of facial, mandibular and dental features aligned with early or recent modern humans and a more primitive braincase. It states the site's age as 300 to 350 thousand years, a figure it takes from the companion dating paper (Richter et al. 2017 Jebel Irhoud Age).
