## What It Is Used For
The age of the oldest angiosperm pollen (Hauterivian and Barremian ages of the Early Cretaceous, about 132.9 to 125 million years ago) and the 2013 report of angiosperm-like pollen of Middle Triassic age (about 247.2 to 242 million years ago) from Switzerland, which suggests an earlier origin.
