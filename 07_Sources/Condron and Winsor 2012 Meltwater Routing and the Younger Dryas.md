## Citation
Condron, A. and Winsor, P. 'Meltwater routing and the Younger Dryas.' PNAS 109 (2012).

## What It Is Used For
Younger Dryas timing (about 12,900-11,700 years ago) and the meltwater-driven AMOC weakening explanation.

## Caveats
Read in full on 2026-09-26 as raw text from a Wayback Machine snapshot dated 2025-06-07 of pnas.org (www.pnas.org answered HTTP 403 to automated requests); the snapshot may differ from the current page (39,305 characters). The paper dates the start of the Younger Dryas to 12.9 kya and calls it a 1,200-year cold episode; the 11,700-year end date used in the vault is that start minus the duration and is not stated in the text. Its own conclusion is that meltwater from the Arctic (Mackenzie Valley) rather than the St. Lawrence Valley more likely triggered the cooling, while treating a meltwater trigger as the generally held view.
