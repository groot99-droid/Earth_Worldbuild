## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Albrecht Dürer' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Dürer's birth and death dates, Nuremberg and Hungarian-born goldsmith father, the Wolgemut apprenticeship, Maximilian I's patronage from 1512, the 1520 to 1521 Netherlands journey, the treatise dates and the dated key works.

## Caveats
Read through a summarising fetch tool rather than the raw page; it dates the second Venice trip to early 1506 to spring 1507, gives sixteen Apocalypse designs, and does not mention the Aztec diary remark, the AD monogram dispute, Bellini's regard for him or Raphael's drawing exchange.
