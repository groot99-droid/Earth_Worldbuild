## Citation
Web search results on the Armenian Genocide, listing the United States Holocaust Memorial Museum's Holocaust Encyclopedia, Britannica, Wikipedia ('Deportation of Armenian intellectuals on 24 April 1915'), EBSCO Research Starters and the Armenian National Institute.

## What It Is Used For
The arrests of 24 April 1915, the extension of deportations, the causes of death, the range of death toll estimates, and the history of Turkish denial and international recognition.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-20 (results listed the Holocaust Encyclopedia, Britannica, Wikipedia, EBSCO Research Starters and the Armenian National Institute); none of the pages was opened. The recognition count comes partly from the Armenian National Institute, an advocacy organization, so the figure of 'more than twenty countries' is approximate and counts change over time. The death toll is estimated within a range and no single figure is settled.
