## Citation
Web search results on the Ghana (Wagadu) Empire, Koumbi Saleh and the Almoravids, listing World History Encyclopedia, Wikipedia ('Ghana Empire', 'Koumbi Saleh'), Britannica and EBSCO Research Starters.

## What It Is Used For
The empire's location, origins, gold-salt trade, capital, al-Bakri's description, the Almoravid sack of about 1076, and the decline that ended with absorption into Mali.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-20 (results listed World History Encyclopedia, Wikipedia, Britannica, EBSCO Research Starters and several university course texts); none of the pages was opened. The summaries hedge the Almoravid role ('perhaps'), and the 1240 destruction by Sundiata is traditional; both are treated as contested. Dates in this note are approximate.
