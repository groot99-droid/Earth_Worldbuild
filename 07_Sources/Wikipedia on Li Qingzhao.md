## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Li Qingzhao' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports her 1084 birth and c. 1155 death, the 1101 marriage to Zhao Mingcheng, the 1127 flight south, Zhao's 1129 death, the afterword to the catalogue, and the roughly one hundred surviving poems.

## Caveats
General-audience encyclopedia article read via WebFetch summaries rather than raw text; it gives no year for the remarriage and states the surviving-poem count as around a hundred.
