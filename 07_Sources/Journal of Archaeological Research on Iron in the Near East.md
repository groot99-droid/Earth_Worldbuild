'The Innovation and Adoption of Iron in the Ancient Near East.' Journal of Archaeological Research, 2019.

## What It Is Used For
Extractive iron metallurgy on a limited scale in Anatolia in the early 2nd millennium BCE, followed by the first major expansion of iron in Anatolia and the wider Near East in the late 2nd and early 1st millennium BCE.
