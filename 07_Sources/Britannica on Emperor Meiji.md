## Citation
Encyclopaedia Britannica. 'Meiji.'

## What It Is Used For
Birth on 3 November 1852 at Kyoto, accession to the throne in 1867 after his father's death, the coronation ceremony and Charter Oath of Five Principles in 1868, the 1889 Meiji Constitution, and death on 30 July 1912 aged 59.
