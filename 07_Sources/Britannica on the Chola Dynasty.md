Encyclopaedia Britannica. 'Chola dynasty.'

## What It Is Used For
Vijayalaya's initiation of the conquest of Pallava territory (reigned about 850 to 870), Rajaraja I (reigned 985 to 1014) and the Brihadishvara Temple at Tanjore (Thanjavur), and the end of the dynasty in 1279.
