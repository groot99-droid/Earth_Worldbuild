## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Anacaona' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Anacaona's birth c. 1474, her kinship to Bohechío, her accession in 1500, her areíto compositions, and the 1503 Jaragua massacre, imprisonment and hanging (c. 1504).

## Caveats
A general encyclopedia article that gives death as c. 1504 and Caonabo's capture as 1496, both of which vary in other sources, so dates should be hedged.
