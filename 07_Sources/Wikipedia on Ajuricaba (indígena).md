## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Ajuricaba (indígena)' (Portuguese edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the September 1727 death, the 1723 just war, Governor Maia da Gama organizing the force that captured him, and the account of him fighting his guards in chains and then throwing himself into the water on the way to Belém.

## Caveats
The Portuguese edition itself says the Dutch alliance comes mainly from Portuguese sources and may be a partial colonial view, and it cites Sweet 1974 and Guzmán 1997 without inline footnotes in the extract.
