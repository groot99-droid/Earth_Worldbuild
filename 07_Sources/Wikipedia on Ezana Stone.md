## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Ezana Stone' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the trilingual inscriptions (Ge'ez, Sabaic, Greek), the 330 to 356 reign span given there, and the campaign against the Kushites around 350.

## Caveats
Presents the campaign as bringing down Kush more flatly than the Ezana of Axum article, which records scholarly disagreement.
