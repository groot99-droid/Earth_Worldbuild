Encyclopaedia Britannica. 'Russian Revolution.'

## What It Is Used For
The two revolutions of 1917 (February, March New Style, and October, November), Nicholas II's abdication on 15 March 1917, and the Bolshevik seizure of power leading to the creation of the Soviet Union.
