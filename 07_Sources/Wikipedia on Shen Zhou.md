## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Shen Zhou' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports his dates (1427 to 1509, aged 82), birthplace near Suzhou, refusal of official exams to care for his widowed mother, Wu School founding, Four Masters status and Lofty Mount Lu (1467).

## Caveats
A short general-reference article that does not mention Wen Zhengming as pupil, Night Vigil, Poet on a Mountaintop, the forgery anecdote or Dong Qichang.
