## Citation
Wikipedia. 'History of New Zealand' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the archaeological dating of Polynesian settlement (nothing confidently earlier than the Kaharoa Tephra c. 1314 CE, mtDNA 1250-1300) and the relegation of the Kupe-and-fleet synthesis to myth.

## Caveats
Wikipedia is a tertiary source and this article's early-synthesis date for Kupe (750 CE) differs from the 925 CE given in the Kupe article.
