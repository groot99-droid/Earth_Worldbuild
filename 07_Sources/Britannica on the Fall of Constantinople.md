## Citation
Encyclopaedia Britannica. 'Fall of Constantinople.'

## What It Is Used For
The 55-day siege ending on 29 May 1453 under Mehmed II, the artillery barrage from 6 April, the Hungarian gunsmith Urban's cannon, and the death of Constantine XI in battle.
