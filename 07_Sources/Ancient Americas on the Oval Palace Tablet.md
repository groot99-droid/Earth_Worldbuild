## Citation
'Drawing of Palenque Oval Palace Tablet.' Ancient Americas digital archive (drawing after Linda Schele). Accessed 2026-09-27.

## What It Is Used For
Supports that the Oval Palace Tablet depicts Lady Sak K'uk' offering the headdress of rulership to her son Pakal, with both figures named in the tablet's captions.

## Caveats
The record describes a scholarly line drawing of the relief (by Linda Schele) rather than the eroded original stone monument, though the iconographic reading is standard in Maya epigraphy.
