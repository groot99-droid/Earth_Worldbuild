Wikipedia. 'Sofonisba Anguissola' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports her birth c. 1532 in Cremona, family, teachers, Spanish court service, the 1571 marriage, and the corrected 1629 dates for the van Dyck visit and her death.

## Caveats
General encyclopedia article; the court-residence length is stated inconsistently (about 1559-1568 in the text versus a 14-year figure in the infobox) and the death date follows recent scholarship that older reference works have not yet adopted.
