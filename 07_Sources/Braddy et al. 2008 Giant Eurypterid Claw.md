## Citation
Braddy, S. J., Poschmann, M., and Tetlie, O. E. 'Giant claw reveals the largest ever arthropod.' Biology Letters 4(1), 2008: 106-109.

## What It Is Used For
The pincer of Jaekelopterus rhenaniae from the Early Devonian of Germany and the resulting body-size estimate of about 2.5 m.

## Caveats
Cited from memory and not consulted. The size rests on scaling from a fragment, and a published comment argues for a smaller animal.
