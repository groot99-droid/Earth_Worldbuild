## Citation
Wikipedia. 'House of Slaves' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the House of Slaves being built by Nicolas Pépin around 1776 and the dispute over how many enslaved Africans passed through the Door of No Return.

## Caveats
Its c. 1776 construction date conflicts with the 1780-1784 date in the Wikipedia Gorée article, so the date should be hedged.
