## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Nur Jahan' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Nur Jahan's birth and death dates, parentage, marriages to Sher Afgan Khan and Jahangir, seal and coinage, family posts, the tomb of I'timad-ud-Daulah (1622 to 1628, pietra dura), and the 1626 Mahabat Khan episode.

## Caveats
Crowd-edited tertiary source; it says some historians see her as the real power behind the throne while others doubt parts of the traditional narrative, and the text I read gave no year for the Nur Jahan title.
