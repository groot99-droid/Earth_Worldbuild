## Citation
Encyclopaedia Britannica. 'Menilek II.'

## What It Is Used For
Birth on 17 August 1844 at Ankober, kingship of Shewa (1865-89) and imperial reign 1889-1913, imported firearms, expansion of the empire almost to its present borders, the victory over Italy at Adwa on 1 March 1896, and death on 12 December 1913 at Addis Ababa.
