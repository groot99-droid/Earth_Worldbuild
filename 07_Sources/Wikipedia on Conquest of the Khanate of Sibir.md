## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Conquest of the Khanate of Sibir' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the 540 plus 300 force, the disputed start year (1579, 1581, 1582), the probable October 1582 fall of Sibir, the August 1585 death and armor-drowning story, and the founding of Tyumen (1586) and Tobolsk (1587).

## Caveats
The article itself says the Siberian chronicles and English secondary sources are incomplete and contradictory, and it hedges on the start year and the location of the battle at Sibir.
