## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Philip the Handsome' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the statement that in 1504 Philip commissioned Bosch to paint a large Last Judgement triptych that is now lost.

## Caveats
The Bosch commission appears in a single sentence citing Nils Buttner (2016), and the separate Vienna Last Judgment article frames the 1504 event as an acquisition by Philip, which some scholars deny.
