## Citation
Encyclopaedia Britannica. 'Columbian exchange.'

## What It Is Used For
Crosby's 1972 book dividing the exchange into diseases, animals and plants, the fall of the population of the Americas by 50 to 95 percent by 1650, and the crops and animals exchanged (maize, potatoes, cassava, wheat, rice, horses, cattle).

## Caveats
