## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Christopher Wren' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Wren's birth and death dates and places, his father's role, the 1669 Surveyor of Works appointment, the count of City churches and the burial in the St Paul's crypt.

## Caveats
Wikipedia is a tertiary source and is internally inconsistent on a few figures (51 versus 52 churches, Monument dates 1671 to 1676 versus the Monument article's 1671 to 1677).
