## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Mogao Caves' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the 366 CE tradition of Le Zun (Yuezun), the Library Cave discovery on 25 June 1900 and early-11th-century sealing, the Stein (1907) and Pelliot (1908) acquisitions, UNESCO listing in 1987 and the Dunhuang Academy founding in 1944.

## Caveats
General encyclopedia article; it opens with 500 temples, gives the mural area as about 46,000 square metres, dates Tibetan rule as 787-848 and Western Xia's invasion as 1035, so several figures differ slightly from the note and from the Dunhuang article.
