## Citation
Web search results on the Bengal famine of 1943, listing Britannica, Wikipedia ('Bengal famine of 1943'), Oxford Academic (Sen, Poverty and Famines), PubMed Central and news coverage of a 2019 study.

## What It Is Used For
The range of death toll estimates, Sen's entitlement argument, the loss of Burmese rice imports, and the criticism of Churchill and the War Cabinet's discussions.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-20 (results listed Britannica, Wikipedia, Oxford Academic, PubMed Central and news reports of a 2019 study); none of the pages was opened. The death toll is contested and the note gives the range. Assignment of blame among London, Delhi, and Bengal officials is debated and the note records only the positions in the summaries. One listed source was a blog post and was not relied on.
