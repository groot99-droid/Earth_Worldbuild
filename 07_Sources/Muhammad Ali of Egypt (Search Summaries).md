## Citation
Web search results on Muhammad Ali Pasha and the Massacre of the Citadel, listing EBSCO Research Starters, History Today, World History Edu and several travel and biography pages.

## What It Is Used For
Muhammad Ali's origins, his appointment in 1805, the 1811 massacre of the Mamluks, his economic and military changes, and his dynasty.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-20 (results listed EBSCO Research Starters, History Today, World History Edu, a Wikipedia page on Ali Pasha and travel-site biographies); none of the pages was opened. The total of about 3,000 Mamluks killed is 'perhaps' in the summary and should be treated as approximate. Several listed pages were travel sites of lower reliability and were not relied on for specifics. His death date and the end of his rule were not in the summaries, so the note gives only a start date.
