## Citation
Barboni, M. et al. 'Early formation of the Moon 4.51 billion years ago.' Science Advances 3 (2017).

## What It Is Used For
A lunar zircon age of 4.51 Ga: uranium-lead and lutetium-hafnium data on Apollo 14 zircons require the Moon to have formed by 4.51 Ga, within the first 60 million years of the Solar System. The paper also reviews later estimates of 4.35 to 4.42 Ga, about 150 to 200 million years after the Solar System began.
