## What It Is Used For
Supports the Sasanian end and Arab conquest, al-Ma'mun's residence at Merv (813 to 818), Sultan Sanjar's reign and mausoleum, the 12th-century size estimate, the Mongol sack of 1221 with chronicle death tolls, and the Abu Muslim declaration at Merv in February 748.

## Caveats
