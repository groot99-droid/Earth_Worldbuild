## Citation
Zhu, Z., et al. 'Hominin occupation of the Chinese Loess Plateau since about 2.1 million years ago.' Nature 559 (2018).

## What It Is Used For
Stone tools at Shangchen dated from about 2.1 to 1.3 million years ago, the oldest evidence of hominins outside Africa.

## Caveats
Cited from memory and not consulted. No hominin fossils were found at the site, so the toolmakers are unidentified.
