## Citation
Web search results on the Kanem-Bornu Empire, listing university course texts on the Bornu Empire, the New World Encyclopedia and Wikipedia ('History of Chad', 'Idris Alooma', 'Umar I Idrismi').

## What It Is Used For
The move from Kanem to Bornu about 1396, the origin of the Kanuri, Idris Alooma's reign and reforms, and the 1808 conquest, 1846 dynastic end and 1893 fall.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-20 (results listed university course texts on the Bornu Empire, the New World Encyclopedia and Wikipedia); none of the pages was opened. The summaries gave no founding date for Kanem, so the note's start date of about 900 is an approximation from general knowledge and is not supported by these sources.
