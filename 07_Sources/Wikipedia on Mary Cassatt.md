## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Mary Cassatt' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports her birth and death dates and place, the 1866 move to Paris, the 1877 Degas invitation and the Impressionist exhibitions, the Modern Woman mural and its disappearance, and the Havemeyer advisory role.

## Caveats
General-purpose encyclopedia article; it does not state the count of ten colour prints, describes the Havemeyer collecting as Impressionist rather than Old Master, and gives her Academy studies as 1861 to 1865.
