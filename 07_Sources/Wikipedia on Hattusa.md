Wikipedia. 'Hattusa' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the c. 1650 BC move of the Hittite capital to Hattusa, the Tarhuntassa relocation and return under Mursili III, the c. 30,000 tablets and 1906 excavations, and the c. 1200 BC destruction and gradual abandonment.
