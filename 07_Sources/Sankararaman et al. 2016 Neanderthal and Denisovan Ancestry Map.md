## Citation
Sankararaman, S., et al. 2016. 'The Combined Landscape of Denisovan and Neanderthal Ancestry in Present-Day Humans.' Current Biology 26.

## What It Is Used For
Present-day non-Africans carry no more than about 2 percent Neanderthal ancestry; most non-Africans carry under 1 percent Denisovan ancestry, with much higher levels in Oceanians.

## Caveats

The abstract gives about 2 percent Neanderthal ancestry and up to about 5 percent Denisovan ancestry in some present-day humans, with more in Oceanians; it does not say that most non-Africans carry under 1 percent Denisovan ancestry, so that detail in the Out of Africa Dispersal note was removed. Estimates of Denisovan ancestry differ between studies and methods, so the abstract's up to about 5 percent is used only as a figure for some Oceanians, not as a general one.
