## What It Is Used For
Supports the 5 August 1496 founding by Bartholomew Columbus, the 1502 hurricane and relocation across the Ozama River under Ovando, the oldest continuously inhabited European settlement claim, the 1521 slave revolt and the 1538 university.

## Caveats
General-purpose tertiary encyclopedia; its Real Audiencia date (1512 in the extract read) differs from the dedicated Real Audiencia article (decree of 1511), so the note relies on the dedicated article for that claim.
