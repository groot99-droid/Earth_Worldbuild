## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Tupaia (navigator)' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Tupaia's birth c. 1725 on Ra'iatea, his flight to Tahiti after the Bora Bora invasion, joining the Endeavour in July 1769, the August 1769 chart, and his death on 20 December 1770 at Batavia.

## Caveats
A general-audience encyclopedia article that is internally muddled on where the Endeavour was when he joined and on the 74-versus-130 island wording, and it does not cover Taiata.
