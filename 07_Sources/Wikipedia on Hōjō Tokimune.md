## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Hōjō Tokimune' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports his birth and death dates, his accession as eighth shikken on April 18, 1268, his rejection of Mongol demands, and the 1275 and 1279 envoy executions.

## Caveats
Read through a WebFetch text extraction rather than the raw page, and the article gives his accession age as 18, which is an East Asian style count that would be 16 by Western reckoning from a June 1251 birth.
