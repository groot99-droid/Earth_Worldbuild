## Citation
Wikipedia. '735–737 Japanese smallpox epidemic' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the estimated 25 to 35 percent (about one third) mortality figure and the article's link between the epidemic and Shōmu's Tōdai-ji and Daibutsu project.

## Caveats
Mortality figures are demographic estimates that the article itself presents with hedging language, and the article's causal link to the Buddha project is an interpretation, not a settled fact.
