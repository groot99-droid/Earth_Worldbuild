## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Deir el-Medina' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the workmen's strike over delayed rations, hedged as possibly the first sit-down strike in recorded history, and the surviving ostraca and ration records from the village.

## Caveats
Dates the strike to about the 25th year of Ramesses III (c. 1170 BC), which differs from the c. 1157 BCE date in the note and in other common accounts, so the exact year is unsettled here.
