## What It Is Used For
Last Glacial Maximum timing and sea-level lowering.

## Caveats
Read in full on 2026-09-26 as raw text from a Wayback Machine snapshot dated 2025-08-08 of pnas.org; the snapshot may differ from the current page (66,158 characters). The paper places the fall of equivalent sea level to about 134 m below present at 29 to 21 ka BP and the glacial maximum at about 30,000 years ago; the wider Last Glacial Maximum window of about 26.5 to 19 thousand years ago and the range of 120 to 135 m in the Last Glacial Maximum note are not stated in it. The 26,500 to 19,000 year window in the note comes from Clark et al. (2009), which was seen only in search summaries and has not been read.
