## Citation
'Late Colonization of Easter Island.' Science, 2006.

## What It Is Used For
Radiocarbon dates from the earliest layers at Anakena, Rapa Nui, implying colonization about 1200 CE, followed by rapid population growth, environmental change, and monument building within a century or two.
