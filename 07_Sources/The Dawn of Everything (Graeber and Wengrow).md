## Citation
Graeber, David, and David Wengrow. The Dawn of Everything: A New History of Humanity. Farrar, Straus and Giroux, 2021.

## What It Is Used For
Challenges linear models of social evolution and stresses the variety of early social experiments.

## Caveats
Arguments are influential but disputed by many specialists; treat as one perspective, not as consensus.
