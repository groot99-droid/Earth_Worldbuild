Wikipedia. 'Caravaggio' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Caravaggio's birth, apprenticeship, move to Rome, the Contarelli Chapel commission, the 1606 killing of Ranuccio Tomassoni and his death at Porto Ercole in 1610.

## Caveats
General encyclopedia article; the cause of death and the reason for the 1606 fight are debated, and the article does not mention the 1951 Milan exhibition.
