## Citation
Raikar, Sanat Pai. 'Savitribai Phule.' Encyclopaedia Britannica.

## What It Is Used For
Supports her birth and death dates and places, marriage at nine, being taught by Jyotirao Phule, the 1848 Bhidewada school, the stones-mud-dung harassment, and death from plague after running a plague clinic.

## Caveats
General-audience encyclopedia entry that hedges her as one of the first female teachers in India and gives dates for her shelters (1854, 1864) that differ from Wikipedia.
