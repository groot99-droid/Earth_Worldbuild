Wikipedia. 'William of Rubruck' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports that Rubruck set out on a missionary journey on Louis IX's orders in 1253 and visited Karakorum in 1253-1254.

## Caveats
Wikipedia is a tertiary source, and its Rubruck article and its Karakorum article differ slightly on the arrival date at the Khan's court versus the city.
