## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Gorée' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the 1444 Portuguese settlement, the 1677 French capture, the uncertain Dutch takeover and 1628 first documented Dutch presence, the signares, and the historians' debate over slave numbers.

## Caveats
A general encyclopedia article that gives the House of Slaves date as 1780-1784, which conflicts with the c. 1776 date in the separate House of Slaves article, and it does not mention the 1817 return to France or visits by Mandela and John Paul II.
