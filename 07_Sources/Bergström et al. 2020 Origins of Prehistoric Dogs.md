## Citation
Bergström, A. et al. 'Origins and genetic legacy of prehistoric dogs.' Science 370 (2020).

## What It Is Used For
Genome sequencing of 27 ancient dogs showing a single origin distinct from any living wolf population, and the roughly 40,000-to-27,000-year divergence estimate.

## Caveats
Cited from memory and not consulted. Other ancient-wolf-genome studies have since reopened the single-versus-dual-origin question.
