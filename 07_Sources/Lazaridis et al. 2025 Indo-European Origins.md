## Citation
Lazaridis, I., et al. Two papers on the genetic origins of the Indo-European languages, Nature, 5 February 2025, as reported by the Harvard Gazette.

## What It Is Used For
A Caucasus-Lower Volga population as the source of the ancestral 'proto-Indo-Anatolian' language community (about 4400 to 4000 BCE), with the Yamnaya descending from it and spreading the language later; the Anatolian branch (Hittite and relatives) not deriving from the steppe.

## Caveats
The Harvard Gazette page was fetched 2026-09-20 with WebFetch, which returns a model-generated summary, and details were supplemented from web search result summaries (Reich lab, Wikipedia and other listings). The Nature papers themselves were not read.
