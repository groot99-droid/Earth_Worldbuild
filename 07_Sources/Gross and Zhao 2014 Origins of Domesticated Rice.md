## Citation
Gross, B. L. and Zhao, Z. 'Archaeological and genetic insights into the origins of domesticated rice.' PNAS 111 (2014).

## What It Is Used For
Domestication of japonica rice in the Yangtze basin about 7,000 years ago, indica arising later through hybridization, and wild ancestor Oryza rufipogon.

## Caveats
Read in full on 2026-09-26 as raw text from a Wayback Machine snapshot dated 2025-06-10 of pnas.org; the snapshot may differ from the current page (67,991 characters). The text supports japonica rice cultivated in the Yangtze valley by about 8,000 BP from wild Oryza rufipogon, with the non-shattering trait fixed 1,000 years or more later, and indica arising through introgression from japonica; it does not give '7,000 years ago' as a domestication date, which an earlier check had claimed.
