## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Siege of Isfahan' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the March to October 1722 Hotaki siege, the famine and capitulation of Shah Sultan Husayn on 23 October, and the fall as the beginning of the end of the Safavid dynasty.

## Caveats
The article text gives no civilian death toll (a figure seen only in a summarised infobox is not cited) and is internally loose on the siege length (six months versus after 8 months).
