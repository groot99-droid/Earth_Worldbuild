## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Frida Kahlo' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Kahlo's birth date and claimed 1910 birth year, the 1925 bus accident, her marriages to Rivera, the range of painting-output estimates, the 1938 Julien Levy show, the Louvre purchase of The Frame, and her death and later rediscovery.

## Caveats
General-purpose encyclopedia article that can change over time, and it gives only the years, not the months, for the 1939 divorce.
