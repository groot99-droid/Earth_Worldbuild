## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Ajuricaba (chief)' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports his identity as a Manaos leader, the unclear origins, the c. 1728 death, the Dutch alliance, the 1723 campaign under Governor João da Maia da Gama, the possible betrayal by allies, and the leap into the river.

## Caveats
A short general-audience article that relies on Portuguese colonial chroniclers and a thin bibliography, and it gives c. 1728 for his death where the Portuguese edition gives September 1727.
