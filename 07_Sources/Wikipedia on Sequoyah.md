## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Sequoyah' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Sequoyah's approximate birth year and place, lack of English schooling, the 1809 to 1821 syllabary work, the 86-then-85 symbol count, and his death in Mexico in August 1843.

## Caveats
The article is internally inconsistent on birth year (c. 1770 in the lead and infobox, around 1778 in the Early life text) and gives a death window of 1843 to 1845 alongside the August 1843 letter.
