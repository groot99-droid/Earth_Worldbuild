## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Nabataeans' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the first mention of the Nabataeans in 312/311 BC, when they were attacked at Sela or perhaps at Petra, and the Roman conversion of the kingdom into the province of Arabia Petraea.

## Caveats
Tertiary encyclopedia text that is internally inconsistent on the annexation year, giving both 105 and 106 AD.
