## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Ambiorix' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Ambiorix and Cativolcus as joint leaders of the Eburones, the winter 54 BCE uprising and ambush of Sabinus and Cotta's troops, his escape across the Rhine per Florus, and Caesar's De Bello Gallico as principal source.

## Caveats
The article is short and does not give the commanders' individual deaths, the legion composition or an exact final date for Ambiorix; it also has no birth date or evidence for any activity before 54 BCE.
