## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Leonidas I' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Leonidas I's reign dates of 489-480 BC, his marriage to Cleomenes's daughter Gorgo before his accession, and his death at Thermopylae in 480 BC.

## Caveats
General-purpose encyclopedia article that relies on Herodotus for the marriage and battle details; the exact day of death is given as 11 August 480 BC but is not needed for the note.
