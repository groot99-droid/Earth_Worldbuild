## Citation
Anderson, P. S. L., and Westneat, M. W. 'Feeding mechanics and bite force modelling of the skull of Dunkleosteus terrelli, an ancient apex predator.' Biology Letters 3(1), 2007: 77-80.

## What It Is Used For
The linkage model of the skull and the estimated bite force of about 4,400 N at the tip and 5,400 N at the blade edge.

## Caveats
Cited from memory and not consulted. The estimate assumes a larger animal than recent size revisions support, and a 2025 study argues the skull held more cartilage than the model assumed.
