## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Truganini' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Truganini's c. 1812 birth, 8 May 1876 death in Hobart, the 1878 disinterment, 1904 skeleton display, 1976 cremation and scattering, and the rejection of the 'last Tasmanian Aboriginal' narrative.

## Caveats
Tertiary, crowd-edited source whose birthplace (Recherche Bay) differs from the Bruny Island birthplace given in some other accounts, and the exact birth year is approximate.
