## What It Is Used For
Supports the 1468 Timbuktu annexation, the Djenne siege, the Sonni Ali to Sonni Baru to Askia Muhammad succession, and the 1493 change of dynasty.

## Caveats
Tertiary encyclopedia article that is internally inconsistent on Djenne (seven-year siege ending 1473 in the body, 1475 in the lead) and cites Britannica only via a generic reference-suite entry.
