## Citation
Web search results on the Guayaquil Conference (1822), the Battle of Ayacucho (1824), Gran Colombia (1819 to 1831) and the Napoleonic invasion of Spain (1808), listing Britannica, Wikipedia, EBSCO Research Starters and university course texts.

## What It Is Used For
Bolivar and San Martin meeting at Guayaquil on 26 and 27 July 1822; Sucre's victory at Ayacucho on 9 December 1824; Gran Colombia proclaimed on 17 December 1819 and dissolved in 1830 and 1831; and the Napoleonic invasion of 1808 as a catalyst but not the only cause of the independence wars.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-25 (results listed Britannica, Wikipedia, EBSCO Research Starters, Encyclopedia.com and university course texts); none of the pages was opened. Sources differ on whether Gran Colombia counts from 1819 or from the 1821 Congress of Cucuta, and on whether it dissolved in 1830 or 1831; the note gives both.
