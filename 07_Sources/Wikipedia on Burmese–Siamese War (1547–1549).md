## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Burmese–Siamese War (1547–1549)' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the October 1548 start of the main Burmese invasion, the failed month-long siege and truce, and the point that Suriyothai appears only in Siamese and Portuguese accounts and not in Burmese history.

## Caveats
Tertiary source; it dates Maha Chakkraphat's accession to 1546, which conflicts with the Suriyothai article's placement of the invasion barely six months into his reign in 1548.
