## Citation
Web search results on the dating of the Thera (Santorini) eruption, including 'Annual radiocarbon record indicates 16th century BCE date for the Thera eruption' (Science Advances, 2018), 'On the enigma of dating the Minoan eruption of Santorini' (PNAS, 2020) and 'Olive shrub buried on Therasia supports a mid-16th century BCE date for the Thera eruption' (Scientific Reports, 2023).

## What It Is Used For
Radiocarbon dates on material buried by the eruption clustering around 1650 to 1600 BCE, against archaeological arguments from Egyptian synchronisms for a date around 1500 BCE.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-20; none of the papers was opened. The summary states the discrepancy is about a century and remains unresolved.
