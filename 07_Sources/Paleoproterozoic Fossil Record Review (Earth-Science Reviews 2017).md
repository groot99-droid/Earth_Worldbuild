## Citation
'The Paleoproterozoic fossil record: Implications for the evolution of the biosphere during Earth's middle-age.' Earth-Science Reviews 176: 68-86 (published online 2017, volume dated 2018).

## What It Is Used For
Oldest unambiguous eukaryote microfossils (about 1.65 Ga) and the disputed status of older candidates.

## Caveats
Read in full on 2026-09-26 from the open-access PDF at www.vliz.be (publishedVersion version, 146,220 characters), so wording, figures or pagination may differ slightly from the published version. The review calls large organic-walled vesicles (Valeria lophostriata, from the more than 1.65 Ga Mallapunyah Formation, Australia) the oldest unambiguous eukaryotic microfossils, and says younger coiled filaments called Grypania (about 1.45 Ga, India) are more convincingly eukaryotic than Grypania spiralis from the 1.87 Ga Negaunee Iron Formation.
