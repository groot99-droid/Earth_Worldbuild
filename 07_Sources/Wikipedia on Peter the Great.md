## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Peter the Great' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Peter's birth and death dates, the 1682 accession with Ivan V and sole rule from 1696, the 1697 to 1698 Grand Embassy, the Poltava victory, the 1703 founding of Saint Petersburg and the 1712 capital move.

## Caveats
Read through a WebFetch extraction of the article rather than the full page text, and it does not describe Peter personally cutting boyars' beards (it gives an order to cut beards and a beard tax).
