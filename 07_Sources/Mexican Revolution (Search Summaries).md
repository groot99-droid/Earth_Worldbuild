## Citation
Web search results on the Mexican Revolution, listing Wikipedia ('Mexican Revolution', 'Emiliano Zapata'), Britannica, Encyclopedia.com, the OER Project and Study.com.

## What It Is Used For
The dating of the revolution, the sequence of leaders and assassinations, the 1917 Constitution, and the commonly cited death toll of more than one million.

## Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-20 (results listed Wikipedia, Britannica, Encyclopedia.com, the OER Project and Study.com); none of the pages was opened. The death toll is a rough round figure from a summary and not a demographic study; it is treated as uncertain. The statement that the toll mixes disease and emigration effects is a general caution added in the note and was not in the summary.
