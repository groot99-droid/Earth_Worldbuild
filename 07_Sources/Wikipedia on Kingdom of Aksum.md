## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Kingdom of Aksum' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports the Periplus mention of Axum and Adulis, the start of Aksumite coinage under Endybis, the gold, silver and bronze coin series, and the differing dates for Ezana's conversion.

## Caveats
Gives more than one date for Ezana's conversion (about 325 and about 340) and lists causes of the 7th-century decline without labelling them as debated.
