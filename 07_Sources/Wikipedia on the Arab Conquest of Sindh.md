## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Arab conquest of Sindh' and 'Umayyad state of Cordoba.'

## What It Is Used For
The Umayyad conquest of Sindh, 711-713, in which the Umayyad Caliphate defeated the Chach dynasty (the last Hindu dynasty to rule Sindh) and incorporated Sindh, part of southern Punjab and Makran as a caliphal province.
