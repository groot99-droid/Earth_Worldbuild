## What It Is Used For
Supports the late-1831 date of the print, the Prussian blue pigment, the division of labour in ukiyo-e production, Debussy's La Mer cover and the 2024 banknote redesign.

## Caveats
Does not give the opening year of the Sumida Hokusai Museum, and the banknote item is an announcement of a redesign rather than a description of the note itself.
