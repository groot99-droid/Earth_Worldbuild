## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Dunhuang' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports Zhang Yichao's expulsion of the Tibetans in 848, the Guiyi period lasting to about 1036, and the Tangut (Western Xia) capture of Dunhuang in 1036, plus the Han commandery being established shortly after 104 BC.

## Caveats
General encyclopedia article on the city rather than the cave complex; its 1036 date for the Tangut capture differs by a year from the 1035 given in the Mogao Caves article.
