## Citation
USDA Economic Research Service. 'Genetically engineered varieties of corn, upland cotton, and soybeans, by State and for the United States, 2000-25' (XLSX data file), in the data product Adoption of Genetically Engineered Crops in the United States.

## What It Is Used For
The Soybeans sheet gives herbicide-tolerant (HT) soybeans as a percent of all soybeans planted in the United States each year from 2000 to 2025: 93 percent in 2010, and 96 percent in 2025. HT is the only GE soybean type with data, so the HT and all-GE rows are the same.

## Caveats
Read on 2026-11-19 by downloading the XLSX file linked from the data product page and reading its Soybeans sheet in full (the CSV of the same name holds only the 2025 rows). The figures are ERS estimates from survey data, not a census; the file starts in 2000, so the 1997 to 1999 figures are not in it.
