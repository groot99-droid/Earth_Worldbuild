## Citation
Wikipedia. 'Túpac Amaru II' (English edition).

## What It Is Used For
Supports his kuraka and muleteer background, his adoption of the name of Túpac Amaru I to claim Inca descent, and the birth year given as c. 1742.

## Caveats
Tertiary reference; its infobox birth year (c. 1742) differs from the 1738 used in older reference works, and its Arriaga execution date (4 November) differs from the rebellion article (10 November).
