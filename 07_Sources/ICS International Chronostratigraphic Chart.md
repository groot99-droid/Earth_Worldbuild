## Citation
International Commission on Stratigraphy. International Chronostratigraphic Chart (regularly updated).

## What It Is Used For
Authoritative boundaries and names for geological eons, eras, periods, and epochs.

## Caveats
Boundary ages were read directly from the text layer of the ICS chart PDF (ChronostratChart2023-06) on %s: Cambrian base 538.8 +/- 0.2 Ma, Triassic base 251.902 +/- 0.024 Ma, Jurassic base 201.4 +/- 0.2 Ma, Paleogene base 66.0 Ma, Pleistocene base 2.58 Ma, Holocene base 0.0117 Ma, Devonian/Carboniferous 358.9 Ma, Frasnian/Famennian 372.2 Ma, Archean/Proterozoic 2500 Ma, base of the Eon-level charts 4000 and 4567 Ma. Other notes: Dates are revised periodically; check the current version. Informal terms such as Hadean and Anthropocene are not official units.
