## What It Is Used For
Supports Harald's birth and death dates, exile after Stiklestad, Varangian Guard service, return to Norway, the Danish war and its peace, and the 1066 invasion of England.

## Caveats
General-audience encyclopedia article; the Norse saga sources it rests on are late and partly legendary, and details such as the 1064 versus 1065 peace date are flagged as uncertain in the article itself.
