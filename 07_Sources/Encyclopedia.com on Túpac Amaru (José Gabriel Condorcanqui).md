## Citation
Encyclopedia.com. 'Túpac Amaru (José Gabriel Condorcanqui) (1738-1781)'.

## What It Is Used For
Supports his descent from the Inca Túpac Amaru executed by Toledo in 1572, the 10 November 1780 date of Arriaga's execution, the 18 May 1781 execution in Cusco, and the March 1738 birth date.

## Caveats
Short reference-work entry that gives a single birth date (March 1738) without discussing the 1742 alternative and dates the capture to 6 April 1781.
