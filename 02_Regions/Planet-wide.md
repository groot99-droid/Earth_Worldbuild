## Summary
Planet-wide is the region tag for events and processes that affect the whole Earth, or that have no single location, such as mass extinctions, climate shifts, and global exchange networks.

## Facts
- Used as the `region` value for deep-time events and for global human phenomena.
- Earth's surface is about 510 million square kilometers, of which about 71 percent is ocean.

## Context & Connections
Use this tag for planetary events such as the [[Great Oxidation Event]], for global history such as the [[Columbian Exchange]] and the [[Great Acceleration]], and for anything without a specific place.

## Observer's Reading
The Observer treats the planet as the primary unit of analysis, and human regions as subdivisions of a single connected system. Regional labels are useful bookkeeping, not natural divisions.
