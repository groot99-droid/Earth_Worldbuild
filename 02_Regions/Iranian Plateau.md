## Summary
The Iranian Plateau is the highland region between Mesopotamia and South Asia. It was the heartland of Persian empires and a link on overland routes across Eurasia.

## Facts
- The [[Achaemenid Persians]] built a vast empire from here in the 6th century BCE.
- The plateau sits on the routes of the [[Opening of the Silk Roads]].
- It was later incorporated into the [[Abbasid Caliphate]] and the [[Mongol Empire]].

## Context & Connections
It links [[Mesopotamia]], [[South Asia]] and the [[Central Asian Steppe]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer sees the plateau as a hinge region: its historical role was less to originate than to relay, translate, and administer.
