## Summary
Southeast Asia is the mainland and island region between India and China. It was a hub of maritime trade and hosted powerful states influenced by Indian and Chinese culture.

## Facts
- The [[Khmer Empire]] built [[Angkor]] between the 9th and 15th centuries CE.
- Austronesian-speaking peoples spread from here into the Pacific; see [[Polynesian Settlement of the Pacific]].
- Spice trade from the Malay Archipelago drew traders from across Eurasia.

## Context & Connections
The region links [[South Asia]], [[East Asia]] and [[Oceania]] by sea.

## Observer's Reading
The Observer treats Southeast Asia as the great intersection zone of the maritime exchange-web, and the launching point for the largest human ocean migrations.
