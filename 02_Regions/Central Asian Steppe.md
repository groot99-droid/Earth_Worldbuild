## Summary
The Central Asian Steppe is the vast grassland belt across northern Eurasia. Mounted pastoralists there shaped the history of every neighboring civilization.

## Facts
- Horse domestication and mounted warfare developed on the Western steppe.
- The [[Mongol Empire]] came from the eastern steppe in the 13th century.
- The steppe was a corridor for the [[Opening of the Silk Roads]] and for the [[Indo-European Language Spread]].

## Context & Connections
The steppe borders [[East Asia]], [[Europe]] and the [[Iranian Plateau]]; see [[Genghis Khan]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes how a mobile way of life without cities repeatedly rearranged the settled world. Absence of monuments is not absence of influence.
