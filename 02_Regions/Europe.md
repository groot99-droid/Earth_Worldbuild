## Summary
Europe is the western peninsula of Eurasia. It hosted early farming societies, the Roman Empire's northern provinces, and later the technologies and empires of the early modern and industrial periods.

## Facts
- Farming spread into Europe from Anatolia over several thousand years.
- The [[Norse]] traveled from Scandinavia across the Atlantic and through eastern rivers.
- The [[Scientific Revolution]] and [[Industrial Revolution]] began here, drawing on knowledge from many regions.
- The continent was the main theater of [[World War I]] and [[World War II]].

## Context & Connections
Europe links to the [[Mediterranean Basin]] and to the [[Central Asian Steppe]]. The [[Movable-Type Printing Press]] appeared here in the 15th century.

## Observer's Reading
The Observer cautions against a common habit: a small peninsula's history is often told as if it were the world's. Its outsized effect in the modern era is real, but recent.
