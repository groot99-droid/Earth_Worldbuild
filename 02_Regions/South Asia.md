## Summary
South Asia is the Indian subcontinent. It is home to one of the earliest urban civilizations and to major religious and mathematical traditions.

## Facts
- The [[Indus Valley Civilization]] flourished about 2600 to 1900 BCE; see [[Mohenjo-daro]].
- Hindu, Buddhist, Jain and Sikh traditions originated here; see [[Siddhartha Gautama]] and [[Ashoka]].
- The decimal place-value system developed in India and spread outward.
- Monsoon winds shaped Indian Ocean trade.

## Context & Connections
South Asia connects to [[Southeast Asia]] by sea and to the [[Iranian Plateau]] by land.
