## Summary
The Levant and Anatolia are the eastern Mediterranean coast and the Turkish peninsula. They are a crossroads between Africa, Asia, and Europe and a center of early farming.

## Facts
- Early farming and monumental building appear here, including [[Göbekli Tepe]] and [[Çatalhöyük]].
- The [[Phoenicians]] developed a widely copied alphabet; see [[Alphabet]].
- Later, [[Constantinople]] stood at the junction of Europe and Asia.
- The region saw the collapse of many Bronze Age states; see [[Bronze Age Collapse]].

## Context & Connections
Together with [[Mesopotamia]], this is the core of the Fertile Crescent and of [[The Agricultural Revolution]].
