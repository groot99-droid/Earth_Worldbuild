## Summary
North America north of Mexico was settled by peoples who arrived at least 15,000 years ago and who developed diverse societies, from mobile hunters to farming towns and confederacies.

## Facts
- The [[Peopling of the Americas]] began during the last ice age.
- Regional societies included the mound-building cultures of the Mississippi valley and Ancestral Puebloans.
- The [[Haudenosaunee Confederacy]] was a political union of five (later six) nations.
- European colonization from the 16th century changed the continent through disease and conquest.

## Context & Connections
The region is closely tied to the [[Columbian Exchange]] and to the [[Age of Revolutions]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes a wide range of political forms that did not resemble the states of the Old World, and that are easy to overlook if 'civilization' is defined by cities and writing.
