## Summary
Mesoamerica is the cultural region from central Mexico to Central America. It developed farming (maize, beans, squash), monumental cities, writing, and a sophisticated calendar independently of the Old World.

## Facts
- Early complex societies included the [[Olmecs]].
- The [[Maya]] developed a full writing system and precise astronomy.
- Major cities included [[Teotihuacan]] and [[Tenochtitlan]].
- The [[Aztec Empire]] was conquered by Spain in 1519 to 1521.

## Context & Connections
Mesoamerica was cut off from the Old World until the [[Columbian Exchange]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer regards Mesoamerica as a natural experiment: a second, independent case of the path from farming to writing, cities and states.
