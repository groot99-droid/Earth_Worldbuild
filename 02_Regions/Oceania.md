## Summary
Oceania includes Australia, New Guinea, and the Pacific islands. It holds both the oldest continuing cultures outside Africa and the last large landmasses settled by humans.

## Facts
- Humans reached Australia at least 50,000 years ago; see [[Aboriginal Australians]].
- New Guinea was an independent center of agriculture.
- Polynesian navigators settled the remote Pacific; see [[Polynesians]] and [[Polynesian Settlement of the Pacific]].
- Aotearoa New Zealand was among the last habitable lands settled, around 1250 to 1300 CE.

## Context & Connections
Oceania sits at the edge of [[Southeast Asia]] and the wider Pacific.

## Observer's Reading
The Observer regards Oceania as home to the species' most spectacular feats of navigation and its longest continuous cultural traditions.
