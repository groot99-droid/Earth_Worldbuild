## Summary
The Andes are the mountain spine of western South America. Highland and coastal societies developed complex states without wheeled transport or a conventional writing system.

## Facts
- The [[Inca Empire]] became the largest pre-Columbian state in the Americas.
- Potatoes and llamas were domesticated in the Andes.
- The Inca recorded information on knotted cords called quipu.
- The site of [[Machu Picchu]] dates to the 15th century.

## Context & Connections
The Andes were cut off from the Old World until the [[Columbian Exchange]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes that the Andes show a state coordinating millions of people using memory-outsourcing that is not writing as most people define it.
