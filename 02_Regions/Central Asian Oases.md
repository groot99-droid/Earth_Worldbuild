## Summary
The Central Asian Oases are the river valleys and oasis cities between the steppe and the Iranian plateau, including Samarkand and Bukhara, which served as hubs of the Silk Roads.

## Facts
- The region, known in antiquity as Sogdiana and Transoxiana, was home to the [[Sogdians]].
- It was conquered by [[Alexander's Conquests]] and later brought into the Islamic world in the early 8th century.
- It was the center of the [[Timurid Empire]], with its capital at [[Samarkand]].
- Scholars such as [[Ulugh Beg]] made it a center of astronomy in the 15th century.

## Context & Connections
The oases lay between the [[Central Asian Steppe]] and the [[Iranian Plateau]], and were crossroads of the [[Opening of the Silk Roads]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes how these cities lived by relaying goods and ideas between larger powers. A place between empires can become an empire of its own kind.
