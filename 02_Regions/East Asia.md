## Summary
East Asia includes China, Korea, Japan and neighboring regions. China's centralized state tradition is among the longest continuous political traditions.

## Facts
- Farming of millet and rice began independently in the Yellow and Yangtze river basins.
- The first unified empire was created in 221 BCE; see [[Unification of China under Qin]].
- Major inventions include [[Paper]], [[Gunpowder]], the [[Magnetic Compass]] and printing.
- Major states included the [[Shang Dynasty]], [[Han Dynasty]] and [[Song Dynasty]].

## Context & Connections
East Asia trades with the [[Central Asian Steppe]] and [[Southeast Asia]]; see [[Zheng He]] and [[Confucius]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes a distinctive continuity: a written language and administrative tradition that persisted through repeated collapses and re-unifications.
