## Summary
The Human Timeline covers the last 300,000 years: the origin of Homo sapiens, prehistoric dispersals, early states, world religions, empires, and the modern era.

## How to Read This
Includes events, people, cultures, technologies, and places dated from about 300,000 years ago. See the [[Deep-Time Timeline]] for earlier history and the [[Master Timeline]] for the combined sequence.
