## Summary
The Deep-Time Timeline covers Earth's history before humans: planetary formation, the origin of life, oxygenation, mass extinctions, and the evolution of the lineage leading to humans.

## How to Read This
Includes events and organisms dated before about 300,000 years ago. See the [[Master Timeline]] for the full sequence and the [[Human Timeline]] for the last 300,000 years.
