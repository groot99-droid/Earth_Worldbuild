## Summary
The Master Timeline lists every era and event in the vault in chronological order, from the formation of the Solar System to the present.

## How to Read This
Dates are years relative to 1 CE (negative is BCE). Deep-time dates are shown in Ga (billions of years ago), Ma (millions), or ka (thousands). All dates before about 1000 CE are approximate. This table is generated from note frontmatter by `_RAG/tools/update_hubs.py`; edit the source notes, not the table.
