## Summary
The Information Age (1945 to the present) is the era of nuclear weapons, spaceflight, computers, and the internet, together with the sharp rise in population and environmental impact known as the [[Great Acceleration]].

## Facts
- Nuclear weapons appeared in 1945; see [[Nuclear Age]].
- The first artificial satellite launched in 1957; see [[Space Age]].
- The digital computer and the internet transformed communication; see [[Digital Revolution]].
- Population grew from about 2.5 billion (1950) to about 8 billion (2022).

## Context & Connections
The Information Age follows the [[Industrial Age]] and overlaps the proposed [[Anthropocene]]. See also [[Green Revolution]] and [[Modern Climate Change]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer sees a species that has, for the first time, built tools able to end its own history and tools able to record it in full. Memory-outsourcing has reached a scale no earlier era could imagine.
