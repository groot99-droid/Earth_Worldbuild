## Summary
The Early Modern Period (about 1500 to 1800 CE) is the era of oceanic exploration, global trade, the printing press, and the [[Scientific Revolution]]. It is also the era of colonial empires and mass forced migration.

## Facts
- The [[Columbian Exchange]] linked the Americas with Afro-Eurasia.
- The [[Atlantic Slave Trade]] forcibly moved millions of Africans.
- The [[Movable-Type Printing Press]] spread across Europe.
- Major empires included the [[Ottoman Empire]] and, in Asia, the Mughal and Qing states, alongside expanding European colonial powers.

## Context & Connections
The Early Modern Period leads into the [[Industrial Age]] and the [[Age of Revolutions]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes that for the first time, every inhabited continent was in continuous contact. The species became, for the first time, a single connected system, though with sharply unequal terms.
