## Summary
The Iron Age (about 1200 to 500 BCE, varying by region) is the era in which iron tools and weapons became widespread and new empires and belief systems emerged.

## Facts
- Iron ore is more widely available than tin and copper; see [[Iron Smelting]].
- The [[Alphabet]] spread widely and simplified literacy.
- Large territorial empires, including the [[Achaemenid Persians]], emerge near the end of the era.
- The [[Axial Age]] begins in this period.

## Context & Connections
The Iron Age follows the [[Bronze Age Collapse]] and leads into [[Classical Antiquity]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer sees the Iron Age as a democratization of tools and, therefore, of armies: cheaper metal meant more people could be equipped.

## Open Questions
- Did iron drive social change, or follow it?
