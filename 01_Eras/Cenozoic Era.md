## Summary
The Cenozoic Era began 66 million years ago and continues today. It is the age of mammals, grasslands, and cooling climate, and it includes all of human history.

## Facts
- Periods: Paleogene, Neogene, Quaternary.
- Mammals and birds diversified after the [[Cretaceous-Paleogene Extinction]].
- Global climate cooled overall, and ice sheets appeared at the poles, then repeatedly expanded and retreated.
- Primates appeared early in the era; the hominin lineage diverged about 7 million years ago. See [[Divergence of the Hominin Lineage]].

## Context & Connections
The Cenozoic contains the [[Pleistocene Epoch]], the [[Holocene Epoch]], and the proposed [[Anthropocene]].
