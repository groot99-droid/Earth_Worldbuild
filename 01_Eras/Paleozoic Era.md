## Summary
The Paleozoic Era (538.8 to 251.9 million years ago) saw the rise of animals with hard parts, the colonization of land, forests, and the first tetrapods. It ends with the largest mass extinction in Earth's history.

## Facts
- Periods: Cambrian, Ordovician, Silurian, Devonian, Carboniferous, Permian.
- Plants and animals moved onto land; see [[Colonization of Land]].
- Major extinctions include [[Late Ordovician Extinction]], [[Late Devonian Extinction]], and the [[Permian-Triassic Extinction]].
- Trilobites, one of the era's signature animals, are described in [[Trilobites]].

## Context & Connections
The Paleozoic opens with the [[Cambrian Explosion]] and closes with the end-Permian collapse, which cleared the way for the [[Mesozoic Era]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer sees the Paleozoic as the era when life became a planetary system: it began building soil, forests, and oxygen-rich air that later species inherited.
