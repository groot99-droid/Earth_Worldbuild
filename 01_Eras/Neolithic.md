## Summary
The Neolithic is the era in which farming, animal herding, pottery, and permanent villages developed in several parts of the world independently, starting about 11,500 years ago in Southwest Asia.

## Facts
- Agriculture arose independently in several regions, including the Fertile Crescent, China, Mesoamerica, the Andes, and New Guinea.
- See [[The Agricultural Revolution]] and [[Animal Domestication]].
- Pottery, [[Pottery]], became widespread.
- Large monuments such as [[Göbekli Tepe]] and early towns such as [[Çatalhöyük]] date to this era.
- The timing varies by region; the Neolithic ends when metal use begins, so the end date differs across the world.

## Context & Connections
The Neolithic sits in the [[Holocene Epoch]] and leads into the [[Bronze Age]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer sees the Neolithic as a bargain with consequences: more food, more people, more disease, more inequality. See [[The Settling and Its Bargain]].
