## Summary
The Bronze Age (about 3300 to 1200 BCE in the Near East) is the era of bronze metallurgy, early states, and the first writing.

## Facts
- Bronze is an alloy of copper and tin; see [[Bronze Metallurgy]].
- The first cities and writing systems appear: [[The Urban Revolution]], [[Invention of Writing]].
- Major Bronze Age states included the [[Sumerians]], [[Ancient Egyptians]], [[Indus Valley Civilization]], [[Minoans]] and [[Shang Dynasty]].
- In the eastern Mediterranean the era ends in the [[Bronze Age Collapse]].

## Context & Connections
The Bronze Age sits between the [[Neolithic]] and the [[Iron Age]]. Long-distance tin trade made it an era of wide exchange-webs.

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes that the first states and the first writing arrived together. Writing seems to have started as accounting, not poetry.

## Open Questions
- How much did long-distance tin trade shape early states?
