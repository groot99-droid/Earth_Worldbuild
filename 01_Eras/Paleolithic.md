## Summary
The Paleolithic (Old Stone Age) is the long era of chipped-stone tools and foraging societies, from about 2.6 million years ago to the end of the last ice age.

## Facts
- The earliest known stone tools date to about 3.3 million years ago (Lomekwi) and 2.6 million years ago (Oldowan); see [[Oldowan Stone Tools]].
- Fire control, [[Control of Fire]], appears well before the end of the era.
- Modern humans spread across the globe; see [[Out of Africa Dispersal]].
- Symbolic behavior, including [[Emergence of Symbolic Art]], appears in its later stages.
- It is about 99 percent of the time humans and their ancestors have made tools.

## Context & Connections
The Paleolithic sits inside the [[Pleistocene Epoch]] and ends with the shift to farming known as the [[Neolithic]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes that for roughly 99 percent of the tool-making record the species lived as small mobile groups. Everything called 'civilization' is a recent variation on a very old pattern.
