## Summary
The Holocene Epoch began about 11,700 years ago, at the end of the last ice age, and is the warm, relatively stable interval in which agriculture, cities, and writing all arose.

## Facts
- The Holocene is an interglacial period with a comparatively stable climate.
- All of recorded history falls inside it.
- Its lower boundary is defined by a marker in Greenland ice cores.
- Human population grew from a few million to about eight billion during it.

## Context & Connections
The Holocene is the geological frame for the [[Neolithic]] through the [[Information Age]]. See [[The Agricultural Revolution]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer notes a striking correlation: the species built farms and cities only after the climate stopped swinging. Whether this is cause, coincidence, or both is a serious open question.

## Open Questions
- Did the stable Holocene climate make agriculture possible, or did agriculture arise anyway?
