## Summary
The Anthropocene is a proposed geological interval in which human activity is the dominant influence on Earth's systems. In March 2024 the International Union of Geological Sciences declined to formalize it as an official epoch, but the term remains widely used.

## Facts
- A proposed start date is around 1950, tied to the [[Great Acceleration]] and to markers such as radioactive fallout.
- Other proposed starts include the beginning of agriculture and the [[Industrial Revolution]].
- Cited signals include changes to atmospheric carbon dioxide, plastics, and species extinction rates.
- The Subcommission on Quaternary Stratigraphy voted on 4 March 2024 to reject formalization as an epoch, and the IUGS upheld the result on 20 March 2024. The formal geological decision does not settle the scientific or cultural question.

## Context & Connections
The Anthropocene is a lens on the [[Information Age]] and the [[Great Acceleration]]. See [[The Species as a Geological Force]].

## Observer's Reading
The Observer regards the Anthropocene as the point where the species became legible in the rock record. It is the first time a single species' behavior is a planetary variable.
