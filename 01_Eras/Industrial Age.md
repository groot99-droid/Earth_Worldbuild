## Summary
The Industrial Age (about 1760 to 1945) is the era in which machines and fossil fuels transformed production, transport, and warfare, and in which populations and cities grew rapidly.

## Facts
- Steam power, factories, railways, and telegraphs spread; see [[Steam Engine]] and [[Industrial Revolution]].
- Electric power, oil, and chemistry expanded the transformation in the late 19th and early 20th centuries; see [[Electrical Power Grid]].
- Global population passed one billion around 1804.
- The era closes with the two world wars: [[World War I]] and [[World War II]].

## Context & Connections
The Industrial Age follows the [[Early Modern Period]] and precedes the [[Information Age]]. The [[Age of Revolutions]] overlaps its first century.

## Observer's Reading
The Observer sees a species learning to release ancient stored sunlight as fuel. This one change multiplied the power available to each person, and made sanctioned harm far more destructive.

## Open Questions
- How much of the industrial takeoff was contingent on specific locations?
