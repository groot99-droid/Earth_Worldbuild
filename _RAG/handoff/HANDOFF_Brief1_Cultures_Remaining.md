## 2. What the pilot taught (use before launching the rest)

- **Pipeline that worked:** per title one writer agent (checks title free, reads Celts.md and Kalinago.md, WebSearch research, writes the file, trims to 300-330 words, validates its own file) then an independent skeptic agent (lists every checkable claim, tries to refute each, repairs with surgical Edits, re-measures words, re-validates). **All 8 verifiers returned `fixed`**, so the second pass is earning its cost: typical fixes were attributing a claim to its source instead of asserting it ("Livy describes a league of twelve"), correcting counts (three Pyrgi tablets, not one bilingual tablet), hedging a legendary date ("traditionally 396 BCE" for Veii), and softening causal claims in the Reading.
- **Cost:** 17 agents, **2.36 million subagent tokens, 588 tool calls, about 25 minutes for 8 notes** (about 295k tokens per note). The remaining 70-ish notes would be about 20 million tokens and 3-4 hours if run batch by batch. Options: cap verifier searches (for example "at most 10 WebSearch calls, spend them on the writer's flagged claims first"), run two batches concurrently (concurrency cap is min(16, CPUs-2); each batch is 8 chains), or accept the cost.
- **Search budget:** several verifiers reported that their WebSearch budget ran out (one said 200 of 200, one said after four searches), leaving some claims checked against a single source. Find out whether this cap is per agent or shared across the workflow before a big run, and tell verifiers to spend searches on the writer's `unverifiedOrDisputedClaims` first.
- **Britannica returns HTTP 403 to WebFetch.** Notes that cite `[[Encyclopaedia Britannica]]` rest on search snippets only; Garifuna therefore cites only `[[English Wikipedia]]`. Rule for the rest: cite Britannica only if a search snippet or other page actually supports the claim; English Wikipedia alone is acceptable. Yamnaya also cites `[[Lazaridis et al. 2025 Indo-European Origins]]` (an existing source note); other existing source notes in `07_Sources/` may be cited if they are on topic.
- **Judgment calls the fact-check pass should look at** (verifiers left these as they are, stated in the notes as approximate): Etruscans `date_end -264` is a convention (Volsinii) and `era: Iron Age` covers a span that runs into Classical Antiquity; Anglo-Saxons `date_start 450` sits below the Medieval bound of 500 (validator accepts it) and `date_end 1066` is political; Garifuna `date_start 1635` is traditional (some sources say about 1675) and deportation figures conflict (4,195 vs over 5,000 to Baliceaux; 2,248 vs about 2,500 survivors); Lakota `date_start 1760` postdates the horse acquisition and Missouri crossing date is unstated; Mississippian `date_end 1450` (chiefdoms still flourished at de Soto, 1539), Cahokia peak population disputed (6,000-40,000); Hawaiian Kingdom Committee of Safety membership split and the Blount versus Morgan reports not re-checked; Funan is known mainly from Chinese sources, the canal dating and Rudravarman embassy years are thin, `low` confidence.
