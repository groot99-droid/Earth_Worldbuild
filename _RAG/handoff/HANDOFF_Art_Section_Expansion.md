## CONVENTIONS
- Vault note frontmatter: `type: person`, `era` and `region` links, `themes` from the closed set (`art`; plus `language` for literature, `technology` for architecture, `religion` for sacred music), `date_start`/`date_end`, `date_precision`, `related` only to titles that exist, `sources: ["[[English Wikipedia]]", "[[Art-Talk (Expansion Profiles)]]"]`, `confidence: medium` (low for the uncertain identities below, and say so in Facts), `status: seed`, `tags: [artist, <discipline>]`, `art_talk: <slug>`, no `fact_checks` (the sweep adds them). Body sections: Summary, Facts, Context & Connections, Observer's Reading, Open Questions. Match the exemplar notes. Every Context ends by naming the era, region and theme hubs.
- Era by career (vault ranges: Bronze Age -3300 to -1200, Iron Age -1200 to -500, Classical Antiquity -500 to 500, Medieval 500 to 1500 (born before about 1470), Early Modern 1500 to 1800, Industrial Age 1760 to 1945, Information Age after 1945). 20th-century rule: the era containing the career midpoint, ties to the earlier era; mainly after 1945 is Information Age.
- Regions are the 21 vault region titles in `02_Regions/`. Art-Talk profile region uses the 8-value schema: Mediterranean Basin to Europe; Nile Valley and Sub-Saharan Africa to Africa; Mongolia to East Asia; Kazakhstan to West & Central Asia; Aron of Kangeq to Americas; Johan Turi to Europe; the rest as in the ledger's `arttalk_region`. Check the Siberia and the Arctic region note's scope before writing Aron of Kangeq and Johan Turi.
- Art-Talk profile: front matter per `Art-Talk-main/README.md` (slug = file name, `region` from the 8 values, `discipline` list from the 12 values, BCE years negative with `lifespan` as display text, `year_label` if needed), at least 5 works each with `commons_file` or `wiki`, sections Overview, Life, Style & Technique, Legacy, Key Works (left empty; build fills it), Sources. Optional `kind:` on a work (likeness, document, venue, instrument, depiction, place) is ignored by the build but records the slot: performing and document-based artists need at least 3 slot kinds among their 5 files; `cover` is the most iconic slot. Recipes: composer = portrait, autograph page, premiere venue, instrument, period print; dancer = portrait, notation or programme, venue, costume, lithograph; playwright = portrait, first edition, theatre photo, mask or set, scene illustration; calligrapher = portrait, signed specimen, architectural inscription, tools, later copy.
- Titles unique vault-wide (Wikipedia lead-article title, with disambiguator when needed); check again immediately before each write. The vault is shared and live: other sessions edit it continuously.
- Low-confidence identities/dates (state uncertainty in `confidence: low` and Facts): Homer, Bharata Muni, Kalidasa, Andal, Saint Yared, Imru' al-Qais, Fre Seyon, Imhotep, Marcos Cipac de Aquino, Francisco Tito Yupanqui, Izumo no Okuni.
- High image risk (at most 2 per batch): see ledger `img_risk`.
