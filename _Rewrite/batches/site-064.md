=== source/science-news-on-the-single-exodus-from-africa
title: Science News on the Single Exodus from Africa
type: source
date: 
themes: 
src: d23183eec9c1
mode: essay-self-help
observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
Genetic studies placing the single major dispersal that gave rise to present-day non-Africans at roughly 50,000 to 72,000 years ago, and evidence of an earlier, smaller exodus about 120,000 years ago that left about 2 percent of Papuan genomes.
--- extra 1: Caveats
Fetched 2026-09-20 with WebFetch, which returns a model-generated summary of the page rather than the raw text. The article itself warns that genetic dating is imperfect (mutation rates, sampling, method), and reports a climate study that favored 80,000 to 100,000 years ago as more plausible. The vault's 70,000 to 50,000 year range is therefore an approximation, and the older window is a live alternative.
