=== teatro-olimpico
title: Teatro Olimpico
artist: Andrea Palladio (1508–1580)
year: 1580–1585
medium: Wood, stucco and painted trompe-l'oeil scenery over a masonry shell
location: Vicenza, Republic of Venice (Italy)
src: 095ef74be363
mode: essay-self-help
--- description
Palladio's last design, a covered theatre modelled on his study of ancient Roman precedent, completed after his death by Scamozzi. Behind its stage arch, streets built in forced perspective recede at a scale that makes them appear far longer than the shallow space allows, an illusion still used at the world's oldest surviving indoor theatre in continuous use.

=== san-giorgio-maggiore
title: San Giorgio Maggiore
artist: Andrea Palladio (1508–1580)
year: 1566–1610
medium: Istrian stone facade over a brick basilica plan
location: Isola di San Giorgio Maggiore, Venice, Republic of Venice (Italy)
src: 36ce406cbd0a
mode: essay-self-help
--- description
A monastery church on its own island across the water from St Mark's Square, its bright white facade overlapping two classical temple fronts of different heights to mask the awkward profile of a basilica's tall nave and lower side aisles, a problem Palladio solved more elegantly here than any architect before him.

=== il-redentore
title: Il Redentore
artist: Andrea Palladio (1508–1580)
year: 1577–1592
medium: Istrian stone facade over a brick and stone basilica
location: Giudecca, Venice, Republic of Venice (Italy)
src: ebff2359a3f8
mode: essay-self-help
--- description
A votive church commissioned by the Venetian Senate after a plague that killed roughly a third of the city's population, still visited every July in a footbridge-borne procession of thanksgiving. Its luminous white facade and centrally planned interior, lit from hidden windows, are considered among the calmest and most resolved of all his church designs.

=== netherlandish-proverbs
title: Netherlandish Proverbs

year: 1559
medium: Oil on oak panel, 117 × 163 cm
location: Gemäldegalerie, Berlin
src: d2dc456ab8aa
mode: epic-fantasy
--- description
A village packed with people acting out more than 100 sayings, taken literally. A man bangs his head against a brick wall, another fills in the well after the calf has drowned, a woman ties the devil to a pillow. === childrens-games
title: Children's Games
artist: Pieter Bruegel the Elder (c. 1525–1569)
year: 1560
medium: Oil on oak panel, 118 × 161 cm
location: Kunsthistorisches Museum, Vienna
src: b40d0f321e26
mode: confessional-poetry
--- description
Some 230 children fill a town square with about 80 games: leapfrog, hoops, stilts, blind man's buff, spinning tops, playing at weddings and processions. Seen from above, the crowded scene is a remarkable record of 16th-century play. It may also carry a moral: that adult affairs are just as foolish as a child's games.

=== tower-of-babel
title: The Tower of Babel

year: 1563
medium: Oil on oak panel, 114 × 155 cm
location: Kunsthistorisches Museum, Vienna
src: e4389afa46f0
mode: horror-prose
--- description
A colossal spiralling tower, modelled on Rome's Colosseum, rises above a Flemish harbour city. King Nimrod inspects the work in the foreground while hundreds of tiny labourers haul stone, operate cranes and build scaffolding. But the tower is already leaning, and its lower levels are crumbling before the top is finished: a warning about human pride.

=== hunters-in-the-snow

location: Kunsthistorisches Museum, Vienna
src: b88d78de6969

--- description
Three tired hunters and their dogs trudge home over a hill with a single fox to show for their day. Below, villagers skate on frozen ponds under a green-grey sky. === the-harvesters

year: 1565
medium: Oil on wood, 119 × 162 cm
location: Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York
src: d4acc2bd18e1
mode: epic-fantasy
--- description
Another of the seasons series, showing late summer. Peasants scythe and bundle golden wheat while others rest under a pear tree to eat bread, porridge and cheese, one sprawled asleep. The landscape stretches to a hazy bay. It is one of the first great paintings of ordinary working life, with no religious or mythological story attached.

year: 1567
medium: Oil on oak panel, 114 × 164 cm
location: Kunsthistorisches Museum, Vienna
src: cc652daa8c50
mode: epic-fantasy
--- description
A wedding feast in a barn: two men carry bowls of porridge on an unhinged door, a piper looks hungrily at the food, a child licks a plate clean. location: National Museum, Poznań
src: 7c558be0a4ce

--- description
Three of Sofonisba's sisters, Lucia, Minerva and Europa, play chess outdoors while a servant looks on. --- description
Anguissola paints herself at work on an image of the Virgin and Child, brush in hand, looking straight at the viewer. A double portrait with a twist. Sofonisba shows her teacher Bernardino Campi painting her portrait, but she is the one who painted the whole picture. src: 145a3884202b

A slumped man in European dress sits on the ground with a wine flask, carefully shaded to give him volume. One page of a double-page scene from the illustrated history of Akbar's reign. year: c. 1590–1595
medium: Opaque watercolour and gold on paper
location: Victoria and Albert Museum, London
src: c361efaabeab
mode: essay-self-help
--- description
In 1567 two rival bands of Hindu ascetics fought over the right to a sacred bathing place at Thanesar. Akbar watched, and let some of his soldiers join the fight. year: c. 1600
medium: Ink and opaque watercolour on paper
location: Cleveland Museum of Art
src: 4d0d7d02098a
mode: epic-fantasy
--- description
A lean, white-robed Jain monk walks along a river beneath trees, absorbed in thought, while life goes on around him. Signed and dated when Artemisia was just 17, this is her earliest known painting. Two older men lean over a wall to pressure the bathing Susanna, who twists away in distress. year: c. 1620
medium: Oil on canvas, 199 × 162.5 cm
location: Uffizi Gallery, Florence
src: 2a1bf2acb624
mode: horror-prose
--- description
Judith and her maid pin down the Assyrian general and saw through his neck, the blood spraying in arcs across the white sheets. Unlike Caravaggio's hesitant, delicate Judith, Artemisia's women are strong, working together with physical effort. Painted in Seville when Velázquez was about 19, this *bodegón*, a kitchen scene with still life, shows an old woman cooking eggs in a clay dish while a boy brings a melon and a flask. location: Museo del Prado, Madrid
src: a5624fcb4af6
mode: epic-fantasy
--- description
Painted for the Hall of Realms in the Buen Retiro Palace, it commemorates a Spanish victory of 1625 in the war with the Dutch.
