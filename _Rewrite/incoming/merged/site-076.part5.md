=== source/wikipedia-on-sun-yat-sen
src: 15981d8f0266
mode: essay-self-help
observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
This source backs Sun Yat-sen's birth and death dates and places, the 1894 Revive China Society, the 1905 Tongmenghui and its 1906 membership, his role around the 1911 Wuchang uprising and provisional presidency, and the 1904 Hawaii birth certificate.
--- extra 1: Caveats
It is a general-reference encyclopedia article whose dates and figures are consistent with standard accounts, though it was not cross-checked against Britannica or specialist biographies.

=== source/wikipedia-on-suriyothai
src: bdb2792c9a63
mode: essay-self-help
observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
This source backs her marriage to the future King Maha Chakkraphat, her death in December 1548 in the elephant duel with the Viceroy of Prome, the absence of the episode from Burmese chronicles, the memorial chedi, and the 2001 film.
--- extra 1: Caveats
It is a tertiary source whose infobox gives an unqualified birth year of 1511, and it does not discuss whether the woman who died was the queen herself.

=== source/wikipedia-on-tamar-of-georgia
