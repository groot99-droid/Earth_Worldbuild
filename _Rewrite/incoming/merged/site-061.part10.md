--- summary
This entry presents the El Dorado legend as a coronation ritual in which a new Muisca ruler, covered in gold dust, entered Lake Guatavita, and describes the Muisca as skilled goldsmiths who obtained gold largely by trade.
--- extra 1: Caveats
This was consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-25 (results listed World History Encyclopedia, Smarthistory, Wikipedia and Brewminate); none of the pages was opened. The summaries did not confirm the claim that the Spanish found emeralds rather than golden cities, so that statement in the note stays unchecked. The legend is known largely from Spanish accounts written after the conquest.
