title: Wikipedia on Wangari Maathai
type: source
src: fc6a14039222
mode: essay-self-help
observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
This source backs her birth and death dates, the 1971 doctorate, the 1977 founding of the Green Belt Movement, the 2002 election and 2003 ministerial post, the 2004 Nobel Peace Prize, and the 2004 HIV dispute.
--- extra 1: Caveats
It is a general encyclopedia article that has not been checked against the Nobel Committee or Britannica pages, and the claim that she was the first woman in East and Central Africa to hold the doctorate is stated in Wikipedia's own voice.

=== source/wikipedia-on-war-of-the-golden-stool

type: source
src: 796ef0c65689
mode: essay-self-help
observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
This source backs Hodgson's arrival in Kumasi on 25 March 1900 and his demand for the Golden Stool, the siege and relief of the fort on 15 July 1900, the 1901 exile of Yaa Asantewaa to the Seychelles, and the fact that the Golden Stool was never captured.
--- extra 1: Caveats
It is a tertiary, crowd-edited reference; the article's quoted speeches come through translation and later sources, and it gives no precise annexation date.

=== source/wikipedia-on-yaa-asantewaa
title: Wikipedia on Yaa Asantewaa
type: source
src: 93b00441482e
mode: essay-self-help
observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
This source backs her birth around 1840, her role as Queen Mother of Ejisu, the 1896 exile of Prempeh I, the March 1900 siege of Kumasi fort, her death in exile in the Seychelles on 17 October 1921, and the 1 January 1902 annexation.
--- extra 1: Caveats
It is a tertiary, crowd-edited reference; it quotes two different versions of her council speech, and gives the birth year only as c. 1840.
