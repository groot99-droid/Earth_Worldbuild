--- summary
This source backs Goya's birth and death dates and places, his 1773 marriage to Josefa Bayeu, his 1792-93 deafness, the 1799 Caprichos, the 1863 publication of The Disasters of War, the 1814 Second and Third of May paintings, the 1819-1823 Black Paintings, and the 1824 move to Bordeaux.
