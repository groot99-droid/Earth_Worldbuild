observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
A source for genetic studies that place the single major dispersal behind present-day non-Africans at roughly 50,000 to 72,000 years ago, and for evidence of an earlier, smaller exodus about 120,000 years ago that left about 2 percent of Papuan genomes.
--- extra 1: Caveats
Read in full on 2026-11-19 from sciencenews.org, fetched directly (10,610 characters). It was fetched on 2026-09-20 with WebFetch, which returns a model-generated summary of the page rather than the raw text. The article itself warns that genetic dating is imperfect (mutation rates, sampling, method), and it reports a climate study that favored 80,000 to 100,000 years ago as more plausible. So the vault's 70,000 to 50,000 year range is an approximation, and the older window is a live alternative. A date with error bars is still a date; you just hold it more loosely.
