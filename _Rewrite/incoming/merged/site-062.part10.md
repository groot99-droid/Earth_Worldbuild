=== source/moody-et-al-2024-last-universal-common-ancestor
src: c25f5315ba0d
mode: essay-self-help
observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
This estimates that the last universal common ancestor of all living cells lived about 4.2 billion years ago (a 95 percent interval of 4.09 to 4.33 billion), already part of an ecosystem, and it treats the route from the origin of life to that ancestor as an open question.
--- extra 1: Caveats
This was read in full on 2026-09-26 from the Europe PMC full-text XML of the open-access copy (PMC11383801; 67,785 characters). The url earlier in this note had pointed to a short commentary in the same journal issue (8(9): 1573-1574), not the research article the citation names; it now points to the article itself. The estimate comes from a molecular clock calibrated on pre-LUCA gene duplicates and carries the interval given above.
