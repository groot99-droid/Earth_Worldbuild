=== source/barboni-et-al-2017-early-formation-of-the-moon
title: Barboni et al. 2017 Early Formation of the Moon
src: 8538ba95a759
mode: essay-self-help
observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
A lunar zircon age of 4.51 Ga: uranium-lead and lutetium-hafnium data from Apollo 14 zircons require that the Moon had formed by 4.51 Ga, within the first 60 million years of the Solar System. The paper also reviews later estimates of 4.35 to 4.42 Ga, about 150 to 200 million years after the Solar System began.
--- extra 1: Caveats
This was read in full on 2026-09-26, from the Europe PMC full-text XML of the open-access copy (PMC5226643; 37,199 characters). The date of the Solar System itself, 4.5673 Ga, which the Theia note used to cite here, does not appear in this paper.
