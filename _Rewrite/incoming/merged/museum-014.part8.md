=== people--vincent-van-gogh--1
src: b14628dcbf53
mode: confessional-poetry
--- description
I stand before Vincent van Gogh (30 March 1853 to 29 July 1890), Dutch painter who worked in France, who painted for only about ten years, produced around 2,100 works, and sold almost nothing, yet whose vivid colour and expressive brushwork made him the most famous artist of modern times.

I read that he was born in Zundert in the southern Netherlands, the son of a Protestant minister; that at 16 he joined the art dealers Goupil & Cie and was dismissed in 1876; that he then tried teaching and preaching among coal miners in the Borinage; that in 1880 he decided to become an artist. I read that his younger brother Theo, an art dealer, supported him for the rest of his life, and that his letters, mainly to Theo, are among the most moving documents of any artist's life.

In 1886 he moved to Paris, met Pissarro, Toulouse-Lautrec, Émile Bernard and Gauguin, and discovered Impressionist colour and Japanese prints; he painted copies of two Hiroshige prints. In 1888 he moved to Arles. Gauguin joined him in October, and the partnership collapsed in December after Van Gogh cut off part of his own ear. He entered the asylum at Saint-Rémy in May 1889, where he painted The Starry Night.

In May 1890 he moved to Auvers-sur-Oise and painted about one canvas a day. He shot himself on 27 July 1890 and died two days later, aged 37.

A brother who never stopped answering. Who holds me up, and do I write back? Some lives are carried by one faithful correspondent.
