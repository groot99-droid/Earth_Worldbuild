=== source/slavery-abolition-in-britain-and-brazil-search-summaries
src: 78247e31c401
mode: essay-self-help
observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
This record holds Britain's 1807 ban on the slave trade, the 1833 abolition of slavery across its colonies with £20 million paid in compensation to slaveholders and none to the enslaved, and Brazil's Lei Aurea of 13 May 1888, the last abolition enacted in the Americas.
--- extra 1: Caveats
Consulted only through web search result summaries on 2026-09-25 (results listed Wikipedia, the History of Parliament, the Library of Congress, EBSCO Research Starters and Duke University Press); none of the pages was opened. The summaries describe the £20 million as about 40 percent of government spending in 1833, a comparison this record leaves aside. Actual freedom under the 1833 act arrived only through a staged apprenticeship, which this note does not cover.
