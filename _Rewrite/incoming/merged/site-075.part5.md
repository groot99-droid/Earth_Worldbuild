title: Wikipedia on Merv

--- summary
This page backs the Sasanian end and the Arab conquest, al-Ma'mun's residence at Merv from 813 to 818, Sultan Sanjar's reign and mausoleum, the 12th-century size estimate, the Mongol sack of 1221 with its chronicle death tolls, and the Abu Muslim declaration at Merv in February 748.
--- extra 1: Caveats
This is a general encyclopedia article read through a fetch tool that returned extracted passages; it gives Erk Kala as 7th century BC, does not give 1219 for Yaqut, and neither describes the 1221 death tolls as exaggerated nor mentions a 15th-century rebuilding.

=== source/wikipedia-on-mogao-caves
title: Wikipedia on Mogao Caves
src: 585f53f68b40
mode: essay-self-help
observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
This page backs the 366 CE tradition of Le Zun (Yuezun), the discovery of the Library Cave on 25 June 1900 and its early-11th-century sealing, the Stein acquisition of 1907 and the Pelliot acquisition of 1908, the UNESCO listing of 1987, and the founding of the Dunhuang Academy in 1944.

This is a general encyclopedia article; it opens with 500 temples, gives the mural area as about 46,000 square metres, dates Tibetan rule as 787-848 and Western Xia's invasion as 1035, and its figures differ slightly from those in the note and in the Dunhuang article.
