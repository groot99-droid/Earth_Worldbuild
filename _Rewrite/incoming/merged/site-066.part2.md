=== source/wikipedia-on-khadija-bint-khuwaylid
src: 532e56e1ca53
mode: essay-self-help
observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
This source backs her marriage to Muhammad circa 595 CE after he managed her Syria caravan, her standing as the first believer after his first revelation circa 610, her four daughters (Fatima among them) and two sons who died young, her death in November 619 as the start of the Year of Sorrow before the 622 Hijra, and Ibn Ishaq and Ibn Sa'd as her earliest eighth-ninth century biographers.
--- extra 1: Caveats
Wikipedia is a collaboratively edited encyclopedia rather than a primary source, and the article itself notes that her age at marriage — 40 versus roughly 28 — is disputed among the traditional sources it draws on.

=== source/wikipedia-on-la-malinche
src: 9a571b8fa92c
mode: essay-self-help
observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
This source backs the birth and death date estimates, Bernal Diaz del Castillo's account of her noble Nahua birth near the Coatzacoalcos River and her enslavement, the 1519 Tabasco presentation of enslaved women to Cortes, her abilities in Nahuatl, Maya and Spanish, her interpreting role alongside Geronimo de Aguilar, including at the 1519 Moctezuma II meetings in Tenochtitlan, her son Martin (b. c. 1522), and her marriage to Juan Jaramillo.
--- extra 1: Caveats
Wikipedia is collaboratively edited; the article itself flags her exact birthplace and death date as disputed among historians, and it gives only 'before February 1529' rather than a 1527-1529 range. It was read in full via WebFetch on 2026-09-27.
