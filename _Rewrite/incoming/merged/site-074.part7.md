=== source/wikipedia-on-history-of-jerusalem

mode: essay-self-help
observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
This entry backs the 516 BCE Second Temple, the 70 CE destruction, Bar Kokhba and Aelia Capitolina, the 1099 massacre, the 1187 Saladin recapture, and the 1517 Ottoman conquest.
--- extra 1: Caveats
A tertiary source: it dates the Bar Kokhba revolt 132 to 136 CE and the Muslim conquest to 638 CE, and archaeologists are divided over the biblical account of Solomon.

This entry backs the archaeological dating of Polynesian settlement — nothing confidently earlier than the Kaharoa Tephra c. 1314 CE, mtDNA 1250-1300 — and the relegation of the Kupe-and-fleet synthesis to myth.
