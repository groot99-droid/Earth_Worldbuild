title: Britannica on the Battle of Adwa
src: 6f0338d9787b
mode: essay-self-help
observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
The Battle of Adwa, on 1 March 1896 near Adwa in north-central Ethiopia: an Italian force of 14,500 men against an Ethiopian army of approximately 100,000, more than 6,000 killed within the Italian army (slightly more than half of them Italians, the rest African askari), 3,000 to 4,000 taken prisoner, and the Treaty of Addis Ababa of 26 October 1896, which abrogated the Treaty of Wichale.

The page does not give a low estimate of 73,000 Ethiopians, or an Italian strength of 17,800.

--- summary
The Battle of Austerlitz, on 2 December 1805, the first engagement of the War of the Third Coalition, in which Napoleon's 68,000 troops defeated almost 90,000 Russians and Austrians.
