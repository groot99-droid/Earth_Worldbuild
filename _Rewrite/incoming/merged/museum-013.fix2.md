I stand before Liliuokalani (1838 to 1917), the last monarch of the Kingdom of Hawaii, who reigned from 1891 until she was deposed in January 1893 by a group of residents backed by United States troops.

I read that she was born in Honolulu on 2 September 1838 and died there on 11 November 1917. I read that she succeeded her brother Kalākaua on 29 January 1891, the kingdom's only reigning queen. I read that in January 1893 she moved to issue a constitution restoring royal powers reduced by the 1887 "Bayonet Constitution."

On 17 January 1893 a Committee of Safety of mostly American and European residents overthrew her while United States Marines were ashore. She yielded to the United States government to avoid bloodshed and asked it to reinstate her. After a failed royalist uprising she was arrested and signed an abdication in January 1895.

She chose no bloodshed, and asked to be restored. How do you yield without surrendering? By asking, on the record, for what was taken.
