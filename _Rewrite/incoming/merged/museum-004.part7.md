=== daniel-in-the-lions-den
src: d7f698a7e521
mode: epic-fantasy
--- description
The prophet Daniel stands calmly in the pit, still as a stone at the bottom of a well, while lions pace around him like restless tides, and a shaft of light falls on him from a high window. Tanner reworked the subject over many years, returning to it as the sea returns to the shore. The deep blue-green tones and glowing light are characteristic of his mature style, and they give the scene a quiet, spiritual mood rather than violent drama.

=== gismonda
src: 904db2cf2ef6
mode: essay-self-help
--- description
Opportunity rarely arrives on schedule. This was a rush job, taken on over the Christmas holidays when no other designer was available, and this poster for Sarah Bernhardt's play made Mucha famous overnight. Its unusually tall, narrow format and muted, mosaic-like decoration were so unlike conventional theatre advertising that Bernhardt signed him to a six-year contract designing posters, sets and costumes for her productions. Say yes to the job nobody else can take, and do it in a grammar nobody else speaks.

=== job-poster
src: 823b075325ba
mode: essay-self-help
--- description
Consider how far a single line of hair can travel. This is an advertisement for a brand of cigarette rolling papers, and its central figure's hair spirals out to fill the entire circular frame in smoke-like tendrils, as if the product's own smoke had become ornament. It became one of the most widely reproduced commercial posters of the era, spreading the "Mucha style" of decorative female figures far beyond theatre advertising into everyday consumer branding. A style, once it finds the street, belongs to everyone.
