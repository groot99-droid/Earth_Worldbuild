=== source/britannica-on-beringia
title: Britannica on Beringia
src: 457785bd62f3
mode: essay-self-help
observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
Beringia served as an intermittent Pleistocene land connection between northeastern Asia and northwestern North America: the most recent one began to appear about 38,000 years ago, reached its greatest extent about 20,000 years ago, when sea level had dropped by as much as about 120 m, ran some 1,600 km from north to south, and stayed largely dry and unglaciated, covered in tundra vegetation; it is also named as at least one route by which humans reached the Americas.
--- extra 1: Caveats
This was read in full on 2026-09-26 as raw text from a Wayback Machine snapshot dated 2026-02-25 of britannica.com; the snapshot may differ from the current page (7,980 characters). The page does not mention mammoths or bison, the Beringian standstill hypothesis, or a flooding date of about 11,000 years ago; it says only that the Bering Strait opened at the end of the Pleistocene, about 11,700 years ago. Those details in the Beringia note are not confirmed here.
