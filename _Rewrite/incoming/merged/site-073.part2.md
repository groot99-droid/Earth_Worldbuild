=== source/wikipedia-on-ambiorix
src: ee2dc93c5ab5
mode: essay-self-help
observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
This source affirms Ambiorix and Cativolcus as joint leaders of the Eburones, the winter 54 BCE uprising and ambush of Sabinus and Cotta's troops, his escape across the Rhine per Florus, and Caesar's De Bello Gallico as principal source.
--- extra 1: Caveats
The article is short and gives neither the commanders' individual deaths, the legion composition, nor an exact final date for Ambiorix; it also offers no birth date or evidence of activity before 54 BCE.
