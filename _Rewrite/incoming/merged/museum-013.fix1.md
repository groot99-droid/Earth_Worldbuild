=== people--truganini--3
src: 55c76223936b

--- description
I stand before the name Truganini (about 1812 to 1876), a Tasmanian Aboriginal woman of the Nuenonne people, who grew up on Bruny Island, lived through the British colonization of Tasmania, and died in Hobart. She was later widely called "the last Tasmanian Aboriginal," a label that Tasmanian Aboriginal people reject, and I do not repeat it as true.

I read that she was born about 1812, reportedly at Recherche Bay in southern Tasmania. I read that accounts say members of her family were killed or abducted by colonists during the Black War of the 1820s and early 1830s. I read that in the 1830s she worked with George Augustus Robinson, a government-appointed conciliator who persuaded surviving Aboriginal people to move to Flinders Island, where disease and hardship were severe.

She later lived at Oyster Cove, south of Hobart, and died on 8 May 1876. Her skeleton was exhumed in 1878 and displayed in the Tasmanian Museum from 1904 until 1947, despite her reported fear of mutilation.

Her fear was reported, and still it was done. Who was the museum for? I stand in one now, and I owe her my quiet.
