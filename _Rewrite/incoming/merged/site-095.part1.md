=== source/usda-ers-ge-varieties-by-state-data-2000-25
src: 54ac49f1ff6d
mode: essay-self-help
observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
The Soybeans sheet gives herbicide-tolerant (HT) soybeans as a percent of all soybeans planted in the United States each year from 2000 to 2025: 93 percent in 2010, and 96 percent in 2025. Because HT is the only GE soybean type with data, the HT and all-GE rows are the same.
--- extra 1: Caveats
Read on 2026-11-19 by downloading the XLSX file linked from the data product page and reading its Soybeans sheet in full; the CSV of the same name holds only the 2025 rows. The figures are ERS estimates from survey data, not a census, and the file starts in 2000, so the 1997 to 1999 figures are not in it.
