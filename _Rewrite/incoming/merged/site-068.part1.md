=== source/wikipedia-on-yermak-timofeyevich
title: Wikipedia on Yermak Timofeyevich
type: source
src: 8596139bba1c
mode: essay-self-help
observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
This entry backs Yermak's life dates (c. 1532 to 5 or 6 August 1585): the 840-man force, the October 1582 capture of Qashliq, the tsar's pardon and gifts, the ambush that killed him and the legend that he drowned, and the founding of Tyumen in 1586 and Tobolsk in 1587.
--- extra 1: Caveats
The article contradicts itself on the death year — 1585 in the infobox, 1584 in the narrative — and on when the expedition began, and its evidence rests on late, biased chronicles: the Stroganov Chronicle and the Sinodnik.
