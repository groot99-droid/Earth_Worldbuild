--- summary
This source backs Tomyris as a 6th-century BC Massagetae queen known only from Herodotus, the 530 BC date of Cyrus's death in her battle, her refusal of marriage, and the death of her son Spargapises.
--- extra 1: Caveats
It is a general-audience encyclopedia article that retells Herodotus, so it verifies what the tradition says rather than what historically happened, and this read was a WebFetch extract of the page rather than a full-page reading.

=== source/wikipedia-on-truganini
title: Wikipedia on Truganini
type: source
src: 792f80037519
mode: essay-self-help
observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
This source backs Truganini's c. 1812 birth, her death in Hobart on 8 May 1876, the 1878 disinterment, the 1904 skeleton display, the 1976 cremation and scattering, and the rejection of the 'last Tasmanian Aboriginal' narrative.
--- extra 1: Caveats
It is a tertiary, crowd-edited source whose birthplace, Recherche Bay, differs from the Bruny Island birthplace given in some other accounts, and the exact birth year stays approximate.
