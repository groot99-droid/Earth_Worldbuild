=== tree-of-knowledge-1
title: The Tree of Knowledge, No. 1
src: 14b69d3579d8
mode: epic-fantasy
--- description
This is one of eight delicate works on paper that show the biblical tree of knowledge as a symmetrical, diagram-like form of branches, birds, circles and hearts, balanced as a frost-pattern on a pane. Af Klint braided Christian, Theosophical and scientific imagery into a personal symbolic language, as three streams join to make one river. It is as much a spiritual diagram as a painting, a map of roots and crowns. Glenstone acquired the series of eight in 2022, among the most significant af Klint works ever sold.

=== the-swan-17
title: The Swan, No. 17 (Group IX/SUW)
src: 12e3a8c83e1b
mode: epic-fantasy
--- description
The Swan series begins with realistic black and white swans and gradually turns them into pure geometry, as a bird on a lake becomes, at dusk, only a shape of light and shadow. In this late work a single circle, split into black and white on one side and blue and yellow on the other around a pink core, floats on a red ground in perfect balance, still as a moon on a windless lake. The series shows af Klint moving from symbol to hard-edged abstraction years before such painting became common: a path walked before the road was built.

=== altarpiece-1
title: Altarpiece, No. 1 (Group X)
src: 50c2450e339b
mode: epic-fantasy
--- description
This belongs to the last series of the *Paintings for the Temple*. A rainbow-coloured triangle rises like a mountain or a pyramid toward a golden disc, a sun waiting at a summit, symbolising the soul's ascent from the physical to the spiritual world. Af Klint imagined these altarpieces hanging in a spiral-shaped temple, winding upward like a shell, a building that was never realised. The paintings remain; the temple stands only in the mind that dreamed it.

=== the-scream
title: The Scream
src: 6bfc62a3daf0
mode: horror-prose
--- description
A bridge. A blood-red sky. A skull-like figure, clutching its face. Munch recalled walking at sunset and feeling "an infinite scream passing through nature". Not heard. Felt. He made several versions: painted, pastel, printed. Again. And again. The image has become a universal symbol of modern anxiety. A 1895 pastel version sold for $119.9 million in 2012. The scream is still passing.

=== madonna
title: Madonna
src: 20e5a9135b74
mode: confessional-poetry
--- description
I see a nude woman with long black hair and closed eyes. I see a red halo above her head. I see her sway against a dark, swirling ground. Munch combined the sacred and the erotic, conception and death, and I was taught to keep those in separate rooms. In a later lithograph version he added a frame of swimming sperm and a foetus-like figure. It belongs to his *Frieze of Life* cycle about love and mortality. Where does the one end and the other begin? Every beginning carries its ending inside it.

=== vampire
title: Vampire (Love and Pain)
src: 4ec20bc9b389
mode: horror-prose
--- description
A woman. Flaming red hair. She bends over a man. Her mouth at his neck. Her hair falls over him like streams of blood. Munch first called it *Love and Pain*. Then a critic named it *Vampire*. The title stuck. It never came off. The embrace is ambiguous: comforting, or consuming. Tender. Destructive. For Munch, love was both at once.

=== the-dance-of-life
title: The Dance of Life
src: ecada6c3bbb5
mode: epic-fantasy
--- description
Couples dance on a shore on a midsummer night, beneath the pillar of the moon's reflection, laid across the sea like a road of pale gold. Three women frame the scene, as dawn, noon and dusk frame a day: a young woman in white; a woman in red at the centre, locked in a dance with a man; and an older woman in black. They represent innocence, passion and grief, a whole life turning on one shore. It is one of the key paintings of Munch's *Frieze of Life*.

=== girls-on-the-bridge
title: The Girls on the Bridge
src: b016190f589a
mode: confessional-poetry
--- description
I remember summer nights that never grew dark. I see three girls lean on the railing of a jetty in the small coastal town of Åsgårdstrand on a light summer night, while a great tree and its reflection loom over the water. The mood is calmer than in *The Scream*, which is set on a similar bridge, but still charged with longing and melancholy. I know that calm. I know that charge. Munch painted many versions of this popular subject. Some evenings we return to all our lives. Longing does not need a scream to be heard.

=== the-blue-rider
title: The Blue Rider
src: c23432e4a596
mode: epic-fantasy
--- description
A rider in a blue cloak gallops across a sunlit meadow, painted in dabs of colour that break up the forms as sunlight breaks on a running stream. The small painting is still figurative, but colour is already doing the expressive work, like wind that is felt before the storm arrives. Its title later gave its name, as a river gives its name to a valley, to the artists' group that Kandinsky and Franz Marc founded in 1911.

=== composition-iv
title: Composition IV
src: 4720ada5e913
mode: epic-fantasy
--- description
Behind the bursts of colour and black lines lie hidden images, as old ruins lie beneath a forest floor: Cossacks with lances, a castle on a blue mountain, reclining lovers, a rainbow. Kandinsky was gradually stripping away recognisable subject matter, as autumn strips a tree down to its branches, so that colour and line could act directly on the viewer's emotions, like music.

=== composition-vi
title: Composition VI
src: be69ba17a5b4
mode: epic-fantasy
--- description
For months Kandinsky struggled with the theme of the Flood, as a swimmer struggles against a current, and in the end he found release by repeating the word "Deluge" to himself, like a charm spoken against the dark. The finished canvas is a storm of colliding colour, line and form, a vision of destruction and rebirth in which the original subject is almost entirely dissolved, as a drowned village dissolves beneath rising water.
