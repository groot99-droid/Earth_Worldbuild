=== source/britannica-on-the-chola-dynasty
title: Britannica on the Chola Dynasty
src: 0a85480c06dd
mode: essay-self-help
observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
The Chola line begins with Vijayalaya, who reigned about 850 to 870 and opened the conquest of Pallava territory, continues through Rajaraja I, who reigned 985 to 1014 and raised the Brihadishvara Temple at Tanjore (Thanjavur), and ends with the dynasty's close in 1279.
--- extra 1: Caveats
This was read in full on 2026-09-26 as raw text from a Wayback Machine snapshot dated 2026-08-06 of britannica.com; the snapshot may differ from the current page (13,145 characters). The page does not say that Vijayalaya captured Thanjavur, that the temple was completed in 1010, or anything about UNESCO's Great Living Chola Temples; those details in the notes are not confirmed here. The earlier citation also named Wikipedia, 'Rajaraja I' and 'Great Living Chola Temples'; those pages were not read here, so nothing taken from them is confirmed by this source.
