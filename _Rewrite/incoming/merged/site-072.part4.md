=== source/art-talk-expansion-profiles
src: e89e8912ea5e
mode: essay-self-help
observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
This source supplies biographical facts, dates and lists of key works for the artist notes in 03_Entities/People tagged `artist` that were added after the original 53; each of those notes records its profile in the `art_talk` frontmatter field, while the original 53 instead cite the earlier source note Art-Talk (Artist Profiles).
--- extra 1: Caveats
This is a compiled secondary source, like the original it follows. A research agent drafts each profile from Wikipedia and a few museum pages, and the profile is read in full when converted into a vault note. It has not been checked against primary or scholarly sources, so the vault notes it feeds carry no `fact_checks` until the fact-check sweep reaches them. Pictures and their credits stay in Art-Talk-main/images and Art-Talk-main/data/image-credits.json; the vault notes hold text only. Artists who died recently, or who are still living, have no freely licensed images and receive text-only notes that do not cite this source.
