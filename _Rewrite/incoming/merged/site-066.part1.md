src: be98c9889829
mode: essay-self-help

--- summary
This entry's ledger holds Martí's birth and death dates, the 1869 arrest and the six-year sentence it brought, the 1871 exile to Spain, his major works, the 1892 founding of the Cuban Revolutionary Party and of Patria, and the April 1895 landing.
--- extra 1: Caveats
Wikipedia remains a tertiary source: it gives six years in prison rather than hard labor, never states that Dos Ríos was his first engagement, and does not reach the 1902 independence.
