=== people--william-morris--1
src: 4d92201ffdcd
mode: epic-fantasy
--- description
William Morris (1834 to 1896) was an English designer, writer and socialist, the central figure of the Arts and Crafts movement. He turned from cheap mechanised ornament toward patterns drawn from nature and made by traditional craft, as a gardener turns from paper flowers to living ones.

He was born in Walthamstow in 1834 into a family grown wealthy through copper mining. At Oxford he met the painter Edward Burne-Jones and, through him, the circle around Dante Gabriel Rossetti.

He built Red House (1859 to 1860) and, unable to find furnishings he considered well made, founded a decorative arts firm in 1861 with Burne-Jones, Rossetti, Philip Webb and others. In 1875 it was reorganised as Morris & Co.

He founded the Socialist League in 1884 and wrote much on politics and utopian fiction. In 1891 he founded the Kelmscott Press, and its illustrated Chaucer was completed months before his death in 1896.

He designed from real plants, disciplining them into flattened repeating patterns. He revived hand block-printing, indigo-discharge dyeing, and letterpress printing from hand-cut type, over faster industrial methods.

His principle was "have nothing in your house that you do not know to be useful or believe to be beautiful". Yet his goods were luxuries that generally only the wealthy could afford.

=== people--liliuokalani--1
src: 3b79914075fa
mode: epic-fantasy
--- description
Liliuokalani (1838 to 1917) was the last monarch of the Kingdom of Hawaii, reigning from 1891 until she was deposed in January 1893 by a group of residents backed by United States troops.

She was born in Honolulu on 2 September 1838 and died there on 11 November 1917.

On 29 January 1891 she succeeded her brother Kalākaua, the kingdom's only reigning queen.

In January 1893 she moved to issue a constitution restoring royal powers cut back by the 1887 "Bayonet Constitution."

On 17 January 1893 a Committee of Safety of mostly American and European residents overthrew her while United States Marines were ashore. She yielded to the United States government to avoid bloodshed and asked it to restore her.

After a failed royalist uprising she was arrested, and in January 1895 she signed an abdication, the last light of a kingdom going down with the sun.
