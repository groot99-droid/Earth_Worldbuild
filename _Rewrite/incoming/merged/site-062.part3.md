Japonica rice was domesticated in the Yangtze basin about 7,000 years ago; indica arose later through hybridization, from the wild ancestor Oryza rufipogon.
--- extra 1: Caveats
This was read in full on 2026-09-26 as raw text from a Wayback Machine snapshot of pnas.org dated 2025-06-10; that snapshot may differ from the current page (67,991 characters). The text supports japonica rice under cultivation in the Yangtze valley by about 8,000 BP from wild Oryza rufipogon, with the non-shattering trait fixed 1,000 years or more later, and indica arising through introgression from japonica; it does not give '7,000 years ago' as a domestication date, the claim an earlier check had made.
