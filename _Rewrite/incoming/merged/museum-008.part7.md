=== people--timur--1
src: 21cddbb5b6e8

--- description
Timur (Tamerlane, traditionally dated 1336 to 1405) was a Central Asian conqueror who built the Timurid Empire and made Samarkand its centre. Begin, as so often with him, with what is uncertain.

His birth year is contested: 1336 is the traditional date, but many contemporary accounts imply the late 1320s. He was born near Kesh (now Shahrisabz, Uzbekistan).

By about 1370 he had besieged his rival Amir Husayn at Balkh and, after Husayn's assassination, proclaimed himself sovereign of the Chagatai line of khans, with dominion over Transoxiana.

Notice the careful architecture of his legitimacy. He was not a descendant of Genghis Khan and did not take the title khan. He ruled as amir through a puppet Chaghatayid khan, Suyurghatmish, and took the title güregen (royal son-in-law) through his marriage to Saray Mulk Khanum, of the Chinggisid line.

His armies campaigned in Persia, Iraq, the lands of the Golden Horde, India (Delhi was sacked in 1398), and Anatolia (he defeated the Ottoman sultan Bayezid I at Ankara in 1402).

Later chronicles describe massacres in cities he conquered. Their casualty figures are contested (see Open Questions). The dead are owed an honest count, even when it cannot be made.
