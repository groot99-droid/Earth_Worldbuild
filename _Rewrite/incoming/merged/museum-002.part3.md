=== madonna-van-der-paele
title: The Madonna with Canon van der Paele
src: 372a8411a704
mode: essay-self-help
--- description
Precision is a form of attention. The elderly canon Joris van der Paele, spectacles in hand, is presented to the Virgin by St George, while St Donatian stands at the left. Look closely and you will see the method: van Eyck painted the old man's wrinkled skin, the shining armour and a richly patterned oriental carpet with such precision that they seem almost real enough to touch. The painting is a showcase of what oil paint could do, a demonstration of the medium's whole grammar. Attention, given fully, brings the distant within reach.

=== margaret-van-eyck
title: Portrait of Margaret van Eyck
src: 57cfcdb556ef
mode: confessional-poetry
--- description
The artist's wife, then 33, looks directly out at us, and I feel her looking at me. This is among the earliest European portraits of an artist's spouse. Its original frame records that "my husband Johannes completed me" on 17 June 1439. Completed me. I turn the words over in my hands. The painting is plain and unflattering, but warm: a record of a real person rather than an ideal. Who would paint me as I am? Would I let them? To be seen plainly and kindly is the rarest gift.

=== four-seasons-autumn
title: Landscapes of the Four Seasons – Autumn (attributed)
src: 7c07f701365c
mode: epic-fantasy
--- description
One of a set of four tall scrolls, the seasons turning like a great wheel, painted in the monumental style of Chinese landscape painting: towering cliffs, a temple half hidden in trees, houses in a misty valley where the air hangs like breath. Works like this show how carefully Sesshū studied the Chinese masters of the Song and Ming dynasties, before and during his journey to China, as a traveller learns the stars before setting out and keeps reading them on the road.

=== autumn-and-winter-landscapes
title: Autumn and Winter Landscapes
src: 2e3853ac8eb6
mode: epic-fantasy
--- description
Two small scrolls that are among the most admired works of Japanese ink painting. In *Winter*, a single jagged black line plunges down through the sky beside a cliff like a crack running through the world. It is not really a mountain edge, and it slices the composition in two with startling abstract force. Below, a lone traveller climbs toward a temple through the snow, a snow suggested only by blank paper, white as silence.

=== haboku-landscape
title: Haboku Landscape (Splashed-Ink Landscape)
src: 6e533e54b191
mode: essay-self-help
--- description
What does it mean to pass on a craft? Painted for his disciple Josui Sōen as a certificate of his training, this landscape is made almost entirely of wet, splashed washes of ink. Now watch: a few quick dark strokes turn the blots into a wine-shop flag, a roof, a boat and two tiny figures. Above it Sesshū wrote a long inscription about his teachers and his trip to China, a genealogy set down beside the gift. It is a celebrated example of the spontaneous "broken ink" style. Mastery is the moment chaos accepts a few right strokes.

=== huike-offering-his-arm
title: Huike Offering His Arm to Bodhidharma
src: dc9b8c0894d6
mode: horror-prose
--- description
Painted when Sesshū was 76. A Zen legend. The monk Huike, refused as a disciple by Bodhidharma. So he cuts off his own arm. To prove his sincerity. Bodhidharma sits meditating before a cave wall. A heavy, unbroken outline. Unmoved. Huike stands beside him, holding his severed arm. Around them the rock swirls. The simple figure does not. That bold contrast makes it one of the great images of Zen art.

Still unmoved.

=== flowers-and-birds
title: Flowers and Birds of the Four Seasons (attributed)
src: ef24d43d1e31
mode: essay-self-help
--- description
Decoration is also a way of keeping time. Pines, plum trees, water and birds stretch across large folding screens, moving through the year from spring to winter, so that a single room holds the whole turning year. Screens like these, painted in Sesshū's manner, helped make bird-and-flower painting a staple of Japanese interior decoration, part of the very architecture of a room. They prepared the way for the grand screens of the Kanō school. What you hang on your walls can become a lineage.

=== view-of-ama-no-hashidate
title: View of Ama-no-Hashidate
src: b72cc6c56398
mode: epic-fantasy
--- description
One of Sesshū's last works, and one of the earliest large views of a real Japanese place. The famous pine-covered sandbar of Ama-no-Hashidate stretches across the bay like a green thread drawn over the water, surrounded by temples, shrines and villages, many labelled with their names as on the map of a known realm. Painted in his eighties, it shows the old master turning from Chinese models to the scenery of his own country, the long road of his art arriving at its own doorstep.

=== lofty-mount-lu
title: Lofty Mount Lu
src: 998eef75d5bd
mode: epic-fantasy
--- description
A towering mountain of stacked peaks, waterfalls and pines rises like a stair into the sky, with a tiny scholar standing at its foot. Shen Zhou painted it as a 70th-birthday gift for his teacher Chen Kuan. The mountain's grandeur is a tribute to the old man's character, virtue lifted into stone. He had never visited Mount Lu. He painted it in the dense, textured manner of the Yuan master Wang Meng, and it is one of his largest and most ambitious works.

=== boating-on-an-autumn-river
title: Boating on an Autumn River
src: ae365ae90eb4
mode: confessional-poetry
--- description
I watch a small boat drift on a quiet river beneath autumn trees and distant hills. The tall, narrow format leads my eye upward through the landscape, and I let it lead me. Scenes of boating, visiting friends and retreat were favourite themes of the Suzhou literati, who valued leisure and friendship above official careers. I think of my own ledger of hours. When did I last choose a friend over a deadline? When did I last drift? A life is measured by its unhurried hours.

=== walking-with-a-staff
title: Walking with a Staff
src: 2313780e0a35
mode: epic-fantasy
--- description
A small scholar with a walking stick pauses among tall, slender trees near a stream and a thatched pavilion, beneath towering, rounded mountains that stand like old guardians. The dry ink and clean contours follow the Yuan painter Ni Zan, whose austere style Shen Zhou admired but found hard to imitate, as clear spring water is hard to carry in the hand. Here he makes it fuller and bolder. The figure is often read as a self-portrait of the artist, a wanderer set within his own world.

=== night-vigil
title: Night Vigil
src: 10f60090112d
mode: confessional-poetry
--- description
Shen Zhou sits awake in a small house among mountains late at night. Above the painting he wrote a long essay describing how, in the silence, he listened to the sounds of the night: wind, dogs, a distant drum. The experience cleared his mind. I read it and I remember my own nights. I remember the wind. I remember a dog somewhere down the road. I remember the drum of my own pulse when sleep would not come. Text and image together make this a rare, intimate record of a Chinese painter's inner life. Why does the dark teach us to listen? Silence is where the mind is swept clean.
