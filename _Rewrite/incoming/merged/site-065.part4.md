=== source/wikipedia-on-chico-mendes
title: Wikipedia on Chico Mendes
src: 034bb0a2a295
mode: essay-self-help
observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
This source backs the birth date, place and family background, the 1985 founding of the National Council of Rubber Tappers, and the 1988 assassination and 1990 conviction of Darly Alves da Silva and his son Darci.
--- extra 1: Caveats
Community-edited and subject to change, it also names a third person, the employee Jerdeir Pereira, convicted alongside Darly and Darci, which the note omits without being wrong; the current article text gives no year for the Xapuri-specific rural workers' union, only 1975 for the Brasileia rubber tappers' union.

=== source/wikipedia-on-christopher-dresser

observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
This source backs Dresser's birth and death dates and places, his training at the Government School of Design, the 1876-77 Japan mission and Tiffany commission, and his 1879-82 post at Linthorpe Art Pottery.
--- extra 1: Caveats
It does not mention the Jena doctorate, botany lecturing, Coalbrookdale, James Dixon & Sons, William Morris or the Bauhaus, and its body text calls the book Principles of Design (1873) while its bibliography calls it Principles of Decorative Design (1873).
