=== melencolia-i
title: Melencolia I
src: b7d2940a4807
mode: horror-prose
--- description
A winged figure. Brooding. Around her, scattered tools of measurement and craft. A compass. A polyhedron. A sphere. An hourglass. Behind her hangs a magic square. Its rows add up to 34. Its bottom row contains the year: 1514. It has been called the most debated print ever made. It is often read as a portrait of the creative mind stalled by doubt.

Stalled. Still stalled.

=== rhinoceros
title: The Rhinoceros
src: d110d1a521a2
mode: epic-fantasy
--- description
In 1515 an Indian rhinoceros arrived in Lisbon, the first seen in Europe since Roman times, a wonder carried from far shores. Dürer never saw it. He worked from a letter and a sketch, and imagined an animal clad in riveted armour plates, like a knight forged by some smith of the wild, with a small extra horn on its back. The print sold in thousands and remained Europe's standard picture of a rhinoceros for about 250 years, a creature of rumour standing in for the truth.

=== pieta
title: Pietà
src: 8b27e95f683f
mode: confessional-poetry
--- description
I stand before a mother holding her dead son across her lap. Michelangelo made it in his early twenties, for a French cardinal's tomb: the Virgin cradling the dead Christ. Her calm, youthful face. The heavy folds of her robe. The marble turned to flesh and cloth. I have seen grief look older than this. Why is hers so calm? It is the only work he ever signed: his name is carved on the sash across Mary's chest. Vasari says he added it after hearing visitors credit the statue to another sculptor. I understand wanting to be known for what you made. But I keep looking at her face, not his name. Some sorrow runs so deep it looks like peace.

=== david
title: David
src: 7c65a09068c4
mode: epic-fantasy
--- description
Carved from a tall, narrow block that other sculptors had abandoned, this giant David is shown before his fight with Goliath, tense, watchful and fully alert, like a drawn bowstring in the breath before release. The Florentine Republic set it in front of the Palazzo Vecchio as a symbol of the city's defiance of stronger enemies, a vow carved in stone. It was moved indoors in 1873, and a copy now stands in its original place, a sentinel's twin keeping the old watch.

=== doni-tondo
title: Doni Tondo (The Holy Family)
src: 41591f5b170b
mode: confessional-poetry
--- description
I stand before Michelangelo's only generally accepted finished panel painting, made for the wedding of the merchant Agnolo Doni. Mary, Joseph and the Christ Child twist together in a tight spiral of sculptural bodies and sharp, acid colours. I know that knot. I know how a family can hold on and pull at once. I know how close is never quite still. Nude youths lounge in the background, apart from it all. The original carved frame, probably designed by Michelangelo himself, survives, a circle around the circle. What holds a family, if not the frame we build around it? Love is a spiral, not a line.

=== creation-of-adam
title: The Creation of Adam (Sistine Chapel ceiling)
src: 2d0beceb5d60
mode: epic-fantasy
--- description
The best-known scene of the vast Sistine ceiling, which Michelangelo painted largely by himself for Pope Julius II. God, borne on a swirl of angels like a storm cloud full of wings, reaches toward the languid Adam; their fingers almost, but not quite, touch, and in that narrow gap all of creation holds its breath. Around this and eight other Genesis scenes, he painted prophets, sibyls, ancestors of Christ and 20 muscular nudes, over 300 figures in all, a heaven crowded with the living.

=== moses
title: Moses
src: 9d457c59eed9
mode: epic-fantasy
--- description
The centrepiece of the tomb of Julius II, a project that dragged on for 40 years like a winter that would not break, and that Michelangelo called "the tragedy of the tomb". The seated prophet grips the tablets of the law, his beard flowing like water, his gaze fierce as a desert wind. The horns on his head come from a traditional mistranslation of the Hebrew for "rays of light": light, in the retelling, hardened into horn.

=== last-judgment
title: The Last Judgment
src: 1012615dd325
mode: horror-prose
--- description
More than 20 years after the ceiling, Michelangelo came back. The altar wall. He covered it with a whirling vision of the end of time. A powerful, beardless Christ raises his arm. The saved rise. The damned are dragged down. Its crowds of nudes caused scandal, and after the Council of Trent another painter was hired to add draperies. Then St Bartholomew. He holds a flayed skin. Its face is thought to be Michelangelo's self-portrait.

His own face. Emptied. Held in another's hand.
