=== era/neogene-period
title: Neogene Period
src: d239261c8db2
mode: epic-fantasy
observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
The Neogene Period, from 23.03 to 2.58 million years ago, is the second period of the Cenozoic Era. In its span the grasslands spread outward, the climate cooled toward the ice ages, and the hominin lineage parted from that of the other apes, a single branch bending away from the grove.
--- fact 1
The Neogene Period spans 23.03 to 2.58 million years ago and holds two epochs, the Miocene and the Pliocene. Its base is fixed at Lemme-Carrosio in Italy, and its top, the base of the Quaternary, at Monte San Nicola in Sicily.
--- fact 2
Warm in the early Miocene, the climate then cooled. About 14 million years ago the East Antarctic ice sheet spread its white hand wider, and about 2.7 million years ago glaciation tightened its grip on the Northern Hemisphere.
--- fact 3
About 8 to 6 million years ago, grasses using the C4 pathway of photosynthesis spread widely, and open grassland and savanna unrolled like a golden carpet, favouring the grazing mammals.
--- fact 4
In the Miocene the apes were many and varied across Africa, Europe and Asia. Around 7 million years ago the hominin lineage parted from that of the chimpanzees (see [[event/divergence-of-the-hominin-lineage|Divergence of the Hominin Lineage]]), and [[species/sahelanthropus-tchadensis|Sahelanthropus tchadensis]] and [[species/australopithecus-afarensis|Australopithecus afarensis]] date to this period.
--- fact 5
In the [[event/messinian-salinity-crisis|Messinian Salinity Crisis]], about 5.96 to 5.33 million years ago, the Mediterranean was left largely dried out, a sea reduced to salt. The land bridge of Panama, generally dated to about 3 million years ago though the timing is debated, joined the Americas hand to hand; see [[event/closure-of-the-isthmus-of-panama|Closure of the Isthmus of Panama]].
--- context
The Neogene Period comes after the [[era/paleogene-period|Paleogene Period]] and before the [[era/quaternary-period|Quaternary Period]] within the [[era/cenozoic-era|Cenozoic Era]]. It belongs to the theme thread of [[theme/earth-systems-and-life|Earth Systems and Life]].
--- observer
Here is the fork in the road, as the Observer marks it: the Neogene is where the line that includes humans separates from the line of the other apes, in a world drying and cooling into grassland and ice. It is tempting to read the drying as a push toward us. The Observer refuses that story. The same changes affected many lineages, and most Miocene apes left no descendants. A pressure is not a plan. The Observer also names the test: the reading would weaken if the traits of the hominin line could be tied directly to the spread of grassland.
