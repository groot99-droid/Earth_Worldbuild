=== source/outram-et-al-2009-earliest-horse-harnessing-and-milking
src: 331e78a9d6cc
mode: essay-self-help
observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
Three lines of evidence support horse domestication in the Eneolithic Botai Culture of Kazakhstan, dated to about 3500 BCE: the Botai horse metacarpals resemble those of Bronze Age domestic horses, some horses show signs of bridling, and organic residues in ceramics show the processing of mare's milk and carcass products.
--- extra 1: Caveats
Only the abstract was read here, the PubMed record fetched on 2026-09-26, since science.org returns HTTP 403 to automated requests and no open-access copy turned up. It confirms only what the abstract states, and it predates the 2018 genomic study, Gaunitz et al. 2018 Botai Horses, which shows Botai horses were ancestors of Przewalski's horses rather than of modern domestic horses. An abstract-only source never moves a note toward reviewed.

=== source/oxygen-isotopes-and-the-giant-impact-search-summaries
src: e1226a377e9c
mode: essay-self-help
observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
The oxygen isotope ratios in the Apollo samples match Earth's very closely, a fit that is consistent with the giant impact hypothesis only if the impactor, Theia, had an Earth-like composition.
--- extra 1: Caveats
This note stands only on web search result summaries from 2026-09-25 — Science, PNAS, PubMed, astronomy.com and arXiv among the results — and none of the pages themselves was opened. Those summaries describe the near-identical isotopes both as support for the hypothesis and as a challenge to it, and a 2024 paper reports a small difference, 12 parts per million. So the note states it as consistent with the hypothesis and, at the same time, a puzzle.

--- summary
The oldest unambiguous eukaryote microfossils are placed at about 1.65 Ga, with older candidates left in disputed status.
--- extra 1: Caveats
This one was read in full on 2026-09-26 from the open-access PDF at www.vliz.be, the publishedVersion version, 146,220 characters, so wording, figures or pagination may differ slightly from the published version. The review itself calls large organic-walled vesicles — Valeria lophostriata, from the more than 1.65 Ga Mallapunyah Formation, Australia — the oldest unambiguous eukaryotic microfossils, and it says younger coiled filaments called Grypania, about 1.45 Ga, India, are more convincingly eukaryotic than Grypania spiralis from the 1.87 Ga Negaunee Iron Formation.
