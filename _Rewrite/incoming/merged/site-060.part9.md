=== source/brahmagupta-and-fibonacci-on-zero-and-numerals-search-summaries
title: Brahmagupta and Fibonacci on Zero and Numerals (Search Summaries)
src: cefc63bd4e48
mode: essay-self-help
observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
Brahmagupta set out rules for arithmetic with zero in 628 CE, and Fibonacci's Liber abaci of 1202 introduced Hindu-Arabic numerals to Europe.
--- extra 1: Caveats
These facts rest only on web search result summaries consulted on 2026-09-25 (results listed Britannica, MacTutor, Wikipedia and several teaching sites); none of the pages was opened directly. Brahmagupta is described as the first to give systematic rules for zero in recorded history; that is a common claim, and the summaries do not address rival early uses of zero.
