=== source/wikipedia-on-ajuricaba-chief
src: 7bfa207690c3
mode: essay-self-help
observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
This source supports his identity as a Manaos leader, his unclear origins, his death c. 1728, the Dutch alliance, the 1723 campaign under Governor João da Maia da Gama, the possible betrayal by allies, and his leap into the river.
--- extra 1: Caveats
This is a short general-audience article that relies on Portuguese colonial chroniclers and a thin bibliography, and it gives c. 1728 for his death where the Portuguese edition gives September 1727.

=== source/wikipedia-on-al-biruni

mode: essay-self-help
observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
This source supports his birth in Kath in 973, the hedged death date (9 December 1048 in the infobox, around 1050 in the lead), the July 1017 Ghaznavid conquest of Khwarazm, the Chronology (about 1000) and Kitab al-Hind (about 1030) dates, his fluency in Sanskrit, and the Nandana Earth-radius method.
