=== people--sunni-ali--1
src: 64016003a009
mode: epic-fantasy
--- description
Sunni Ali (ruled about 1464 to 1492) was the ruler who turned Songhai from a small state on the Niger River into a West African empire, taking Timbuktu in 1468 as the Mali Empire waned like an evening tide. His birth year is unknown.

He became ruler of Songhai about 1464, the fifteenth in the Sunni dynasty.

He conquered Timbuktu in 1468 and, after a long siege, the trading city of Djenné, seizing the moment of the weakening Mali Empire.

He was nominally Muslim but tolerated older local religious practice, and this drew tension with the Muslim scholars of Timbuktu, whose chronicles remember him harshly.

He died in 1492; accounts differ on whether he drowned crossing a river or died another way.

His son Sunni Baru succeeded him, but in 1493 was overthrown by Askia Muhammad, a general who accused Sunni Baru of insufficient devotion to Islam and founded a new dynasty.
