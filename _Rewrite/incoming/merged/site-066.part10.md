=== source/wikipedia-on-savitribai-phule
src: 4ed1eeba2c2a

observer_mode: essay-self-help
--- summary
This source backs her birth and death dates, her marriage at nine in 1840, her education under Jyotirao Phule and Sagunabai Kshirsagar, the 1848 Bhidewada school, and her death from plague on 10 March 1897.
--- extra 1: Caveats
An open-edit tertiary source that contradicts itself on the infanticide-prevention home's date — 1853 in the lead, 1863 in the body — and relies on secondary writers such as Divya Kandukuri for the harassment story.

=== source/wikipedia-on-sesshu-toyo
